# Russell Held Off Verstappen in Baku on the Weekend Antonelli Finally Slipped

Baku ran on a Saturday this year, moved a day to avoid clashing with Azerbaijan's Remembrance Day, and it gave George Russell the weekend he had been waiting a month for. He took pole, managed two safety car restarts late in the race, and held off Max Verstappen to the line by 0.196 seconds. [Formula 1's official classification](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2026/races/1295/azerbaijan/race-result) reads Russell, Verstappen, Isack Hadjar, a double podium for Red Bull behind him, and [RacingNews365](https://racingnews365.com/2026-f1-azerbaijan-grand-prix-baku-results) counts it as Russell's third win of the year.

The other half of the story is that Kimi Antonelli made a mistake, which has not happened often this season. He crashed in qualifying and started 16th, and while he recovered to fifth behind Charles Leclerc, a fifth is ten points against Russell's twenty-five. What that means is that Russell finally got the kind of afternoon he needed, a win on a weekend when Antonelli's own error kept him off the podium, and it was worth fifteen points.

The late part of the race was messy in the way Baku usually is. The first safety car came after Alex Albon made a mistake in his Williams, and the second followed a collision in which Franco Colapinto locked up and hit his Alpine teammate Pierre Gasly, with Lando Norris also eliminated. Russell had to manage both restarts and then the late pressure from Verstappen, and he gave nothing away. Lewis Hamilton finished sixth, and the Haas cars took eighth and ninth.

The standings after Baku have Antonelli on 302 and Russell on 236, so the lead is back to 66, the same number it was after Monza, with eight rounds left. Hamilton is third on 199 and Norris fourth on 186 after the retirement. Verstappen has quietly become the other story, since three podiums in three races have taken him to 163 and he was the driver closest to Russell all afternoon in Baku.

The pattern I would pull out of Baku is that Russell is only gaining ground when Antonelli loses it himself. A win and a five is the swing he needs on most of the remaining weekends, and Antonelli has not given him many. I keep the season ledger on [Formula 1 Pulse](/formula-1). The next round, after [Madrid](/writing/2026-spanish-grand-prix-antonelli-madrid), is [a Bahrain Grand Prix held in Malaysia](/writing/2026-bahrain-grand-prix-malaysia-verstappen-sepang).
