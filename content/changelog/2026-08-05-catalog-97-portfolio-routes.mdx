The seven pages people see first, [home](/), [work](/portfolio), [writing](/writing), [dashboards](/dashboards), [about](/about), [résumé](/resume), and [contact](/contact), moved from the Working Instrument onto a new design language I call Catalog 97. Those seven pages now own their header, footer, and main landmark through one shell, with a single list in the code deciding which routes belong to it. About picked up the headshot in its portrait slot, and the résumé shows the institution marks beside each entry.

I also added a layouts canvas at `/design/catalog-pages` that stacks all seven pages one after another so I can review them as a set. It is a working page for me, so it stays out of search. The rest of the site stayed on the Working Instrument until September.
