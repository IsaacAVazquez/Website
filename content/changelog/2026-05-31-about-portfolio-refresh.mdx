May was a slower month on purpose, but a few things still landed:

- The about and portfolio pages both got a content and layout pass to match where the site actually is now.
- A shared stats panel component, `HomeStatsPanel`, now feeds the homepage and the writing archive, so live numbers render the same way everywhere.
- Player ID validation and snapshot handling got tightened across the sports modules, and NBA player stats moved to a newer ESPN endpoint.
- UI text across the site was brought in line with the writing voice rules.

The kind of month that doesn't demo well but makes the next month possible.
