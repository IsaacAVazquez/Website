The [rankings board](/fantasy-football) can now rank by FantasyPros projected value over replacement as well as expert consensus, with a toggle between the two and a league-size selector, built from the nine published VORP reports across PPR, half PPR, and standard at 10, 12, and 14 teams. The [draft tracker](/fantasy-football/draft-tracker) shows VORP too, alongside a new decision panel that reads the replacement line, the tier cliff at each position, what it costs to wait until your next pick, and which position is most at risk of drying up.

After the draft, the tracker now has a recap and a turn-by-turn replay that compares what it recommended with what got picked, and league settings can be saved as named presets so I am not re-entering them for every league. The board also keeps two weeks of daily readings and shows how far each player's consensus rank and ADP moved over the last 7 and 14 days on the overall and flex views. The first 40 rows of the board now render on the server, so the page has real players in it before any JavaScript runs.
