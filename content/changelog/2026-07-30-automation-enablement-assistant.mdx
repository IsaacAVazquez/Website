The [Automation Enablement Assistant](/enablement-assistant) is a tool for a platform team rolling out test automation. It gives someone new to test automation a recommended standard stack, a step-by-step onboarding plan, answers to common setup questions, and a running list of where the documentation falls short. The recommendation logic lives in its own tested engine, and there is a [build article](/writing/building-an-automation-enablement-assistant) that walks through why I made the choices I did.
