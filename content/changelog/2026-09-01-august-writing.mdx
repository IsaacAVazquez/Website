Several tools shipped this summer without an article explaining them, so most of this batch catches up on that, with build write-ups for the [trade calculator](/writing/building-a-fantasy-trade-calculator), [rent vs buy](/writing/building-a-rent-vs-buy-calculator), [score pools](/writing/building-a-score-pools-engine), the [best ball draft room](/writing/building-a-best-ball-draft-room), and the [draft companion](/writing/building-a-draft-companion-that-cannot-click). Each one sticks to what the engine actually does.

The other four continue threads that were already running. The Formula 1 series picks up with the [Hungarian](/writing/2026-hungarian-grand-prix-russell-clean-weekend) and [Dutch](/writing/2026-dutch-grand-prix-antonelli-zandvoort) Grands Prix, [Dashboards that read committed files](/writing/dashboards-that-read-committed-files) explains the snapshot pattern most of the dashboards here are built on, and [How I run a draft with my own tools](/writing/how-i-run-a-draft-with-my-own-tools) is the draft-week process piece.
