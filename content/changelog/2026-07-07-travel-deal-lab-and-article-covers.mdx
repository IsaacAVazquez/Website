[Travel Deal Lab](/travel-deals) is a companion to the [travel planner](/travel) that works on what a trip costs. You set the destination region, the dates, the number of nights and travelers, and a budget, and it tells you whether you are inside the cheaper booking window for that kind of trip, whether a quoted fare is actually a deal against a typical fare band, whether an award booking beats cash once you net out taxes and fees, and how to split the budget across flights, lodging, food, activities, and a buffer. Underneath the calculators is a playbook of deal-finding tactics you can mark as applied. The fare bands and the points baseline are my own rough estimates, so the page says that plainly and carries an as-of date.

Covers are now part of publishing an article. Every post has an entry in one plan file, and a scheduled GitHub Action searches Wikimedia Commons for a freely licensed photo, downloads it, and writes the credit and license into the post's frontmatter from the Commons API, so the attribution always matches the file that landed. Abstract pieces that have nothing real to photograph keep the generated title card.

On the writing side I backfilled the season that had gone by without much coverage, with a Premier League series, a La Liga series, race reviews for the first nine rounds of the 2026 Formula 1 season, and, the day after, recaps of the World Cup's round of 32 and round of 16 built from the same snapshot that powers the [World Cup dashboard](/world-cup-2026).
