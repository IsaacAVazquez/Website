Until mid-September the site was really two sites, with the seven main pages on Catalog 97 and everything else still on the Working Instrument. On September 16 every route moved inside the Catalog 97 header, footer, and color tokens, `/admin` included, and the old header and footer were deleted. The dashboards did not need rewriting for that, because a bridge maps their old color names onto the new values, so each one can move over properly later. In the meantime some live indicators on the sports and fantasy pages read quieter than before.

On September 22 eleven pages were rebuilt directly in Catalog 97, including every article, every writing topic page, every project page, [search](/search), [now](/now), and this changelog. On September 24 the seven main pages moved to what I have been calling the print shop layout, where each page uses two lead inks, the tone comes from halftone, and the homepage opens on a collage of riso-printed plates for a few of the projects.

I also ran a fresh SEO audit across all 268 sitemap URLs and 206 posts. Most of what the July checks set up still holds, and the four serious findings, such as project links answering with temporary redirects and long titles being clipped mid-sentence, are logged as a backlog of fixes.
