# Project routes own their design

**Date:** 2026-10-11
**Status:** Direction, scope, the contract, and the archetypes were approved in conversation on 2026-10-10. The foundation PR carries this spec and the two test changes it needs. The four pilot routes come next, Isaac reviews them, and the remaining eight groups run against the bar he sets. Stage 1 merged in #582 on 2026-10-11, stage 2 (the eight groups, PRs #587 to #594, merged through #595) on 2026-10-12, and the close-out deleted the hero, the press rows, and the overprint the same day.

## What this is

On 2026-10-10 Isaac said the project pages lack intentionality, that there are a lot of weird boxes and design decisions that do not come from the project, and that the focus should be on each project and how its design, UI, and UX serve that project. He said it is fine to break the site's design system, Catalog 97, to make each page as effective and legitimate as possible.

This is the third pass at the same complaint. On 2026-09-24 he said the projects felt generic, and the fix (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-project-specific-ui-design.md`, PRs #470 to #484) kept one shared print shop system and gave each route a per-project signature visual and ink pair inside it. On 2026-10-09 a 46-page review-and-fix sweep (PR #581) fixed defects against the Catalog 97 checklist. Both passes stayed inside the system. The new variable this time is permission to leave it.

## What I saw on the live site

On 2026-10-10 I looked at Earthquake Pulse, Wine Cellar, MLB, Budget Planner, Investments, News Pulse, Travel Planner, Decision Lab, SpaceX Mission Control, Food Map, Fantasy Football, Job Search, and Recipe Finder at 1680px in both themes. Every one follows one skeleton. The saffron header. A full-bleed lead-ink hero sheet with the Anton poster h1, a three-to-five line first-person standfirst, a tiny all-caps meta line, sometimes a jump link, and three readouts in a row. Then a plate, meaning a rectangle with the hard offset shadow in the second ink, holding the signature visual, the primary form, or an empty state. Then a torn seam, a body sheet with a poster h2, a list, and a right rail panel, and a bone aside reading "What I use it for" with a build-notes link.

The boxes Isaac is seeing come from that construction. The plate is a box around whatever the route had. `Catalog97ProjectHero` only wraps the signature in a div with a top margin, and each route's signature component draws the plate itself, so it is 34 separate decisions that all made the same choice. Boxes nest. Budget Planner is sheet, plate, readouts, second plate, seven bordered envelope tiles, which is four levels, and Investments is five. MLB's plate holds six bordered division boxes, and Earthquake's rail is six bordered stat tiles. The plate often holds the empty state, so Wine Cellar's first screen is a dark void reading "Your rack is empty", Job Search's is one sentence above a lone readout, Investments' is "Add a holding below to see live quotes here", Travel's is a blank boarding pass over a second plate holding the first-trip form, and Fantasy's holds four small status chips. The hero runs 900 to 1400px on desktop before the first row of the log or the first field, and the 10-09 sweep measured first controls at y995 to y1618 on a phone. And the frame is editorial on every page. The riso poster idiom suits Home, Portfolio, Writing, and About, and on a budget app, a brokerage terminal, a transit board, a standings table, or a recipe matcher it reads as a themed demo rather than a product.

The September redesign solved "the same widget on every page" by swapping the widget. It did not change the frame, the idiom, or the box grammar, and since then every page has been optimised toward the same checklist. I think intentionality has to come from each project's own genre, meaning what a real product of that kind looks like, what the first action is, what the visitor came to read, and what density it needs.

## Decisions

Isaac made four decisions on 2026-10-10.

Each project owns its design inside the site frame. The header and footer stay. Everything inside is the project's call, from the layout and whether there is a hero at all to the type scale, the density, and its own colour system where the genre calls for it. The Catalog 97 tokens stay available as a default and are never required on a project route.

A pilot of four routes goes first, one per archetype, with one Opus 5.5 agent each at high effort, meaning Premier League together with La Liga (a standings board), Wine Cellar (a personal log), Rent vs. Buy (a calculator), and Investments (the console). Isaac reviews the four and says what the bar is, and the remaining groups run in parallel against it.

A project may add one typeface when its genre earns it, loaded with `next/font` on that route only. Otherwise it uses the loaded four (Archivo, Newsreader, Anton, Fragment Mono) plus system stacks.

Fantasy football gets the frame removed and keeps the board. The poster hero, the plate, and the seams the September pass added come off, and the settled tier board is the page. Nothing in its surface briefs' "do not re-litigate" lists reopens.

The seven designed Catalog 97 pages (`/`, `/portfolio`, `/writing`, `/dashboards`, `/about`, `/resume`, `/contact`) are out of scope and keep the system in full.

## Scope

`DASHBOARD_ROUTES` in `src/constants/catalog97Nav.ts` holds 34 routes and matches the case studies one to one. In scope are those 34, the seven fantasy sub-routes (frame removal only), `/score-pools` with its tracker and settings pages, and `/agent-build-index`. `/arcade` already has its own identity and stays as it is. That is 45 pages, of which the eight fantasy pages and the three score pools pages are light.

## The contract every project route follows

This replaces the Catalog 97 checklist on project routes, and `DESIGN_CHECKLIST.md` carries it as the "Project routes" section.

The frame. `Catalog97Shell` keeps the header, the single `main`, and the footer. Inside `main` the project renders its own root, sets its own surface, and owns everything from there down. The `data-c97-surface="paper"` wrapper and `press` in `Catalog97ToolShell` are defaults a project overrides with its own root, so untouched routes do not change. Four shared rules leak into that root unless the project handles them. The base layer in `globals.css` sets the body face on `h1` to `h6`, a 65ch measure and a bottom margin on `p`, padding on `ul` and `ol`, and a one-column `.grid`, so a project root sets its heading font explicitly and resets the paragraph and list defaults it does not want. Any reused `c97-*` class resolves `--c97-ink`, `--c97-surface`, and `--c97-rule` from the paper wrapper, so a project with its own palette redeclares those tokens on its root, or wraps the borrowed part in its own `data-c97-surface`, before reusing a class. The focus ring paints `var(--c97-accent)` with no radius, so the root sets `--c97-accent` to the project's focus colour. `::selection` and the body overscroll stay Catalog 97's and are accepted. `/arcade` reaches independence by avoidance, since it paints its own root, writes per-element focus rules, and borrows no `c97-*` class beyond `c97-shell c97-frame`, and that is the model.

The genre test. Each brief names the kind of real product the page is (a standings app, a transit display, a budgeting app, a brokerage terminal, a field guide, a comparison table) and two or three reference products. The page borrows the genre's conventions, never its branding. Where a convention conflicts with the brief, the brief wins.

The first screen. At 1440x900 the first data row, the first input, or the board itself is on screen without scrolling. At 390x844 it is within one and a half screens. The site header is 120px on desktop and about 263px on a 360px phone and is part of the frame, so those budgets include it. The hero, where a project keeps one, is a title row and one orienting sentence, never a sheet. Poster type (Anton with the overprint offset) is no longer required anywhere on a project route, and a reference piece may keep a display title if it earns it.

No plate. Nothing wraps the primary content in a bordered or shadowed rectangle. A box is allowed only when the box is the object (a ticket, a card in a list, a bottle label) and carries data of its own. At most two levels of visible containment anywhere on the page, and no border around a chart. Every bordered tile grid (six division boxes, six detail tiles, seven envelopes in a plate) becomes a table, a list, or a definition list unless the brief says the tile is the object.

No repeats. Readouts exist only where they are the product's own summary. A trio that restates the first row, the first field, or the chart below is cut. The source, the as-of date, and the storage note each appear once, near the data they describe. The "Data note", "Snapshot note", "How this works", and "Attribution" bands collapse into one source line plus the build-notes link, and long method prose belongs in the build notes write-up, which already exists for every route. Compliance text (the fintech and investments disclaimers, the "curated, unverified" disclosures, the model boundary notes) stays word for word, printed once, next to the result it qualifies.

Colour and type. A project may use the Catalog 97 tokens as its default and may instead declare its own custom properties with a light and a dark story, or, where the genre is a terminal or a display board, one surface in both themes with the shell still switching. Token names avoid the dead patterns `catalog97-closeout.test.ts` bans (`--radius-*`, `--shadow-*`, `--surface-*`, `--text-(primary|secondary|tertiary|inverse)`, `--color-(primary|secondary|accent|success|warning|error)`, `--border-(primary|secondary|accent)`, and any `var(--font-body|heading|display|inter|mono|jetbrains-mono)` read), so a project names them `--<abbr>-*`. Data colours stay data (team liveries, line colours, party colours). It may add one typeface through `next/font` loaded on that route only, kept out of `public/humans.txt`. Text contrast holds 4.5:1 in both themes as measured by `scripts/contrastSweep.mjs`, which is design-agnostic and stays a gate. Tailwind's radius and shadow utilities compile to 0 and none everywhere, so a project that wants a radius writes it in its route CSS.

Unchanged rules. One h1 per page, no nested `main`, heading order, 44px targets, labels on inputs, visible focus, status never by colour alone, `prefers-reduced-motion`, no opacity-0 page fades (`ssr-first-paint.test.tsx`), no horizontal scroll from 320 to 430 (`scripts/layoutSweep.mjs`, which also holds the h1 and the footer to the header's content edge), the print rules in `DESIGN_CHECKLIST.md`, and `WRITING_VOICE.md` for every string. Every e2e test id, exact string, URL parameter, and keyboard path a spec or a route test pins stays, and where a redesign has to change one the PR changes the test in the same commit and says why. Nothing in a surface brief's "do not re-litigate" list reopens, and the brief is updated with the decisions and the verified state.

The build note. The "What I use it for" aside at the foot of every route stays as a colophon, one line at desktop widths holding the heading, the purpose paragraph in small type, and the link, stacked on a phone. It carries the purpose only. Isaac's one note on the pilots (2026-10-11) was that the strip took too much space, and the method prose belongs to the write-up the link opens, so the `method` field is gone from `projectBuildNoteContent.ts` and a route's entry is one `purpose` string. `ProjectBuildNote.test.tsx` asserts the purpose verbatim and the tool shell test asserts the link. The shell owns it, so projects do not restyle it, and it renders after the project root on the frame's paper, between the project and the footer, which is where the frame already changes surface. Twenty of the 43 linked routes still show the placeholder ("Why I built it this way") once the pilots land, since rent-vs-buy and wine-cellar got theirs in #584 and #583: travel-deals, march-madness, agent-build-index, arcade, the three score pools pages, four fantasy pages, decision-lab, enablement-assistant, budget-planner, interchange-iq, food-map, mba-internship-notifications, museum-log, recipe-finder, and travel. Every one in scope gets a real purpose paragraph, two or three sentences in Isaac's voice written from the code in its own route's PR and flagged for his read, and arcade stays as it is.

## The five archetypes

These are brief templates, not shared components. Sibling routes share code only where they already do.

Board. Data you scan, live or snapshot: Earthquake Pulse, Bay Area Transit, SpaceX, News Pulse, GitHub Trending, Formula 1, golf, MLB, NBA, NFL, Premier League, La Liga, World Cup, Polling. The references are ESPN and FotMob standings, the Masters leaderboard, a platform departure display, USGS Latest Earthquakes, F1 TV's timing tower. The board is the first screen. A status strip carries the source, the as-of time, and fresh or stale. Filters and view switches are a sticky toolbar on the board, not a separate band. Detail opens on select in a drawer or inline. Density follows the genre, dense for standings and large for a departure board. Data colours are the only colour that means anything.

Log. A personal collection you add to: Wine Cellar, Museum Log, Travel Planner, Recipe Finder, Food Map, Budget Planner's ledger, Job Search's pipeline. The references are Vivino and Delectable, a paper ledger, YNAB's envelopes, a field guide, a kanban pipeline. The collection is the page and the add action is visible on the first screen. The empty state teaches with a worked example or sample row, never a void. An object card (a label, a ticket stub, an index card) exists only where it carries the object's own data, and otherwise it is a clean list.

Calculator. Inputs that produce a result: Rent vs. Buy, Interchange IQ, Decision Lab, Travel Deal Lab, Before You Buy, Fantasy Formula 1's optimizer. The references are the NYT rent-or-buy calculator, Stripe's pricing calculator, a one-page financial model. Inputs and the result sit side by side above the fold, stacked on a phone with the result first or pinned. The result updates live and has one visual. Secondary inputs sit under disclosure headings in the input column. Assumptions and the disclaimer follow in prose, once.

Console. A dense working tool: Investments, the fantasy boards and trackers, Score Pools, Enablement Assistant, the Trade Calculator. The references are a brokerage terminal, Sleeper's and Underdog's draft rooms, an internal platform dashboard. The page is the app, full width where the route is already wide, a compact title bar, toolbars, sticky controls, keyboard paths, the tool's own type ramp and surfaces. Density is a feature. Investments and fantasy keep the decisions their surface briefs settled.

Reference. Curated data to read and compare: AI Dev Tools, Frontier Models, Tech Startup Tracker, Agent Build Index, March Madness (an archive), World Cup's final record. The references are Artificial Analysis, a Crunchbase list, a sports almanac page. The comparison instrument, a chart or a table, comes first with its filters, the "curated, unverified" disclosure sits beside it, and this is the one archetype where a written introduction and a display title still belong.

## What enforced the old frame, and what the foundation PR changed

Nothing needed a new shell, wrapper, or branch. `ConditionalLayout` wraps every non-designed route in `Catalog97ToolShell`, which is `Catalog97Shell` around a paper div plus the build-note aside, and a project root rendered inside that div paints over the paper. The hard gates on the old frame were two tests, both changed in the foundation PR. `src/constants/__tests__/project-routes-complete.test.ts` required every case-study page, or its imports three levels deep, to match the hero (`Catalog97ProjectHero|c97-project-hero|<h1 className="c97-poster`) and to have a `PROJECT_PRESS` row; both requirements are gone, and the page-exists, no-`<main`, route-classification, and `DASHBOARD_ROUTES` equality checks stay. `e2e/project-routes.spec.ts` asserted the h1's nearest surface was `ink-<lead>`, that it carried `c97-poster`, and that a press row existed; those three assertions are gone, and one h1, one main, hydration in two timezones, and the layout alignment stay. `PROJECT_PRESS` was also the route registry in both files, deciding orphans in the Jest test and building `LAYOUT_ROUTES` in the spec, so a deleted row would have silently dropped a route from both checks; both are keyed on `DASHBOARD_ROUTES` now (the spec adds the press keys so the fantasy sub-routes stay covered), and press rows are deleted per route as routes stop reading them.

Four more pins on the old frame sit in route tests and are each route's to change in its own PR, with the reason in the PR: `e2e/before-you-buy.spec.ts` selects `.c97-project-hero [role="status"]`; `src/app/earthquake-pulse/__tests__/earthquake-client.test.tsx` queries `.c97-project-hero-readouts`; `e2e/investments.spec.ts` pins `#hero`, the wrapper around the hero in `InvestmentsDashboard.tsx`; and `src/constants/__tests__/projectPress.test.ts` required a press row on all eight fantasy routes until stage 2 took the sheets off and the close-out deleted the rows. Any other per-route test that pins `c97-poster`, the hero, or an ink surface changes in the same commit as its route. `Catalog97ProjectHero` and its test are deleted in the close-out once nothing renders them.

Each redesigned route's `lastmod` in `src/lib/sitemap.js` moves with its PR and `public/sitemap.xml` is regenerated before push, or `sitemap-consistency.test.ts` fails.
