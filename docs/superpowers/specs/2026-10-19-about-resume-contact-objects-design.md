# About, Résumé, and Contact as three objects

**Date:** 2026-10-19
**Status:** Isaac approved the three mockups in the brainstorming companion on 2026-10-19 ("Looks good"), then answered the two open questions the same day (the accent for the proof's marks, and the sheet runs as long as the résumé is). This supersedes `2026-10-18-about-resume-contact-design.md`, whose build is the commit on `design/about-resume-contact` and PR #613. That build is replaced on the same branch.

## What this is and why

The 2026-10-18 pass kept the Catalog 97 language and took the stack of one-sentence ink sheets off the three pages. Isaac read the result and said it "feels like a bunch of content thrown on a page without any direction or design thought." He was right, since that pass was subtraction and not a design. The pages had fewer bands, but nothing on them came from the content.

This pass gives each page one object from the print shop's world, and the layout comes from the object. The bio is a proof, meaning a document a proofreader has been through, with every claim marked and checked in the margin. The résumé is the one-page sheet sitting on the press bed. Contact is a postal reply card. Each page is one surface, with no second band and nothing repeated, and the project-sovereign contract from the 2026-10-11 spec binds all three, meaning the first screen holds real content, there is one h1, targets are 44px, contrast is at least 4.5:1 in both themes, and nothing scrolls sideways. The mockups Isaac approved are `.superpowers/brainstorm/10479-1792428056/content/three-directions-v4.html` (not committed) and the builder works from them and from this document.

The header, the espresso footer, `Catalog97Shell`, the saffron and espresso frame colours, and the `--c97-*` tokens all stay. Nothing in `catalog97.css` changes. Each page keeps its `data-c97-surface` sections and reads colour only through the tokens, so dark mode comes for free, and the only shadow is the hard offset, which the mockups draw in the page's second ink. `.c97-offset` paints `--c97-overprint`, so where the object's offset is blue or vermilion the component sets `--c97-overprint` on the offset's own wrapper to the matching token, and never a hex, and never on the object's root, since the same token drives the poster text-shadow and the name on the résumé sheet keeps its vermilion overprint over a blue sheet offset.

## About, the proof

One paper section. At 881px and above it is a two-column grid, the portrait plate at 260px on the left and the document on the right. The plate is the existing `Catalog97Slot` with `data-c97-paint="portrait"` inside the `data-c97-monet` section, so the painted hover reveal and the Gecko print filter both keep working. It carries the vermilion hard offset and a small caption row under it in the kicker style, "Plate 1 · portrait" on the left. The mockup put "Berkeley, 2025" on the right of that row, and that is a guess about the photo, so the right side of the caption is left empty unless Isaac supplies the place and year. There are no registration marks, which Isaac asked to remove.

The document starts with the h1 in poster type, "Before I trust a number, I check what's producing it.", balanced over its lines at about 52px on desktop. Under it the proof grid runs the bio copy on the left at the running measure and the margin on the right at 230px. The copy is the four first-person paragraphs as the approved mockup has them, which differ from the 2026-10-18 build in two places, since the third paragraph gains the clause the third note marks ("where correcting one click event moved the funnel's biggest drop off a step earlier") and loses "because I wanted to move fully into product work", and the fourth loses the sentence that began "since almost every useful thing I did there". Four claims in them are marked. A marked claim gets a highlight, which is a tint of the blue ink under the text (the mockup uses an 18% blue wash over the lower 40% of the line box, drawn with `color-mix` on the accent token so it survives dark mode), followed by a small italic serif superscript number in the accent. Every text mark on the proof, meaning the superscripts, the "n · checked" labels, the diamonds, and the source links, takes `--c97-accent`, which is maroon on paper by day and rose by night, since the riso blue has no dark twin and reads at about 2:1 on dark paper. Isaac chose this over adding a dark blue to the system on 2026-10-19, so blue stays only where it is decoration, on the offsets and the stamp, and there it follows the system's own dark-mode flip. The number is a link to the note (`href="#note-1"` and so on) so a keyboard or screen reader user can reach the evidence.

The margin is a hairline rule on its left with four notes down it, each with a small accent diamond on the rule, an accent italic label "1 · checked", the note in the serif at 13px, and a "Source · link" line in the kicker style where the link is in the accent and underlined. The notes and their sources are, in order, the TextOut alpha demos with the fix shipping within a week (source the Civitech write-up), the $4M pricing figure for 2024 (source the résumé), the Juno finding that the drop off moved to approved members who never clicked through to a lender (source the Juno write-up), and the dashboards' source and as-of stamps and last-good fallback (source `/dashboards`). The wording in the mockup came from memory of the site, so before any note ships the builder opens the write-up or data file it cites and matches the note's facts to it, and where they disagree the note changes and the source stays. The reviewer already matched the first note to the Civitech write-up and the first sentence of the third to the Juno write-up, and the mockup's second sentence of the third note (the advising-meetings comparison) is a different finding, so it is cut and the note is its first sentence. "How I work" is no longer a section, since the notes are the evidence for how I work, and the three principle passages from the 2026-10-18 build are cut.

The FAQ closes the page as a specimen strip, spanning the full document width under the copy and margin, with an ink rule above and below. On the left, "Specimen / Questions I get asked" in small poster type, and then four columns, one per question, each with the question in the serif and the answer in the body face at 11.5px. The data stays in `src/constants/aboutFaq.ts`, which `about/page.tsx` also reads for the FAQ structured data, so the on-page text and the structured data stay identical by construction. The answers stay as they are, which is my call, because the second one carries the $4M figure, TextOut, and RunningMate in 48 words and a 25-word cut would drop facts from structured data search engines already index, so the strip's columns run as long as the answers do.

Below 880px the plate sits above the document at a width of `min(72%, 300px)`, the margin drops under the copy as the same four notes in one column, the superscripts still link to them, and the specimen strip becomes one column. The timeline, pull quote, blue sheet, bone sheet, and button pair from earlier builds are all gone, and the two exits from the hero (See the work, Résumé) are gone with them, since the specimen strip and the notes already link out and the header carries the routes.

## Résumé, the sheet on the press bed

One bone section, the press bed, full width. On it a centred three-column grid, a 180px rail, the sheet at `minmax(0, 680px)`, and a 180px rail, with the bed's gutter around. The sheet is paper with 40px by 44px padding, a blue hard offset of 10px, and letter proportions, meaning `aspect-ratio: 8.5 / 11` so the sheet is never shorter than a page and grows with its content. It will run longer than one page with the real entries, which Isaac confirmed on 2026-10-19 is fine, since the on-screen sheet is the full record and the PDF is its own one-page document, so nothing in the `experience`, `education`, or `capabilities` arrays is cut or merged to fit. Everything on the sheet is set tight the way the one-pager prints, at 11px body with the serif for titles and dates and the poster face for the name and the section headings.

The sheet reads top to bottom as the name in poster type with the overprint, a two by two contact block under it in the kicker style (Berkeley, California, the email, the LinkedIn handle, the GitHub handle) closed by an ink rule, then Experience, Education, Capabilities, and Outside work, each with a small poster heading over a hairline. Experience and Education entries are the existing `experience` and `education` arrays on a two-column grid, the date at 112px in the serif and nowrap, then the title in the serif at 13px, the company line in the kicker style with the existing `Catalog97CompanyMark` logo, and the description. Capabilities is a two-column ledger of all seven groups in the `capabilities` array, category then tools. Outside work is the `interests` string as one line. The existing print-only pressman's line ("I set this page in Berkeley, and you printed it…") becomes the sheet's foot on screen as well, in italic serif at the bottom right over a hairline, since it is the one line on the page that explains the object.

The left rail holds the kicker "Press bed · one sheet", one sentence ("The full version, set in Berkeley. The PDF is the one-page version."), and the download button. The button's visible text stays "Download PDF" with `href` to `RESUME_PDF` and the `download` attribute, since `e2e/resume.spec.ts` pins that text and the 200 on the file, and the rail's sentence is what carries the "take the sheet" idea. The right rail holds the kicker "Also on this site", a sentence that the Juno and Civitech entries each have a write-up, the two write-up links as ghost links, then the kicker "References", the sentence "I'm happy to walk through any project on this site line by line.", and the "Get in touch" ghost link to `/contact`. Both rails start level with the sheet's top edge. The screen-only write-up links beside each experience entry in the 2026-10-18 build go, since the rail holds them once.

Below 880px the download rail sits above the sheet, the sheet fills the width with 24px padding and the date moves above the title the way the existing module already does under 600px, and the right rail sits below the sheet. On paper the bed is paper, the download rail is hidden, the sheet prints without its offset at the page width, and the right rail prints after the sheet at the sheet's left edge, so the "Get in touch" link and the paragraph before it share a left edge, which is what the résumé spec measures in print media. The page has one `h1`, the name on the sheet, in poster type.

## Contact, the reply card

One paper section with the card centred in it and one sentence under the card. The card is `min(100%, 740px)` wide at a 6 by 4 ratio that grows with content, paper, a 1px ink border, a vermilion hard offset of 10px, and inside it two halves split by a 1px ink rule, the left at 1fr and the right at 1.15fr, with 28px by 30px padding.

The left half is the message. The h1 "I'd like to hear from you." in poster type at 34px, which `e2e/footer-cta.spec.ts` pins, then the paragraph ("I'm looking for a full-time product role that starts after I graduate from Haas in May 2027, and I'm always glad to compare notes on product, analytics, or fintech. If something on this site looks wrong to you, I want to hear about that too."), then "Name the project in the subject line so I can pull up the details before I reply." one size smaller, and at the foot of the half, pushed down with `margin-top: auto`, the kicker "Reply within two working days · if it's been longer, send it again".

The right half is the address side. Top right is the stamp, an 84px by 98px dashed ink box holding a blue block rotated 6 degrees with "Isaac Vazquez" in poster type and "Berkeley · CA · 2026" under it. The year is the year the page was last set, so it reads from the same dated constant the sitemap entry for `/contact` uses, and it is not a clock. At the foot of the half, the four address lines, each a label in the kicker style at 56px and a value in the serif over an ink rule, To "Isaac Vazquez, Berkeley, CA", Email as the largest line at 19px and underlined, LinkedIn as the handle, GitHub as the handle. The email is a `mailto:` link and the two handles open their profiles in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, and every one of the three is at least 44px tall as a target through padding on the link. Under the card, centred, "The résumé has the short version of my background, and the work index has the long one." with the two inline links. The vermilion slogan band, the peach band, the blue "Elsewhere" sheet, and the Email me button from earlier builds are gone.

Below 700px the halves stack, message then address, the rule between them becomes horizontal, the ratio is released, and the stamp stays at the top right of the address half.

## Tests that change, and why

`e2e/print.spec.ts` pins three things on `/about` that this design removes on purpose, and each changes in the same commit with the reason in the message. The deckle seam count of one goes, because the proof is one sheet with no second surface, and the seam clip-path test reads the deckle from `/dashboards` instead, which carries one (so does `/writing`, and Home carries none). `/about` leaves `NARROW_GRIDS` and the stacked-gaps loop, because the proof uses no `c97-columns`, and its grid collapse is covered by `scripts/layoutSweep.mjs` at the phone sizes instead. The `img.c97-slot-img` filter check on `/about` stays and must keep passing, because the plate is still a `Catalog97Slot`. `e2e/resume.spec.ts`, `e2e/footer-cta.spec.ts`, `e2e/portfolio-shell.spec.ts`, and `e2e/navigation.spec.ts` do not change. `src/app/__tests__` and `src/constants/__tests__` run as they are.

## What changes beside the components

`Catalog97About.tsx`, `Catalog97Resume.tsx`, `Catalog97Contact.tsx`, and their modules carry the three objects. `aboutFaq.ts` carries the shortened answers. `COMPONENTS.md` and `STYLING.md` describe the three pages as one object each. `docs/EASTER_EGGS.md` keeps the portrait paint entry as it is, since the trigger and the markup are unchanged.
