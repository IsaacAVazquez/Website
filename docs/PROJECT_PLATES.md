# Project plates

The Before You Buy plate is at `public/images/home/retro-before-you-buy.jpg`, encoded at 1200 by 800 pixels. Home, the portfolio, and the build article use it. The image was created on October 9, 2026 with original generation and no reference image.

The final prompt was:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Create a finished editorial risograph illustration for the 'Before You Buy' portfolio project on a personal website. Landscape 3:2 composition, output 1536x1024 or 1200x800. Top-down flat-lay of two slightly overlapping cream-paper portfolio worksheets, one with a simple hand-drawn sector pie chart and the other showing a similar pie chart with one added slice, beside a short wooden vermilion pencil and a small dark analog calculator. Convey comparing a portfolio before and after one stock purchase. Match a vintage printed financial desk illustration: warm saffron yellow paper background, cream paper, near-black ink, subtle vermilion accents, visible coarse halftone dots, offset ink registration, paper grain and worn edges, flat graphic shapes, no gradients. Worksheets should have abstract unlabeled charts and sparse rules, no readable text or numbers, no logos or company marks, no people, no watermark. Strong simple composition legible in a small project card. Illustration only, no frame, no UI screenshot.
```
