## Redesign, 2026-10-12 (project-sovereign stage 2, Reference archetype, an archive)

This is the first brief for the route, written when it took its own design inside the site frame (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-11-project-sovereign-design.md`, G8).

The genre is a bracketology column with its bracket, the kind ESPN's Bracketology and The Athletic's bracket breakdowns are. A reference piece keeps a display title and a written introduction, so the page opens on the title, one standfirst, and its links, and then the bracket is the first thing on the screen.

What the page is now. The client root is `.mm-root`, which paints a newsprint paper with `--mm-*` properties (an off-white in light, a near-black in dark), a navy for links and focus, and an orange-red for upset marks, and it redeclares the `--c97-*` tokens the borrowed table, chip, and button classes and the bracket SVG read. Newsreader is the display face for the title, headings, standfirst, and site names, Archivo is the body, Fragment Mono carries seeds, figures, and labels, and no typeface was added. Inside a `c97-shell c97-frame` column the head holds the as-of kicker, the h1 "March Madness Bracket Analysis", the standfirst, and four links (See best upsets, How the model works, Read the companion article, Open the analysis workspace). The region bracket follows under an "East region" style heading with the region switch, which is now the only region control on the page and drives both the drawn bracket and the matchup list in the workspace. Under it the editorial column has two halves from 900px, the Final Four path with the champion pick, its odds, my reasoning, the result note, and the postmortem link on the left, and the three top upset picks as ruled entries on the right. The workspace follows with its tabs and the copy-link control in one bar, and the method ("Why this model is different", one h3 per model pillar) and the bracket questions close the page as prose, then one sources line.

Decisions made in this pass. The bracket SVG is 984px wide at full size and the frame's column is 1080px at 1440, so the editorial column cannot sit beside the bracket without shrinking the bracket's type. It sits directly under the bracket instead, and a container query puts it beside the bracket once the frame is at least 1340px wide, which only happens if the route joins `WIDE_TOOL_ROUTES` (proposed to the lead, not done). The page used to remount the whole client through a `key` on every URL change, which dropped keyboard focus off the tab that made the change, so the client now follows the URL by comparing its last synced state during render, and the arrow keys keep focus on the region tabs. While one of its own tab changes is pending it only adopts the reading for the state it last pushed, so a late response never flips the board under a newer choice, and Back or a link click clears the pending push. The open pick lives in the client and in the URL as `?pick=` on the picks board. Opening or closing one writes the address at once with `history.replaceState`, passing the entry's own state through so the router's record of the page stays on it, and then goes through `router.replace` the way a tab change does, so the entry's page catches up with its address. The client reads the pick from the address through `useSearchParams` and the page no longer passes it down, because a reader who leaves before the router answers comes Back to an entry whose address names the pick while its page is still the one from before it. An earlier build wrote the address and read the pick from the page, and Back returned to a closed board. The copied link, the next tab change, the three opening links that stay on the page, and the three upset links all carry the open pick. The upset and method links keep the reader's region, because each card in `march-madness-data.ts` names a workspace view and the client builds the address from it. Under 360px the team column on the time zone rows narrows from 8rem to 6rem so a one-word city like Indianapolis fits the middle column whole, and a word that still cannot fit breaks inside its column. On paper the bracket scales to the page width instead of clipping at the Elite Eight.

Cut, and not to be restored. The poster hero sheet, its three readouts (champion, time-zone flips, upsets in the region), and the plate around the bracket, the champion-pick band, the "The short version" band with its thesis and best-upset panels (the best upset was the first top-upset card and an FAQ answer already), the Final Four card trio as its own band, the method and FAQ card grids, the second set of region tabs inside the workspace, the boxed panels that sat around the round columns, the S-curve lists, each injury, and each time-zone impact, since injuries are a table now and the other two are ruled lists, the bone and paper sheets with their seams, and the raw URL printed beside the copy-link button.

Pinned and kept. Everything `e2e/march-madness.spec.ts` names, which is the h1, "Top upset picks" as one element, "Why this model is different" as the one heading and text at `#why-this-model-is-different`, the "See best upsets", "How the model works", and "Read the companion article" links and their hrefs, "S-curve seed errors" as a heading and "Vanderbilt +5" in the method section, "San Jose, CA (PT)" on the West region's matchup list, "Time zone upsets" and "Analytics upsets" once each on the picks view, the FAQPage, Article, and SportsApplication JSON-LD blocks, and no horizontal overflow. The route test's tablists ("March Madness regions", "March Madness primary sections", "March Madness analytics sections"), their `aria-selected` tabs and arrow keys, the URL each tab writes, "Open the analysis workspace" with its hash, "Copy current view link" with "Deep link copied.", every legend line, and the `?pick=` deep link all stay. The route test reads a pick toggle off the router call and off the address it writes, and the e2e spec opens a pick, follows the postmortem link, and expects Back to return to it open, once on a fast connection and once with the route's navigation responses held for 900ms. The "Other impacts" rows on the time zone view print the whole city with its zone suffix dropped ("PT to Oklahoma City"), and the first method pillar's note is a line about the board, since "Open the full rankings table" read as a link beside the real one. The picks stay as I made them on March 17, 2026, beside the result note.
