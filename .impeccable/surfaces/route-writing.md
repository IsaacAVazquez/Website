# Writing index surface brief

**Scope.** The `/writing` route, rendered by `src/components/catalog97/Catalog97Writing.tsx`. One of the seven Catalog 97 surfaces.

**Visitor mode.** Read. Structure for comprehension first.

**Job / action.** Find something worth reading among ~200 pieces and start reading it.

**Route constraints.** Client component; the cluster, length and archive-bucket filter is deliberate and is NOT in the source design, which drew a flat archive for eight mock pieces. The archive band is Paper, not Pine, because at ~200 rows it is over 90% of the route height and whatever field it takes becomes the route. Pine sits on the bounded featured pair instead. This route used to be the deliberate low-Pine outlier at ~1.2%, and it is 11.0% as of 2026-08-03, measured at 1440 over the whole `.c97-page` including chrome. Two changes moved it and neither was a design decision about colour. The archive renders 30 rows rather than 195, so the paper band that used to be over 90% of the route no longer swamps everything, and the pine wordmark band above the footer is gone because the emblem moved into the footer. The reasoning behind the low-Pine choice is unchanged and still correct, since the archive would take the route over again the moment it is expanded. Recompute rather than trusting the number if the archive page size changes.

**Settled 2026-08-03 (layout, mobile). The archive rows were starving their own content, and that was the real reason this route was 26 screens on a phone.** `.c97-row` is `1fr auto`, and `auto` takes its max-content width before `1fr` gets anything. At 390 the meta cell, "5 MIN READ · SPORTS ANALYTICS", measured 199px and left the title and excerpt 98px, so one row wrapped to 817px and the median archive row was 620px tall. The archive was 83% of a 22,309px page because of it. The archive articles now carry `c97-row-stack-sm`, an opt-in class that collapses the row to one column below 640px. The median row is 235px, the page is 11,372px, 13.5 screens, and desktop is untouched at `837.766px 210.234px` with a 147px median. The portfolio ledger deliberately does not take the class, because its second cell is a bare year that already fits beside the title and stacking would only add a line to each of 29 rows. Reducing `archivePageSize` was considered first and was the wrong lever, since it would have hidden the symptom while leaving every rendered row malformed.

**The featured band overflowed the page at 320, and five siblings could have.** Its `repeat(auto-fit,minmax(300px,1fr))` kept a 300px floor inside a 249px shell, which pushed the document to 328px and produced real horizontal scroll. A bare `minmax(Npx, 1fr)` does not yield to its container. Every auto-fit floor in the Catalog 97 code is now wrapped in `min(100%, N)`, six in total across this file, `Catalog97Contact`, `Catalog97Resume` twice, `.c97-mosaic` and `.c97-columns`. Three of the six genuinely overflowed at 320 and the other three survived only because their floors happened to be narrower than the shell. Verified at 320 with zero horizontal scroll and zero overflowing elements on this route and on `/dashboards`, `/portfolio` and `/about`.

**The filter row's row gap is `--c97-sp-5`.** This is the longest of the three filter rows and wraps at every width, and `--c97-sp-3` clamps to 22px on a phone against 50px `.c97-microlink` hit boxes. See `route-dashboards.md` for the measurement that found it. Zero overlapping pairs at 320 with the rows 57px apart.

**Settled 2026-08-03 (archive length). The archive renders 30 rows and offers the rest behind one control.** Unfiltered this index is 197 pieces, 2 featured and 195 in the archive, and it rendered the route at 42,598px, about 47 screens at 1440, with well over 90% of that height being the one list. This brief already treated the archive as unbounded, which is why it sits on Paper rather than Pine, but moving the field off it never made it shorter. The default view is 9,323px now, 10.4 screens, and one click on "Show the rest of the archive" restores all 195 rows and 42,590px, so nothing is unreachable. At 390 the default is 22,309px against a 50px control that clears the 44px floor and collides with nothing. `archivePageSize` is 30 and the limit resets to it whenever a filter tab is clicked, because a new filter is a new list. Two things about the implementation are load-bearing. It slices rather than hiding with CSS, since hiding leaves the height in place and the height is the defect. And discovery does not depend on the rows being in the initial HTML, because every post is written into the sitemap by `scripts/generatePublicSitemap.mjs` at build time, so this is not a crawlability trade. The count opposite the "Archive" heading still reports the true total rather than the visible one, and the number on the button is what remains folded away, so the two numbers never contradict each other.

**Settled 2026-08-03 (heading sweep, degraded single-context).** "Archive" was an `h2` carrying `c97-kicker`, so it rendered at 11px directly above 26px `h3` children. It carries `c97-serif c97-h2` now. `c97-h3` is not available as the fix here, because the archive rows already draw at that step and the header would have landed exactly on its own children, which is the trap the machine-level notes describe. The featured pair moved with it, from `c97-h3` to `c97-h2`. Those two titles were drawing at 26px, the same step as the archive rows below them, so the route's two tiers were typographically identical and the whole distinction rested on the image slot and the "Featured" kicker, which is not what the band's own comment claims for them. Every `h2` on the route is one size now, 32px at 1440 and 24px at 390, against `h3` at 26px and 20px. Verified live at both widths with no heading overflowing its box, no horizontal scroll and no element overflow. This was the fifth of the seven Catalog 97 routes to carry the 11px section-heading defect.

**Visual world: Catalog 97, not Working Instrument.** `DESIGN.md` describes The Working Instrument and governs the other ~40 routes. It does NOT govern this surface. `context.mjs` auto-loads it anyway, so a pass that treats it as the spec here will read every deliberate Catalog 97 decision as a violation and manufacture a page of false findings. Tokens live in `src/app/catalog97.css`.

**Do not re-litigate (settled 2026-08-02).**
- Tobacco is a large-text-only field. Paper ink on it is 4.36:1, which clears 3:1 for large text and never clears 4.5:1 for body. A tobacco band carries `--c97-fs-h2` (24px floor) and up, or no text at all. Not a defect; solved by size rather than by darkening the colour.
- Anton draws numerals at `--c97-fs-plate` only. Never running text, never headings, and there is no smaller numeral step. This replaced the earlier one-Anton-per-view rule.
- The scales are frozen: 9 type steps, 8 spacing values (`--c97-gutter` plus `--c97-sp-1..7`), 4 line heights, 3 measures plus `--c97-container`. `--c97-touch-y/x` sit outside the spacing ladder on purpose, solved backwards from the 44px floor.
- Every `--c97-*` value is declared under `[data-c97]` or `[data-c97-surface]`, never under a class, so a token consumer can skip class rules wholesale.
- Image slots render as flat Stone or Tobacco fields because no photograph exists yet. That is the design’s own layout rule, not an unfinished asset.

**Verified state.** 0 contrast failures across 2774 text nodes in both themes, each resolved against its own enclosing surface and backdrop. Zero shadows, zero radii above 2px, zero off-palette hex in components. `focus-visible` covers anchors, buttons, inputs, textareas, selects and summaries. Exactly one `aria-current` per route. Espresso footer present. No two Pine bands adjacent, and at least two brown bands per route.

**Commands worth running.** `critique` and `audit` to find. Then only what the findings name. `polish` last. Never `document` here (it would regenerate DESIGN.md against the wrong world), and never both halves of `bolder`/`quieter` or `overdrive`/`distill`.

**Settled 2026-08-02 (critique, degraded single-context).** The anchor reset is `:where(.c97-page) a`, not `.c97-page a`. At `.c97-page a` it scored (0,1,1) and outranked every single-class component rule, so each `.c97-btn` rendered as a `<Link>` discarded its own `color: var(--c97-surface)` and inherited the band ink instead. Measured live: "See the work" was espresso on chocolate at 1.4:1, and `.c97-btn-invert`, whose background is `--c97-ink`, was heading for 1:1 invisible text on six routes. All 13 buttons on the site are anchors, so all 13 were affected. Static token-math audits miss this because they read the declared rule rather than the winning cascade. Do not raise the `:where()` back to a plain class, and re-measure buttons in a browser after any change to the anchor reset.

**Settled 2026-08-02 (mobile pass).** The header wordmark carries `.c97-brand`, which applies the same padding-plus-negative-margin hit target as `.c97-microlink`. At 390px it measured a 23px tap target before this, under the 44px floor the rest of the site holds; it is 55px now. Dark mode is verified live rather than by token math: all eight surfaces render their derived dark values and both themes measure zero contrast failures. When re-measuring after a theme toggle, wait a tick before reading computed styles — reading in the same synchronous block returns the pre-toggle paint and looks like dark mode is broken when it is not.
