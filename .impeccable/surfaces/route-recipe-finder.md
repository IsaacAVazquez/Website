# Recipe Finder surface brief

Checked against the code on 2026-11-02.

Written on 2026-10-12 by the stage 2 project sovereign pass (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-11-project-sovereign-design.md`, archetype Log and Calculator, group G7).

Scope. The `/recipe-finder` route, rendered by `src/app/recipe-finder/recipe-finder-client.tsx` with `recipe-finder.css`. The pantry lives in `localStorage` under `recipe-finder:pantry:v1`, cleaned on every read and write by the client's own `cleanPantryItems`. The recipes are the hand-built corpus in `src/data/recipesSnapshot.ts`, matched by `searchRecipes` in `src/lib/recipes.ts`, with the staples exemption inside `scoreRecipe` in the same file, which `indexCard.ts` reuses for each card's ticks and counts. The stage 2 pass changed none of that. Three corrections came after it. `searchRecipes` now puts the text it searches through the same `normalizeIngredient` as the query, so a hyphenated title or tag ("Stir-Fry", "one-pan") finds itself. The empty-pantry order is pinned to the `en` collation, so the server and a browser in any language print the cards in one order. And the mug cake's "2 tbsp neutral oil" is a staple, as the same line already was in the chicken fried rice and the tofu stir-fry. A meal plan came on 2026-10-22 (`f1d5b56de`, with fixes in `c7bd624dc` and `5e277bd07`), and its saved recipes and planned meals live in `localStorage` under `recipe-finder-plan-v1`, read and written through `useBrowserRecord` with `parseRecipePlan` from `src/lib/recipePlan.ts`.

Visitor mode. Operate. The visitor lists what is in the kitchen and reads down the cards for what to cook.

Genre. A pantry-first recipe app, with SuperCook as the reference and a kitchen recipe box of index cards as the object.

## Decisions

The route paints its own kitchen paper. `.rf-root` declares `--rf-paper`, `--rf-card`, `--rf-field`, `--rf-ink`, `--rf-ink-2`, `--rf-red`, `--rf-blue`, and `--rf-rule`, with a light story (paper `#f6f3ec`, card stock `#fffdf8`, index-card red `#a8321f`, ruled blue) and a dark one under `.dark .rf-root` (`#161311`, card `#221d1a`, `#f2917e`). It redeclares the `--c97-*` tokens the borrowed kicker, skeleton, and ghost button read, and `--c97-accent` is the red, so the focus ring matches. Newsreader for the h1, the section heads, and the recipe titles, Fragment Mono for the pantry labels, the quick adds, the times, the counts, the step numbers, and the card's tags and staple marks, Archivo for the rest. No added typeface.

The first screen is a title row (the h1 and a lede that carries the staples exemption, worded as "Staples like salt, pepper, cooking oil, and water" because ice is one too and sesame oil is not) and the book under it. From 1024px the pantry shelf is a sticky left column (the Pantry heading, the input with its suggestions and Add ingredient, the pantry labels with Remove and Clear, the quick adds, and one line saying the corpus is hand-built and the pantry is saved in this browser only), and the results are the right column. Under 1024px the shelf comes first.

The shelf sticks only on a window at least 820px tall. There it is capped at the window's height (`max-height: calc(100dvh - 2 * var(--c97-sp-3))`) and scrolls inside itself once the pantry outgrows it, so Clear and the quick adds stay in reach, with inline padding cancelled by a negative margin as room for the focus rings. It sets no `overscroll-behavior`, the same as the stuck column in `world-cup.css`, so the wheel passes on to the page at the shelf's ends. I left it off because a December 2025 CSS working group message says Chrome is moving to apply that property to a scroll container with nothing to scroll, where a shelf that fits would hold the wheel and stop the page under the pointer. I did not confirm that against Chrome's release notes or in a browser. A pantry label wraps (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`), so a pasted link or any other unbroken run of up to 60 characters stays inside the shelf.

Enter and a comma commit the typed ingredient. Tab does not, so it moves on to the suggestions under the field, which a keyboard could not reach while Tab saved the half-typed word.

The results open on "Find something to cook" with the search box, then the filters as chips (the four views, then the eight meal times) and the diet select, then one live count, "N of 26 recipes", which adds ", ranked by how much of each recipe your pantry covers" once the pantry has something in it. That wording follows the sort, which is the share of a recipe's ingredients the pantry covers and not the count still missing. The same line ends with a screen-reader-only ", N in your pantry", so an add or a remove that leaves the visible count as it was still gives the live region something to announce. The number on the "Suggested by pantry" chip is the number of recipes that view lists across the whole corpus, meaning every recipe the pantry matches at least one ingredient of.

Each recipe is an index card, and the card is the one box, since it carries the recipe's own data. It has card stock with a hairline edge, the title with cuisine and meal type, the time and servings in the corner (under the title and left-aligned under 640px and on paper), a red head rule, "Missing N of M ingredients" or "You have every ingredient", and the Ingredients and steps toggle, which opens the ingredient lines on blue rules with tick boxes and the numbered steps. A tick box takes a red check where the pantry or the staples cover its line, a staple line carries a small "staple" mark, and the recipe's tags follow the steps. The cards fill a grid of columns at least 19rem wide. When nothing matches, the grid gives way to "No recipes match these filters." and a Reset filters button, or, in the pantry view with an empty shelf, to "Your pantry shelf is empty, so there is nothing to suggest yet." and Show all recipes.

The meal plan came after the stage 2 pass, on 2026-10-22. Each card ends with Save recipe, which reads Saved once pressed, and Plan meal, which picks that recipe in the plan's form and scrolls to the plan. A "Saved recipes only (N)" checkbox sits between the count line and the cards and narrows them to the saved ones, and the count follows it. The plan is its own section under the book, "Your meal plan", with a Week starting date that fills in today after mount, a form with Recipe to plan (saved recipes first, marked "(saved)"), a Meal date held to that week, and Add meal, then the week's meals with Remove meal, and "Shopping list for this week", which groups what the pantry and the staples leave missing, keeps each meal's amount, and offers Download shopping list as `shopping-list.txt`. The plan keeps at most 366 meals. The same change named the count line "Recipe results", since the plan's message and its storage notice are status regions too. The first-card coordinates under Verified state predate the checkbox.

Print hides the pantry form, the quick adds, Clear, the search, the filters, and the card toggles, and keeps the pantry labels and the cards.
