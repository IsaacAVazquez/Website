# Museum Log surface brief

Written on 2026-10-12 by the stage 2 project sovereign pass (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-11-project-sovereign-design.md`, archetype Log, group G7).

Scope. The `/museum-log` route, rendered by `src/app/museum-log/museum-log-client.tsx` with `museum-log.css`. The catalog, reviews, diary, and lists are the curated `src/data/museumSnapshot.ts`. The reader's visits, watchlist, and likes live in `localStorage` under `museum_log_user_state_v1` through `useMuseumLog`, and the view, museum, list, sort, type, and region live in the URL through `museum-log-state.ts`. None of that changed.

Visitor mode. Browse, then operate. The visitor scans the catalog, opens a museum, and logs a visit with a rating and a note.

Genre. Letterboxd for museums. The catalog grid is the first screen, each museum drawn as its admission ticket the way Letterboxd draws a poster, with the reader's own log as the thing they come back to.

## Decisions

The route paints its own gallery wall. `.mlog-root` declares `--mlog-wall`, `--mlog-ticket`, `--mlog-field`, `--mlog-ink`, `--mlog-ink-2`, `--mlog-blue`, and `--mlog-rule`, with a light story (gallery white `#f3f1ec`, ticket stock `#fbfaf7`, museum blue `#284785`) and a dark one under `.dark .mlog-root` (slate `#14181c`, ticket `#1c2228`, pale blue `#93b3ef`). It redeclares `--c97-surface`, `--c97-panel`, `--c97-field`, `--c97-ink`, `--c97-ink-2`, `--c97-label`, `--c97-rule`, `--c97-accent`, and the three status tokens, so the borrowed field, range, chip, kicker, ghost button, and the journal's `c97-row` read the route's palette. Newsreader for the headings, the museum names on the tickets, and the exhibit titles, Fragment Mono for prices, ratings, and counts, Archivo for the rest. No added typeface.

The title row is the h1, one sentence, and the curator and updated line, and once the reader has logged a visit, their last three visits print as small tickets ("Your last visits") that open the museum. On a first visit nothing prints there. From a viewport of 1173px the strip sits beside the title. Its basis is 36rem, which holds three tickets across and, with the title's 26rem and the gap, fits one row of the 1080px shell from that width. The strip arrives once the saved log is read, and beside the title it narrows the lede, so the catalog moves down by a line of the lede from 1198px and by two between 1173px and 1197px (27px and 49.5px in the browser pass on 2026-10-12). Under 1173px the client prints the strip at the end of the main column, under a hairline, which is after the catalog and before the rail on a phone, so it arrives without moving anything above it. On a row of its own over the catalog it had pushed the first ticket down by 304px with two visits and 437px with three at 390px wide. The client picks the place from a `matchMedia` read of `(min-width: 1173px)`, so the strip is one element and the tab order follows the page at every width. That read is the viewport's, so a scrollbar that takes room can still wrap the strip onto its own row for the first few pixels past 1173px. Visits logged on the same day print latest first.

Under it one ruled bar holds the Discover, Journal, and Lists switch (an underlined tab, still `aria-pressed` buttons inside the "Section navigation" nav) and the catalog's one summary line, which carries the catalog, review, and diary counts, how many museums have an exhibit on view today, how many the reader has visited, and the verify-before-visiting sentence word for word. The visited count prints a dash until the saved log is read, so the server render never states a zero. The visited sentence is one inline box as wide as its widest reading, which the client hands the stylesheet in `data-widest`, so the count taking the dash's place cannot rewrap the summary. Before that the line count changed at 11 widths between 320px and 1200px, 440px among them.

Discover is the catalog grid under a toolbar with no box around it, holding the search, the Type and Region selects, and the sort buttons, then the count line. Each museum is its admission ticket (the one box, since the ticket is the object and carries the museum's own name, city, founding year, admission, curator rating, and an On view now chip), then the reader's own visit line, the blurb, and the Log visit, Watchlist, and Like actions, with no panel around the pair. The grid is two columns beside the rail on desktop, since a third column broke the museum names mid-word.

A museum opens in place of the grid as one ruled column, the ticket and the header (type and region, the h2, country and average visit, the curator's and the reader's ratings, the blurb, the actions, and the official site), then Curator review, What to actually see, Exhibition calendar, Your activity (the rate-and-log form), Curator's past visits, and Appears in, each under an h3 and a hairline. The past visits are the curator's diary rows for that museum, and the heading says so because they sit right under the reader's own activity. An Appears in link wraps, since a catalogue's title is longer than a phone's column. The h2 for the active view stays in the DOM as a visually hidden heading while a museum is open, since it is where focus returns on Back to catalog.

The rail is ruled, not boxed, and holds Top liked (or the curator's favorites before the reader likes one) in Discover, Journal, and Lists, or the museum's neighbours in its region while a museum is open, and the one storage note.
