# Catalog 97 shell surface brief

**Scope.** `src/components/catalog97/Catalog97Shell.tsx`. It supplies the header, the wordmark band, the only `main` landmark, and the footer for all seven Catalog 97 routes, so an edit here changes every one of them at once. `StaticHeader` and `ConditionalLayout` both stand down on these routes, which is why this file owns the chrome rather than sharing it.

**Visitor mode.** Operate. The footer is wayfinding and the header is navigation, so scanability and consistency outrank expression in both.

**Job / action.** Get oriented, then leave for the right route.

**Visual world: Catalog 97, not Working Instrument.** `DESIGN.md` describes The Working Instrument and governs the other ~40 routes. It does NOT govern this file. `context.mjs` auto-loads it anyway, so a pass that treats it as the spec here will read every deliberate Catalog 97 decision as a violation and manufacture false findings. Tokens live in `src/app/catalog97.css`.

**Settled 2026-08-03 (layout). The footer has its own grid and no longer borrows `.c97-columns`.** The footer holds one colophon paragraph and three short link lists, and those are not equivalent content, so equal tracks were a framework default rather than a decision. On four equal 219px tracks the colophon set at 27 characters a line, well under the 34ch its own `--c97-measure-body` already allows, which means the track and not the token was what bound it. `.c97-footer-grid` gives the colophon `2fr` against `1fr` for each list above 1080px, so at 1440 the tracks are 350px and three of 175px, the paragraph reaches its 34ch cap at 284px, and "Accessibility", the widest label at 124px, still clears its 175px column with room. The footer also came down from 510px to 436px, because the paragraph stopped wrapping into a ribbon.

**Why the breakpoints are 640 and 1080.** `--c97-gutter` is `clamp(28px, 6vw, 96px)`, so the shell keeps shrinking as the viewport does and a naive breakpoint puts the colophon back under 280px. At a 1080px viewport the gutter has grown to 6vw and the shell is only 950px, where `2fr` still yields about 299px, which is the reason the four-up waits until 1080 rather than starting around 900. Between 640 and 1080 the three lists sit side by side and the colophon spans the whole row above them, measured at 900 as three 229px columns under a 777px colophon. Below 640 everything stacks. Verified at 1440, 900 and 390 with no horizontal scroll and nothing overflowing at any of them.

**Checked and deliberately left alone.**
- Footer link targets are fine and were the first thing suspected. `footerLinkStyle` sets `minHeight: 44` and `.c97-microlink` adds padding plus a negative margin on top of that, which is exactly the combination that produced overlapping hit boxes in the `/dashboards` tablist. Measured here at 1440, 900 and 390 it is 44px minimum with zero overlapping pairs, because the nav gap is `--c97-sp-1` and the rows never come close enough. Do not "fix" this, and do measure again if the nav gap ever tightens.
- The footer content aligns with the body content exactly. Header, body bands, wordmark and footer all put their `.c97-shell` at the same left edge, 172.5px at 1440, all 1080px wide. An earlier read that suggested a 7px drift came from comparing rounded numbers across two separate runs and was wrong.
- The `c97-kicker` column headings are `div` rather than headings, which is correct. A footer does not need to inject four more entries into the document outline, and each `nav` already carries an `aria-label` from the same string.

**Settled 2026-08-03 (layout, second pass). The header nav was overlapping its own tap targets on every phone, on all seven routes.** This is the most serious thing found in the whole Catalog 97 layout sweep. The nav had a single `gap: var(--c97-sp-3)`, which is 32px at 1440 where the seven links sit on one row and the gap never applies vertically, but clamps to 22px at 390 where they wrap to two rows. That put the rows 39px apart while every `.c97-microlink` hit box is 50px tall, so five pairs overlapped: Home over About by 10px vertically and 52px horizontally, Work over Résumé, Writing over both Résumé and Contact, and Dashboards over Contact. At 320 it wraps to three rows and the problem compounds. The nav now carries `columnGap: var(--c97-sp-3)` and `rowGap: var(--c97-sp-5)`, which is 40px on a phone and holds the rows 57 to 65px apart. Verified at 320 and 390 on four routes with zero overlapping pairs, and the header grew only 16px at 390. The column gap was deliberately not touched, because seven links do not fit on one row at any phone width and tightening it only crowds them.

**The active link's rule no longer adds height.** The `aria-current` link renders a 2px accent rule as a second grid row, which made its hit box 60px against 50px for the other six, and which link is active changes per route, so the tallest box moved around. The rule now carries `marginBottom: calc(-2px - var(--c97-sp-1))`, which takes its own height back out of the box. It measures 58px rather than 50px, because the negative margin cancels the rule but not the grid gap above it, and 58px still clears the 57px row separation. The rule itself is unchanged on screen at 2px tall and 52px wide, still inside its link.

**The footer is two columns on a phone, not one.** Stacked single-file the three link lists ran 552px of the footer's 989px at 390, which is more than a whole screen of chrome. The base grid is `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(min(100%, 130px), 1fr))` with the colophon spanning the full row, so it is two columns at 390 and falls back to one at 320, where two columns would leave 112px against the roughly 115px "ACCESSIBILITY" needs. The footer measures 772px at 390 now, down 217px, with zero overlapping link pairs at 320, 390 and 1440.

**Changed 2026-08-03 at Isaac's direction. The script emblem is the colophon, and the pine wordmark band is gone.** The mark used to sit on its own full-bleed pine band between the page and the footer. It now renders in the footer's first column, in place of the Colophon block, and `Catalog97Wordmark` returns the bare SVG rather than the band wrapper. Nothing else imported it and no route ever passed `wordmark`, so the change was contained to this file. The `wordmark` prop survives with a narrower job, gating the mark inside the footer rather than a band above it.

**What the emblem replaced, and what was kept.** The Colophon block held a kicker, a paragraph about the typefaces and the fail-soft convention, and the copyright line. The copyright was kept, because a footer wants one. The typography paragraph was dropped, and the fail-soft sentence it closed on is still stated on `/dashboards` itself where it is load-bearing, so no claim was lost from the site. If that paragraph is wanted back it belongs somewhere other than under the mark, since the point of the change was that the emblem should carry that column alone.

**Measured after the move.** The mark inks itself from the surface it sits on, so it needed no colour change, and paper ink on espresso measures 13.25:1. It is 350px wide and 188px tall in the 1080px shell and 319 by 171 at 390, since `.c97-wordmark` is `width: min(100%, var(--c97-wordmark-w))` and the token is 560px, so the column is always what binds it. The footer came down from 436px to 381px at 1440 and from 772px to 730px at 390, and `/about` lost 567px overall, most of that being the removed band. Zero overlapping link pairs, zero overflow and no horizontal scroll at 390 and 1440. Typecheck clean and 38 tests passing.

**The pine shares moved, and they were recomputed rather than left stale.** Removing the pine band shortens every route, which raises what remains as a fraction, so the recorded percentages were too low rather than too high. Measured at 1440 over the whole `.c97-page` including chrome, `/resume` is 14.6% against a recorded `~12%`, and `/writing` is 11.0% against a recorded `~1.2%`. Both briefs were corrected. The `/writing` jump is mostly not down to this change, because its archive renders 30 rows instead of 195 now, so the paper band that used to be over 90% of the route no longer swamps everything else, and pine's share rose accordingly. The invariants those numbers serve all still hold, with no two pine bands adjacent, the brown count untouched, and espresso still closing every route.

**Commands worth running.** `critique` and `audit`. Then only what the findings name. Never `document` here.

**Tooling note.** `detect.mjs` returns `[]` with exit 0 on this file, which means nothing was matchable rather than that nothing is wrong, and the in-page detector cannot be injected because the site's CSP blocks an external script tag. Do not weaken the CSP to run it. Measure the live DOM through Playwright instead, which is where every figure above came from.
