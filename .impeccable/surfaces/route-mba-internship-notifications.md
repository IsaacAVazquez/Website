## Redesign, 2026-10-12 (project-sovereign stage 2, Console archetype)

This is the first brief for the route, written when it took its own design inside the site frame (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-11-project-sovereign-design.md`, G8).

The genre is a job-search tracker, the kind Huntr, Teal, and LinkedIn Jobs are, so the page has an app bar, a search bar that stays in reach, postings as cards in a list, a kanban pipeline with its counts in the column heads, and a pinned to-do rail. It borrows those conventions and none of their branding.

What the page is now. The client root is `.mba-root`, which paints its own work surface with `--mba-*` properties (a cool off-white with white cards in light, a near-black with dark cards in dark) and redeclares the `--c97-*` tokens the borrowed `c97-btn`, `c97-btn-ghost`, `c97-field`, `c97-chip`, and `c97-disclosure` classes read. Those classes are restyled inside the root to sentence case with a 6px radius, and the primary action and the focus ring are the route's teal. Archivo is the face throughout, with Fragment Mono for counts, and no typeface was added. Inside a `c97-shell c97-frame` column there is a title bar with the h1 "Job search", the fused view switch (Role feed, Application pipeline, and Candidates in dev), one orienting sentence, and the app's status (the live-roles count labelled "Live roles tracked", the updated time, Refresh, notifications, and the new-since-last-visit count with Mark all seen). The private sync line prints in the title bar on the pipeline view in dev. When a board fails, the "Partial results" notice prints under the title bar on every view, so the live-roles count carries its context on the pipeline too.

The feed view is a search bar (search, location, sort, Clear filters) that sticks to the top of the feed from 768px, where it is one row, the refinements as labelled rows with pill toggles and no box (location suggestions with the count before location filtering, role type, role family, company category, sources), a results head with "Live roles", the matching count, and the filter tags, and then the postings two to a row from 1000px. A posting is a white card with the company and department, the title as an h3, location and age, one chip rail (new, status, fit, external lead, role type, category, role families), a two-line snippet, the source line, and its actions with Apply now at the right. The Show more step stays twelve, and the line under it says the feed polls every 30 minutes and links to source health. One "Sources" section closes the feed with Tracked feeds, Source health beside Search elsewhere, and Manual checks as a ruled three-column list.

The pipeline view is a toolbar (search applications, status filter, Add application, By fit, JSON backup, CSV, Import), the to-do rail (interviews in the next seven days, then "What to chase today.") pinned on the right from 1100px and first on a phone, the funnel as the strip of heads over the board once anything is tracked, and the status columns as a kanban that scrolls sideways inside the board, 85% of the width each on a phone. The column head names the status and its count, so the card no longer repeats the status chip. The dialog is a plain bordered card over `var(--c97-scrim)`.

Cut, and not to be restored. The poster hero sheet, its plate, and the placeholder funnel ("Track a role below to start filling this in."), the hero's three readouts and its jump link, every kicker, poster headline, and description opener (Filters, Live roles, Tracked companies, Search elsewhere, Manual checks, Source health, Applications, Candidates), the count-chip row under the roles heading, the three count chips on the tracked-feeds toggle, the boxed Popular locations panel, the bordered company-group boxes, the repeated fetched time in the foot line, the bone and paper sheets and their seams, source health inside the pipeline view, the status chip on pipeline cards, and the StatusPanel boxes for empty and error states, which are plain lines now.

Pinned and kept. `live-jobs-grid`, `manual-checks-grid`, `candidates-grid`, `job-card-<id>-chips` with `flex-wrap`, `tracked-companies-<category>`, `mba-toggle` on the company buttons, `#mba-role-refinements` with `hidden md:flex` and its disclosure, `#mba-source-health-heading`, the group "Job tracker view" and its labels, every filter group's aria-label, "Search roles", "Filter by location", "Sort", "Clear filters", "Show more (N of M shown)", "Updated <time>", the server's feed size, "Live roles tracked", the classes on the LinkedIn, Career page, and Apply now links, the funnel text ("Pipeline funnel", "Response rate", "N of M heard back"), the attention text and buttons, "All caught up.", the interviews agenda, the status history, "Copy path", the sync line, "By fit", and the e2e add-and-reload path. Four heading names changed with the redesign, in the same commit, in `mba-jobs-client.test.tsx` ("Live roles", "Tracked feeds", "Search elsewhere", "Source health"), and the funnel test now checks that the funnel stays out of the feed and prints over the pipeline. Nothing from `private/` is in this brief.

## Corrections, 2026-10-12

These are fixes to what the redesign shipped and to a few faults that were there before it. The layout and the cut list above stand.

The search bar pins from 768px and no longer from 640px. Between those widths it stacks to three rows, about 165px, which was close to half the height of a phone held sideways, so there it scrolls away with the page the way it did before the redesign. Where it pins it reads `--c97-z-sticky`, and every link and button in the feed outside the bar carries a `scroll-margin-top` of 5.5rem, so a control that takes focus while tabbing back up the list comes out from under the bar. The to-do rail is still pinned from 1100px, and it now stops at the window's height and scrolls inside itself, so a long list of follow-ups stays in reach while the board scrolls. Its inline padding, cancelled by a negative margin, is room for the focus rings that a scroll box would clip.

Three borrowed controls had lost a state to the route's own restyle, and each fix is one rule in `mba-jobs.css`. The route tints a field with `background-color` and never the `background` shorthand, which had cleared the arrow the frame draws on every select. A pressed ghost toggle, which on this route is only By fit, prints in ink again. The view switch clips to its radius, so its focus ring sits inside the button in the button's own text colour. `__tests__/mba-jobs-css.test.ts` holds these rules and the two pinned blocks, since jsdom applies no stylesheet.

The to-do panel prints "Loading follow-ups…" in the server HTML and until the saved applications are read, beside "Loading interview dates…", so "All caught up." only ever prints for a pipeline that was actually read. A location pill and the list under it now match on the same terms, so "New York" finds a posting that says NYC and "San Francisco" finds one that says Bay Area. Counts print in the singular for one ("1 matching role", "Stripe · 1 role", "Imported 1 application."), a pipeline card with no department prints its location alone, the empty pipeline no longer repeats that the data stays in this browser because the sentence under the title already says it, the hidden import file input is out of the tab order because the Import button opens it, and a role with no readable date sorts last under Oldest first.

Underneath the page, Mark all seen keeps every seen id that is still in the feed and trims only the ids that have left it, to the newest 500, so the new count clears on a feed of any length. The watched companies start on the default set on the server and on the first client render, and the saved set is restored after mount inside the one initial fetch. Mark applied on a feed card leaves an application that already reached an interview or an offer where it is. An imported date that is not on the calendar, February 30 for one, is dropped. In dev, a candidate that is removed or re-triaged while sourcing rewrites the file has the edit replayed on the server's copy, so a removed candidate stays removed.

None of the corrections on this list were seen in a browser when they were written. The Jest suites for the route, the two hooks, and the applications library passed, and the stylesheet rules were reasoned from the cascade.
