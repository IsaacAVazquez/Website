## Redesign, 2026-10-12 (project-sovereign stage 2, Console archetype)

This is the first brief for the route, written when it took its own design inside the site frame (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-11-project-sovereign-design.md`, G8).

The genre is a job-search tracker, the kind Huntr, Teal, and LinkedIn Jobs are, so the page has an app bar, a search bar that stays in reach, postings as cards in a list, a kanban pipeline with its counts in the column heads, and a pinned to-do rail. It borrows those conventions and none of their branding.

What the page is now. The client root is `.mba-root`, which paints its own work surface with `--mba-*` properties (a cool off-white with white cards in light, a near-black with dark cards in dark) and redeclares the `--c97-*` tokens the borrowed `c97-btn`, `c97-btn-ghost`, `c97-field`, `c97-chip`, and `c97-disclosure` classes read. Those classes are restyled inside the root to sentence case with a 6px radius, and the primary action and the focus ring are the route's teal. Archivo is the face throughout, with Fragment Mono for counts, and no typeface was added. Inside a `c97-shell c97-frame` column there is a title bar with the h1 "Job search", the fused view switch (Role feed, Application pipeline, and Candidates in dev), one orienting sentence, and the app's status (the live-roles count labelled "Live roles tracked", the updated time, Refresh, notifications, and the new-since-last-visit count with Mark all seen). The private sync line prints in the title bar on the pipeline view in dev. When a board fails, the "Partial results" notice prints under the title bar on every view, so the live-roles count carries its context on the pipeline too.

The feed view is a search bar (search, location, sort, Clear filters) that sticks to the top of the feed from 640px, the refinements as labelled rows with pill toggles and no box (location suggestions with the count before location filtering, role type, role family, company category, sources), a results head with "Live roles", the matching count, and the filter tags, and then the postings two to a row from 1000px. A posting is a white card with the company and department, the title as an h3, location and age, one chip rail (new, status, fit, external lead, role type, category, role families), a two-line snippet, the source line, and its actions with Apply now at the right. The Show more step stays twelve, and the line under it says the feed polls every 30 minutes and links to source health. One "Sources" section closes the feed with Tracked feeds, Source health beside Search elsewhere, and Manual checks as a ruled three-column list.

The pipeline view is a toolbar (search applications, status filter, Add application, By fit, JSON backup, CSV, Import), the to-do rail (interviews in the next seven days, then "What to chase today.") pinned on the right from 1100px and first on a phone, the funnel as the strip of heads over the board once anything is tracked, and the status columns as a kanban that scrolls sideways inside the board, 85% of the width each on a phone. The column head names the status and its count, so the card no longer repeats the status chip. The dialog is a plain bordered card over `var(--c97-scrim)`.

Cut, and not to be restored. The poster hero sheet, its plate, and the placeholder funnel ("Track a role below to start filling this in."), the hero's three readouts and its jump link, every kicker, poster headline, and description opener (Filters, Live roles, Tracked companies, Search elsewhere, Manual checks, Source health, Applications, Candidates), the count-chip row under the roles heading, the three count chips on the tracked-feeds toggle, the boxed Popular locations panel, the bordered company-group boxes, the repeated fetched time in the foot line, the bone and paper sheets and their seams, source health inside the pipeline view, the status chip on pipeline cards, and the StatusPanel boxes for empty and error states, which are plain lines now.

Pinned and kept. `live-jobs-grid`, `manual-checks-grid`, `candidates-grid`, `job-card-<id>-chips` with `flex-wrap`, `tracked-companies-<category>`, `mba-toggle` on the company buttons, `#mba-role-refinements` with `hidden md:flex` and its disclosure, `#mba-source-health-heading`, the group "Job tracker view" and its labels, every filter group's aria-label, "Search roles", "Filter by location", "Sort", "Clear filters", "Show more (N of M shown)", "Updated <time>", the server's feed size, "Live roles tracked", the classes on the LinkedIn, Career page, and Apply now links, the funnel text ("Pipeline funnel", "Response rate", "N of M heard back"), the attention text and buttons, "All caught up.", the interviews agenda, the status history, "Copy path", the sync line, "By fit", and the e2e add-and-reload path. Four heading names changed with the redesign, in the same commit, in `mba-jobs-client.test.tsx` ("Live roles", "Tracked feeds", "Search elsewhere", "Source health"), and the funnel test now checks that the funnel stays out of the feed and prints over the pipeline. Nothing from `private/` is in this brief.
