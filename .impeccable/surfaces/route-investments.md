# Investments surface brief

**Scope.** The `/investments` route, rendered by `src/app/investments/investments-client.tsx` into `src/components/investments/InvestmentsDashboard.tsx`, with roughly 30 components under `src/components/investments/` (including the `retirement/` sub-tree) and a 1800-line route-local CSS module.

**Visitor mode.** Operate. Scanability, consistency and density outrank expression. The visitor is working, not being persuaded.

**Job / action.** Track a browser-saved portfolio, research a symbol, and project retirement, without an account and without a login.

**Visual world: "The terminal," and it is NOT the light Working Instrument palette.** This is the one product surface that runs always-dark regardless of the site theme toggle. `.terminalScope` re-declares every `--home-*` token to its `.dark` value on purpose, and that block is documented at the top of `investments.module.css`. Two consequences that have already cost one wasted pass. Any contrast or colour probe must be created *inside* `.terminalScope`, because an element appended to `document.body` resolves the root light values and will never match anything on this surface. And measuring "light mode" then "dark mode" here measures the same always-dark surface twice; an identical text-node count across both runs is the tell. Signal Orange resolves to `#FF6B3B` here, not `#C93F19`, and it is used 24 times. A pass that reports the accent as absent has made this mistake.

**Route constraints.** Financial figures are educational only and the disclaimer plus the assumption disclosure are a compliance constraint, not a style choice. Holdings live in localStorage with no cloud sync, so the empty portfolio is the default state every first-time visitor sees and every surface has to read correctly in it. The dataset chip fails soft and states the convention rather than inventing a timestamp. `StockResearch.tsx` is referenced only by tests and never renders, which is why the route still has exactly one `h1`; do not treat its `<h1>Research</h1>` as a duplicate-heading defect, and decide separately whether to delete it.

**Do not re-litigate (settled 2026-08-05).**
- Fragment Mono carries most of the text on purpose. "Mono numerals throughout" is the stated terminal identity, and mono for figures on a financial dashboard is measurement rather than the costume the craft floor warns about.
- The five display clamps and the 26px retirement gauge numeral sit off the dense type ramp deliberately. They are display type, not steps on the ramp.
- The three `border-radius: 1px` hairline ticks are deliberate and are the `.tickBar`, `.research-asset-logo` and `.invest-retire-legend-band` swatches, none of which is a rounded surface. Corrected 2026-08-06: they are NOT documented anywhere. DESIGN.md's `rounded` scale starts at `sm: 2px` and has no 1px entry, and the module header claims "2px corners everywhere," which contradicts its own three declarations. The decision is defensible but the artifact meant to earn it does not contain it, so either add a 1px hairline step to the scale or reword the header.
- The 3px per-holding colour tick on the quote tape is covered by DESIGN.md, which allows a 3px signal-orange left border on quotes and callouts, though this tick carries per-holding colour rather than signal orange. It trips the `side-tab` detector rule every time. That rule targets a thick coloured border on a card, and this is an inline `span` in a scrolling tape, so the rule's intent does not apply to what it matched.
- The em-relative cents and currency marks (`0.48em`, `0.24em`, `0.55em`) stay proportional to their parent display number and are not ramp steps.
- The type ramp is six named roles on `.terminalScope`: `--invest-fs-micro` 10px, `-label` 11px, `-small` 12px, `-body` 13px, `-lead` 14px, `-value` 17px. New work uses these rather than literal sizes. The ramp's *pitch* was deliberately left alone; re-spacing it for wider separation between steps would change the surface's density character and is a design decision nobody has made yet.

**Fixed 2026-08-05 (adapt). The performance chart deadlocked on shrink and clipped 154px of the hero.** `.invest-chart-wrap` inherited `min-width: auto`, so once the D3 svg had drawn wide it floored the grid track at its own min-content width. The cell then never shrank, its `ResizeObserver` never fired, and the svg never re-measured, leaving the hero clipped behind `overflow: hidden`. It did not reproduce on a fresh load at 390, only on a shrink, meaning phone rotation, a window resize, or devtools opening, which is why a fresh-load check alone would have missed it. `min-width: 0` fixes it, verified by loading at 1440 and shrinking to 390 with the clipping going from 154px to zero. A first hypothesis blaming the bare `1fr` in the 1100px media query recovered only 8px of the 154, so the grid was a secondary contributor at most and the svg was the cause.

**Fixed 2026-08-05 (adapt). The section navigation vanished below 900px with no replacement.** The sidebar is `display: none !important` there, which removed all seven jumps on a route running about nine phone screens. There is a horizontally scrollable rail pinned under the site header now, built from the same `navItems` array so the two cannot drift apart. Exactly one of the two is ever in the accessibility tree, verified at 900 and 901 on either side of the boundary. `--invest-rail-top` is 73px, the header's 72px min-height plus its 1px border, and it is the single place to change if that header ever moves.

**Fixed 2026-08-05 (adapt). Two of the seven nav links pointed at nothing on an empty portfolio.** `#holdings-list` and `#allocation` only render once there is a position, so they were dead in exactly the state a first-time visitor arrives in. `navItems` is filtered now, which fixed the sidebar as well as the new rail.

**Fixed 2026-08-05 (adapt). Eleven controls sat under the repo's 44px floor.** All of them cleared WCAG 2.5.8's 24px, so this was the project standard rather than a WCAG failure. They are raised under `@media (pointer: coarse)` only, so the terminal keeps its density under a mouse, because input method decides this rather than width. All 30 retirement number inputs already measured exactly 44px, which is what marked the other eleven as drift rather than indifference.

**Settled 2026-08-05 (typeset). The `h1` rendered at exactly the `h2` size.** Both clamps shared a 1.2rem floor and a 1.5rem cap, so both pinned to 24px and the outline said one thing while the page said another. The h2 stepped down to a 1.25rem cap rather than the h1 stepping up, because raising the h1 would push the topbar off the 57px fascia line where it aligns with the sidebar brand block. It is 24 over 20 at desktop and 24 over 19.6 at 900 now.

**Settled 2026-08-05 (typeset). 88 font-size declarations across 20 values collapsed onto the six roles.** The problem was not the odd values, it was four steps inside a 1.5px band (10 / 10.5 / 11 / 11.5) that no reader can distinguish, plus a parallel rem ladder restating the same steps at fractional pixels. Type-scoped detector findings went from 74 to 6, and all six remaining are the deliberate display clamps and the gauge numeral.

**Fixed 2026-08-05 (polish). Two sections had no accessible name,** so they were not exposed as landmarks at all despite being sidebar jump targets. `#portfolio-stats` and `#add-holding` carry literal `aria-label`s now, literal rather than `aria-labelledby` because their visible caption is a span rather than a guaranteed heading.

**Critiqued 2026-08-06. 24/40, Acceptable, all ten heuristics applied.** Snapshot at `.impeccable/critique/2026-08-06T03-13-04Z__investments.md`. Cognitive load failed 5 of 8 checks. The deterministic scan contributed nothing, since all ten detector findings were independently verified as false positives and every real finding came from review. Worth knowing before the next run that the site ships an enforced CSP (`script-src 'self' 'unsafe-inline'`), which blocks the live-server overlay injection outright, so `critique` cannot produce a visible overlay here.

**Fixed 2026-08-06 (adapt). Holdings row actions were unreachable on touch, which was the P0.** `.invest-row-actions` was `opacity: 0` revealed only by `:hover` and `:focus-within`, neither of which a touch device has, and the actions sat in the last column of a 695px table inside a 341px wrapper, so they were also 354px of nested horizontal scroll away. Research, edit and remove were all impossible on a phone while the Research empty state told the visitor to "Click Research on any holding above". The coarse-pointer block shows them now, pins the last column with `position: sticky; right: 0` plus an opaque per-state background, and raises the 32px buttons to 44px. Verified at 390 by applying the same declarations, with the actions moving from x=692 and unreachable to a pinned x=338 that holds at both scroll extremes. The `pointer: coarse` query cannot be triggered in a desktop browser, so these rules are verified as parsed in the CSSOM and by applying the identical declarations, not on a real touch device.

**Fixed 2026-08-06 (harden). Cmd-K opened site-wide search instead of the holdings filter.** `StaticHeader.tsx` and `InvestmentsDashboard.tsx` both bound Cmd/Ctrl+K on `window` in the bubble phase, so registration order decided the winner and the header won, which meant the badge printed inside the filter input did the opposite of what it said. The investments listener registers on capture now and stops propagation, since a capture listener on `window` runs before every bubble listener on `window`. Verified live, with the filter taking focus, `defaultPrevented` true, and no dialog opening.

**Fixed 2026-08-06 (clarify). The retirement planner delivered a failing verdict about nobody.** `createDefaultPlan()` produces 41/100, NEEDS ATTENTION, a red shortfall and "money runs out age 79", and the panel holding those inputs was captioned "Your numbers" for a visitor who had entered nothing. `useRetirementPlan` exposes `isSampleScenario` now, which goes false on the first edit and on hydrating a stored plan. The caption reads "Example numbers" until then, and a note sits above the gauge so the framing is read before the score rather than after it.

**The trap inside that fix, and the reason it is recorded here.** The first implementation looked correct and was not. The persist effect wrote the seeded plan to localStorage on first load, so the next visit hydrated a stored plan, concluded the visitor had entered it, and went back to "Your numbers" permanently. The sample framing would have shown on the very first page view and never again, which no single-page-load test can see. The persist effect is gated on `!isSampleScenario` now, so an untouched example is never written. Verified across the whole lifecycle, with the first visit showing the note and writing nothing, and one edit flipping the caption and starting persistence.

**Completed 2026-08-06. The dead-link fix from the 2026-08-05 pass was incomplete.** Filtering `navItems` fixed the sidebar and the mobile rail but missed a second hardcoded link row in `PortfolioStatsGrid.tsx`, where `#allocation` and `#holdings-list` were still dead on an empty portfolio. Both sit behind the same `holdings.length > 0` predicate now. Verified with all 14 in-page anchors on the route resolving and zero dead.

**Verified state (2026-08-05).** Zero contrast failures across 277 text nodes, each resolved against its own enclosing backdrop. All interactive elements carry accessible names. Exactly one `main`, one `h1`, no duplicate ids, no heading-level jumps. Zero console errors. Zero page-level horizontal overflow at 390, 900, 901 and 1440, and the only elements extending past the viewport are the mobile rail's own children, which is what a horizontal scroller does. Typecheck and lint clean, 62 tests passing across 9 suites. Ten detector findings remain on the surface and every one is listed above as deliberate.

**Not done, and deliberately.** No performance trace or LCP measurement was taken, so nothing here claims verified timing. The coarse-pointer sizing was confirmed by reading the parsed CSSOM rule rather than on an emulated touch device. `colorize` was considered and rejected, because the accent finding that prompted it turned out to be a measurement error rather than a defect.

**Commands worth running.** `critique` has never been run here and is the obvious next one. `audit` to find, then only what the findings name, then `polish` last.
