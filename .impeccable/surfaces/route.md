# Home surface brief

**Scope.** The `/` route, rendered by `src/components/catalog97/Catalog97Home.tsx`. One of the seven Catalog 97 surfaces.

**Visitor mode.** Persuade. A professional peer deciding in 20-60 seconds whether Isaac is a credible builder worth remembering.

**Job / action.** See the scale of the work, believe the thinking is sound, and take a next step.

**Route constraints.** The h1 "I build tools that make hard problems easier to act on" is a test anchor in `src/app/__tests__/portfolio-shell.test.tsx` and must not change wording. Carries plates 01 through 05. The three live readouts (quake, market, launch) each fail soft to null upstream, so a missing snapshot drops its column rather than rendering an empty slot; do not "fix" the conditional. Band order paper, pine, chocolate, bone, tobacco, and that order is settled. The hero's internal composition is not settled and changed on 2026-08-03, so read the layout entry at the end of this brief before treating any part of this route's composition as closed.

**Visual world: Catalog 97, not Working Instrument.** `DESIGN.md` describes The Working Instrument and governs the other ~40 routes. It does NOT govern this surface. `context.mjs` auto-loads it anyway, so a pass that treats it as the spec here will read every deliberate Catalog 97 decision as a violation and manufacture a page of false findings. Tokens live in `src/app/catalog97.css`.

**Do not re-litigate (settled 2026-08-02).**
- Tobacco is a large-text-only field. Paper ink on it is 4.36:1, which clears 3:1 for large text and never clears 4.5:1 for body. A tobacco band carries `--c97-fs-h2` (24px floor) and up, or no text at all. Not a defect; solved by size rather than by darkening the colour.
- Anton draws numerals at `--c97-fs-plate` only. Never running text, never headings, and there is no smaller numeral step. This replaced the earlier one-Anton-per-view rule.
- The scales are frozen: 9 type steps, 8 spacing values (`--c97-gutter` plus `--c97-sp-1..7`), 4 line heights, 3 measures plus `--c97-container`. `--c97-touch-y/x` sit outside the spacing ladder on purpose, solved backwards from the 44px floor.
- Every `--c97-*` value is declared under `[data-c97]` or `[data-c97-surface]`, never under a class, so a token consumer can skip class rules wholesale.
- Image slots render as flat Stone or Tobacco fields because no photograph exists yet. That is the design’s own layout rule, not an unfinished asset.

**Verified state.** 0 contrast failures across 2774 text nodes in both themes, each resolved against its own enclosing surface and backdrop. Zero shadows, zero radii above 2px, zero off-palette hex in components. `focus-visible` covers anchors, buttons, inputs, textareas, selects and summaries. Exactly one `aria-current` per route. Espresso footer present. No two Pine bands adjacent, and at least two brown bands per route.

**Commands worth running.** `critique` and `audit` to find. Then only what the findings name. `polish` last. Never `document` here (it would regenerate DESIGN.md against the wrong world), and never both halves of `bolder`/`quieter` or `overdrive`/`distill`.

**Settled 2026-08-02 (critique, degraded single-context).** The anchor reset is `:where(.c97-page) a`, not `.c97-page a`. At `.c97-page a` it scored (0,1,1) and outranked every single-class component rule, so each `.c97-btn` rendered as a `<Link>` discarded its own `color: var(--c97-surface)` and inherited the band ink instead. Measured live: "See the work" was espresso on chocolate at 1.4:1, and `.c97-btn-invert`, whose background is `--c97-ink`, was heading for 1:1 invisible text on six routes. All 13 buttons on the site are anchors, so all 13 were affected. Static token-math audits miss this because they read the declared rule rather than the winning cascade. Do not raise the `:where()` back to a plain class, and re-measure buttons in a browser after any change to the anchor reset.

**Settled 2026-08-02 (mobile pass).** The header wordmark carries `.c97-brand`, which applies the same padding-plus-negative-margin hit target as `.c97-microlink`. At 390px it measured a 23px tap target before this, under the 44px floor the rest of the site holds; it is 55px now. Dark mode is verified live rather than by token math: all eight surfaces render their derived dark values and both themes measure zero contrast failures. When re-measuring after a theme toggle, wait a tick before reading computed styles — reading in the same synchronous block returns the pre-toggle paint and looks like dark mode is broken when it is not.

**Settled 2026-08-03 (layout, degraded single-context). The hero is two columns above roughly 790px and the pitch comes first in the DOM at every width.** This brief previously said the composition was closed to a layout pass, and the pass ran anyway because it was asked for directly. It found one thing worth the change. The hero was a single centered column with the tobacco field sitting between the kicker and the h1, and at 1440x900 that put the h1 at 1207px, so the entire first screen was the plate, one 11px kicker, and 775px of flat tobacco, which is 86% of the viewport, while the headline, the paragraph and both buttons were all below the fold. At 390x844 the h1 started at 775px and only its first line was visible. A route in Persuade mode whose stated job is a peer deciding in 20 to 60 seconds was opening on a blank brown wall. The field is not what was wrong and did not change in kind, because a flat Tobacco field is what the design specifies until a photograph exists. What changed is that the field moved beside the pitch instead of above it, through `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(min(100%, 340px), 1fr))`, which stacks to one column below about 790px with the pitch first. Measured after, at 1440 the h1 runs 388 to 536 and both buttons sit at 779 with all of it inside the first screen, at 390 the h1 runs 329 to 421 and both buttons are in the first screen too, DOM order matches visual order with zero inversions, nothing overflows at either width, and the page went from 6482px to 5366px at 1440. Typecheck clean and 38 tests passing across the seven suites in `src/app/__tests__` and `src/components/__tests__`.

**The hero caption was always meant to be the slot's caption.** `Catalog97Slot` takes a `caption` prop that its own docstring describes as rendering underneath the field in the label step, and the hero was not passing it. The line about one dominant field per view was instead a loose sibling below the h1, where it separated the headline from the paragraph that supports it and read as a third piece of the pitch rather than as a note about the picture. It goes through the prop now. The copy is unchanged, since copy belongs to `clarify` rather than to a layout pass.

**`.c97-columns` went from a 230px track minimum to 200px, and it was a real defect rather than a preference.** The class comment calls it the design's standard three-or-four-up, but four-up was unreachable at 230px, because the shell caps at `--c97-container` (1080px) and `--c97-sp-5` is 68px there, so four 230px tracks need 1124px. Home's live readout row is the case that exposed it, since it renders one to four children depending on which snapshots resolve, and with all three feeds up the fourth dropped onto a row of its own. Four tracks need 1004px at 200px and now fit on one row. The footer, which was `c97-shell c97-columns` in `Catalog97Shell` and carries four children, had the identical orphan on all seven routes and became one row too. **That sentence is superseded.** Later the same day the footer moved off `.c97-columns` entirely and onto its own `.c97-footer-grid`, because four equal tracks set the colophon paragraph at 27 characters a line. The reasoning lives in `.impeccable/surfaces/src-components-catalog97-catalog97shell-tsx.md`, which is the brief for the shared shell. Home's readout row is now the only consumer of `.c97-columns` that the 200px change affected. Nothing with three or fewer children moved, because auto-fit collapses the tracks it cannot fill, and that was verified live rather than assumed. The six consumers account for themselves completely. Both `Catalog97Resume` usages already override `gridTemplateColumns` inline, at 200px and 220px, so the class minimum never reached them. `Catalog97About` renders three principles and `Catalog97Dashboards` renders three data notes, so both were already at their full track count. Home's readout row and the shared footer are the only two that changed, and both were measured.

**Do not "fix" the hairline pair.** The hero brackets its h1 with a 396px rule above and a 448px rule below, and the closing band does the same thing mirrored to the right edge. `.c97-hairline` defaults to `min(100%, 396px)` and the longer one is an inline override. Left-aligned it can read as two rules that failed to match, but it is a motif that appears deliberately in two bands, so it stays.

**Closed 2026-08-03. Band 04 is fixed, and so is the closing band, which the same check turned up.** The paragraph below records the reasoning that was open, and the resolution is that the rubric stays and the lead post moves. "Recent writing" is still the `h2` at `--c97-fs-h2`, because bands 02 and 03 both use `h2` for a rubric and making 04 the only band whose `h2` is an item would break the route against itself. The lead post drops from `c97-display` to `c97-h3`, 52px to 26px, which is the same step bands 02 and 03 give their own items, so every rubric on the route is 32px and every item under one is 26px. The two posts below it drop to `c97-lead` at 22px so the lead keeps its tier. `--c97-fs-h2` was not available for the lead, since it is what the rubric uses and the child would have landed exactly on its parent. Nothing sits between 32px and 52px, because the scale is frozen at 9 steps and `--c97-fs-display`, the only value in that gap at 74px, has no consumer anywhere in the codebase, so introducing one would be a typographic decision for the whole visual world rather than a fix for one band. The same sweep found the closing statement on the tobacco band was an `h2` carrying `c97-display`, rendering at 52px and tying the `h1` in exactly the way the lead post did. It is a `p` now, at the same 52px, which is what the closing line already is on every other Catalog 97 route. That drops the band as a landmark, which matches the CTA bands on `/dashboards`, `/writing` and `/portfolio`, none of which carry a heading either. Verified live with one `h1` at 52px, three `h2` all at 32px, six `h3` at 26px and 22px, nothing at or above the `h1` other than the `h1` itself, and no child at or above its parent, with the mobile figures 32px, 24px, 20px and 19px.

**The reasoning that was open, kept for the record.** Band 04 inverted its heading sizes. "Recent writing" is an `h2` at `--c97-fs-h2`, 32px, and the lead post below it is an `h3` carrying `.c97-display`, which resolves to `--c97-fs-h1`, 52px, so the child renders 20px larger than its parent and ties the page `h1` exactly. Bands 02 and 03 both put 26px `h3` children under their 32px `h2`, so band 04 is the only one that inverts. It is left alone because the frozen scale has no step between 32px and 52px, so every fix available trades one defect for another. Demoting the lead to `c97-h2` ties it to its own parent, which is the same trap one step over. Demoting it to `c97-h3` erases the featured treatment the band is built around. Turning the rubric into a label and promoting the lead to `h2` reads correctly and would suit the section being conditional, which is a case the machine-level notes say wants a literal `aria-label`, but it would make band 04 the only band whose `h2` is an item rather than a rubric. Choosing among those is a decision about how a featured item relates to its section rubric across the whole Catalog 97 world, not a one-route layout fix.
