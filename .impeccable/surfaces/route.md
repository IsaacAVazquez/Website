**Do not re-litigate (settled 2026-08-02).**

- Anton draws numerals at `--c97-fs-plate` only. Never running text, never headings, and there is no smaller numeral step. This replaced the earlier one-Anton-per-view rule.
- The scales are frozen: 9 type steps, 8 spacing values (`--c97-gutter` plus `--c97-sp-1..7`), 4 line heights, 3 measures plus `--c97-container`. `--c97-touch-y/x` sit outside the spacing ladder on purpose, solved backwards from the 44px floor.
- Every `--c97-*` value is declared under `[data-c97]` or `[data-c97-surface]`, never under a class, so a token consumer can skip class rules wholesale.
- Image slots without a photograph render as flat Stone or Tobacco fields, which is the design’s own layout rule for a missing photograph. Corrected 2026-09-14, the headshot at `/images/headshot-home.webp` fills the portrait slot on / and /about, so those two slots show a photograph.

**The reasoning that was open, kept for the record.** Band 04 inverted its heading sizes. "Recent writing" is an `h2` at `--c97-fs-h2`, 32px, and the lead post below it is an `h3` carrying `.c97-display`, which resolves to `--c97-fs-h1`, 52px, so the child renders 20px larger than its parent and ties the page `h1` exactly. Bands 02 and 03 both put 26px `h3` children under their 32px `h2`, so band 04 is the only one that inverts. It is left alone because the frozen scale has no step between 32px and 52px, so every fix available trades one defect for another. Demoting the lead to `c97-h2` ties it to its own parent, which is the same trap one step over. Demoting it to `c97-h3` erases the featured treatment the band is built around. Turning the rubric into a label and promoting the lead to `h2` reads correctly and would suit the section being conditional, which is a case the machine-level notes say wants a literal `aria-label`, but it would make band 04 the only band whose `h2` is an item rather than a rubric. Choosing among those is a decision about how a featured item relates to its section rubric across the whole Catalog 97 world, not a one-route layout fix.
