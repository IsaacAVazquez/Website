## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment. The room is authored. The fascia is a real mono readout row, pick, clock, next turn, pool, with one accent tile for "You" on the clock; the tier plates carry the same oversized ordinal and cliff separators as the rankings board so the two surfaces read as one instrument; the strip's three cards ask the three draft questions in order (who is best, who is at risk, what does waiting cost) and answer them in plain sentences; the pick tape doubles as random-access undo; the drawer now closes its own dead end with "Log this pick". The season note is honest and routes to the weekly board. Where it goes generic or loses its own thread: the accent is spent on a whole column of VORP values, so the one lit indicator becomes forty lit indicators and "You" no longer stands out from a number; the "Why these picks" disclosure does not expand the cards, it renders a second set of them; and the header stack (kicker, chips, warning-tinted note, fascia, tape, roster, strip) has grown to the point that on a phone the recommended pick sits a full viewport below the fold while a third of that viewport is pinned.

Deterministic and mechanical evidence (round-1 sweep, parser gate reproduced 16.29 light and 15.28 dark on all 24 cells). Zero horizontal overflow at 390/768/1024/1440 in every state. Exactly one `main` and one visible `h1` in every state. Zero unnamed `section` landmarks (down from five to seven in the running state on 2026-08-23). 48 tab stops on the setup state at 1440, every one with a visible ring (the one no-ring entry is `nextjs-portal`). Zero sub-44px targets across 157 to 165 checked interactive elements in the running states (the one "under 44" candidate, the note's "weekly board" link, is correctly exempted as an inline link in prose). Zero console errors, zero failed requests. Heading ramps descend in every state (34 to 26 at 1440, 24 to 20.5 at 390); the only inversion is the shared Contact CTA h2 at 44px under the 34px h1, inherited shell, not filed. Six live regions in the running state, two in setup. Contrast: 511 to 649 text elements measured per running cell; dark theme zero failures everywhere; light theme one failure in `runningAfterPick` at 768, 1024 and 1440 and in `running` at 768, the VORP value ("205" or "290", 13.1 to 14px regular, `--home-signal` rgb(201,63,25)) on rgb(242,241,235) at 4.41:1, selector `ul > li > div > span`. Reproduced from the tokens in source below: that background is not paper-alt itself but the row's hover wash, and the same value fails harder on the keyboard-highlighted row.

Where the evidence caught what the review missed: the exact surface under the failing "205" (the hover mix, not a resting background), and that the same treatment fails on the keyboard highlight at 4.01:1 where no sweep pointer ever rests. ## Overall Impression

The room does the hard things right, and it has done them for a while: the fascia is honest, the undo model is complete, the recommendations explain themselves in sentences, and the accessibility plumbing measures clean in every state the sweep can reach. What holds it at 29 is that the operating loop has four small breaks in it, and they all sit on the path a person takes under a 90-second clock. The advertised shortcut opens the wrong search, a keyboard pick strands focus at the top of the document, the phone fascia clips the pick number, and the disclosure that should explain the strip duplicates it instead. None of these needs a redesign, and three of them are one-file fixes.

## What's Working

1. The fascia is a genuine mono readout row and the sr-only status line beside it is the right shape for a live region. The clock tile is `role="timer"` with `aria-live="off"` and the announcement text changes only on a pick or undo (`draft-tracker-client.tsx:1041-1054`), so a screen reader hears "Pick 2 of 180, round 1. Slot 2 is on the clock. Last pick: Ja'Marr Chase." once per pick and never the countdown. Measured six live regions in the running state, all deliberate.

2. The undo model is complete and non-destructive at every layer. Undo and Redo name their disabled reason in the accessible name ("Undo last pick (no picks yet)"), the pick tape is random-access undo ("Undo back to pick 23 (Kenneth Walker III)", `:1131-1152`), U works from anywhere outside a field or the drawer (`:844-863`), New room parks the room behind "Back to room →", and Start over a parked room asks first with copy that says exactly what is lost ("Starting clears the 24 picks already logged in the parked room, and undo cannot bring them back."). Verified through a reload, the room came back at pick 25.

3. The season state is stated plainly and routed. The note says "Week 1 of the 2026 season", says the board is frozen and why, and links to the weekly board; the strip, the drawer and the outlook all keep their dated framing (source stamp, "2025 season · 17 games" on the PPG panel, "710 mock selections · 0.6 pick standard deviation" on the ADP card). Nobody arriving today is invited to draft as if it were August. The drawer also closed the 2026-08-23 dead end: "Log this pick" is a 183x44 button that logs and closes, so a player can be judged and taken without leaving the panel.

## Priority Issues

1. [P1] The documented "/" shortcut opens the site search, not the board search. The board hint reads "Press slash to focus search from anywhere" (`DraftBoard.tsx:582`) and its handler is a `document` keydown listener that focuses the input and calls `preventDefault` (`DraftBoard.tsx:158-189`). The site header registers its own "/" shortcut on `window` (`src/components/StaticHeader.tsx:80-90`, added 2026-09-09) and checks only for a typing target, never `event.defaultPrevented`, so it fires after the board's handler and opens the global overlay on top. Measured at 1440: after "/" the active element is the header's "Search the site" input, typing "Nacua" produced "Searching…" in the overlay, and the board count stayed at "559 of 559 available". Why it matters: this is the only keyboard path the room advertises, on a surface whose brief says the clock is the whole context, and it now does the one thing a shortcut must never do, which is take the user somewhere else. Fix: add `!event.defaultPrevented` to the header's guard so a surface that owns "/" wins, and consider having the board handler run on `window` in the capture phase so ordering is not a coincidence. Suggested pass: harden.

2. [P1] Logging a pick from the keyboard or the drawer drops focus to `body`. Focusing "Log Ja'Marr Chase" and pressing Enter logged the pick and left `document.activeElement` on BODY at both 1440 and 390; "Log this pick" in the drawer did the same (dialog closed, focus on BODY, status line updated). The row button unmounts with the pick, and the only focus management in the tracker is the search refocus that runs when a query was active (`DraftBoard.tsx:108-118`); `logPick` in the client (`draft-tracker-client.tsx:665`) moves focus nowhere. The mock draft solved this on 2026-08-23 by moving focus to the on-clock tile (`mock-draft-client.tsx:555-557`) and the tracker never received the port. Why it matters: a keyboard user starts the next pick from the top of a 4,600px document, behind the site header, the chips, the fascia, the tape, the roster and the strip, every time, on a 90-second clock. Fix: after a pick, move focus to the fascia's "On the clock" tile or to the new first row's name button, and restore to the drawer's opener when the drawer logs. Suggested pass: harden.

3. [P1] "Why these picks" renders a second copy of the recommendations rather than expanding them. The strip already shows Best available and Most at risk with a round "Log" each (`draft-tracker-client.tsx:1250-1340`); the disclosure then mounts `decisionRecs` as a second card row with "Best left", "Most at risk" and "Fills …" and a pill "Log pick" each (`:1377-1427`), so the same player, rank, tier, VORP and index print twice within 180px. Measured at pick 24 with the disclosure open: five Log buttons for three players above the board (Josh Allen at y=567 and y=746, Omarion Hampton at 567 and 764, Ashton Jeanty at 764) at 1440, the same five at 390 stacked from y=940 to y=1619. Why it matters: the room's tightest moment gets its noisiest screen, and the two button treatments (circle "LOG" and pill "LOG PICK") for one action inside one panel read as two different things. Fix: let the disclosure add the missing information to the existing cards (the "why" sentence and the third "Fills" card) and keep one Log per player; the four-position panel underneath already carries the long-form reasoning. Suggested pass: distill.

4. [P1] At 390 the sticky fascia is 192px tall and clips its own numbers. The section is `sticky top-[4.5rem]` (`:1033`) and its grid is `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(min(148px, 30vw), 1fr))` (`:1059`), so at 390 it wraps into two rows of 117px tiles and measures 192px, which with the 73px site header pins 264px of an 844px viewport (31%) at every scroll position. Both value and sub lines are `overflow-hidden text-ellipsis whitespace-nowrap` (`:1083`, `:1089`), and at 117px minus padding they clip: "#24 / 1…", "Prev · J. Chas…", "advisory · 90s…", "in 22 picks · …" all measured in the driven state. The first board row sits at y=1423 on a 7,091px page and the first Log on the user's own turn at y≈940, so the phone user scrolls past a screen and a half while a third of every screen is fascia. Why it matters: the one number the fascia exists to show, the pick counter, is the one that truncates once it reaches two digits, and the sub-lines that make "Slot 2" and "#24" readable are gone. Fix: below `sm`, collapse to a one-row fascia (pick, on the clock, next turn, with the clock riding in the pick tile) and move Undo and New room into the row's trailing slot; drop "Prev ·" and "advisory ·" prefixes at that width instead of ellipsizing them. Suggested pass: adapt.

5. [P1] Signal orange is spent on every VORP value, and that is where the light-mode AA failure lives. `DraftBoard.tsx:443-457` colors the value `var(--home-signal)` whenever `vorp.value > 0`, which is every rostered player, at `text-xs` (13.1 to 14px measured). On plain paper that is 4.57:1, passing by 0.07. The row's hover wash `color-mix(paper-alt 55%, transparent)` (`:398`) composites to rgb(242,241,235) and the sweep measured the value there at 4.41:1 (768, 1024, 1440, light); the keyboard-highlight background `color-mix(signal 10%, paper-raised)` (`:403-405`) computes to 4.01:1, so the row a keyboard user is about to log fails hardest; on real paper-alt it is 4.26:1. Dark clears everywhere (6.5, 6.28, 5.83). The one sibling that shares the treatment is the "Index NN.N" value in `RedraftDecisionPanel.tsx:99-104`, signal at `text-xs`, which sits on the most-at-risk card's `color-mix(warning 8%, paper)` wash (`:63-65`) at a computed 4.22:1. Why it matters: forty accent numerals per screen leave nothing for "You" and "Now" to be the accent against, and the failing cases are the rows and cards the user is actually on. Fix: set VORP in ink with tabular numerals like ADP beside it (it is a column, not a state), keep the accent for the highlighted rail and the queue star, and give the Index value the 72%-toward-ink mix the fascia already uses on tinted grounds. Suggested pass: quieter.

6. [P2] The seasonal note is rendered outside the shell. Every other block in the client sits in `SHELL_CLASS`; the note at `draft-tracker-client.tsx:951-955` does not, so the rounded, bordered plate measures left 0 to right 1440 at 1440 (h1 at x=434, setup card at 350 to 1090) and 0 to 390 on the phone with its border on the screen edge and no gutter. The rankings board (`fantasy-football-client.tsx:1620`) and the best ball tracker (`best-ball/draft-tracker/draft-tracker-client.tsx:168-171`) both wrap it. Fix: wrap it in the shell like its siblings. Suggested pass: layout.

7. [P2] The first screen states the pick number three times and the pool twice. Kicker "Draft assistant · Live · Pick #24" (`:632`), fascia "#24 / 180", and the strip's "Pick #24 of 180 · 1:30 advisory · your next turn #25" (`:1227-1231`) which repeats the fascia's pick, clock and next-turn tiles 40px below them; "559 left / of 559 ranked" in the fascia and "559 of 559 available" in the board controls. The best ball room stopped stating its four numbers three times on 2026-08-23. Fix: the strip header keeps "Your pick is live" and the disclosure toggle and drops the numbers; the kicker can drop the pick number once the fascia carries it. Suggested pass: distill.

8. [P2] "−0.0 pts". `waitCellReading` (`draft-tracker-client.tsx:118-122`) prints `−${pointsDrop.toFixed(1)} pts` when the rounded drop is under one point, so a drop of 0.04 renders "−0.0 pts" (measured at pick 24, back-to-back turn, "to #25 · J. Allen likely lasts"). The comment says a drop that rounds to zero must not read as free, and a negative zero reads as both free and broken. Fix: below 0.05 print "under 1 pt" or "about 0 pts". Suggested pass: polish.

9. [P2] The drawer's Movement line has no visible label. `PlayerDetailDrawer.tsx:331-335` renders `MetricTooltip` with no children, which is the bare "?" button (`MetricTooltip.tsx:257`, aria-label "What is Movement?"), then a literal `": "`, so the line reads "?: rank ±0 in 7d · ±0 in 14d · ADP ↑0.1 in 7d". Every other stat in the drawer leads with its word. Fix: print "Movement" before the trigger like "Points per game" and "Market ADP" do, and drop the colon. Suggested pass: polish.

## Persona Red Flags

Alex (impatient power user, desktop, mid-draft). He reads the fascia, takes the strip's pick, and is on the next turn in under ten seconds when he uses the mouse. The moment he goes to the keyboard the room fights him: "/" opens the site search (issue 1), a picked row strands him on `body` with the whole header stack between him and the next row (issue 2), and U is the only shortcut that works. He would keep the tab open for the fascia and the tape and do his queueing by mouse.

Sam (screen reader, keyboard only). The document is well built: one main, one h1, a named region for every landmark, a status line that announces each pick once, a dialog with a working trap and Esc restore, every disabled control naming its reason. The loop breaks in the same two places as Alex's, and for Sam they are worse, because after every Log the reading position resets to the top of the page and the pick tape and roster are read again before the board. The drawer's Movement line announces "What is Movement?" then ": rank plus or minus 0 in 7 d", which is a label with no subject.

Casey (distracted, phone, one thumb, on the clock). Returning to the tab on her turn, she sees 73px of site header and 192px of fascia with the pick number cut to "#24 / 1…", then scrolls past the tape and roster to find the strip about a screen down (first Log at y≈940 of an 844px viewport). The row micro-labels, the 44px Log pills, the "Back to room" flow and the persistence through reload all hold up. The seasonal note runs edge to edge with no gutter, which is cosmetic, and the 31% pinned band is not.

## Minor Observations

- The h1 uses `clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 2.125rem)` (`draft-tracker-client.tsx:924`), the same off-ramp clamp the rankings board moved to the DESIGN.md title ramp on 2026-08-22; measured 34px at 1440 and 24px at 390 against 33.6 and 24.8 on the ramp. Instrument Serif appears exactly once (the italic "Tracker"), which is correct.
- "Source updated Sep 10, 2026, 12:19 AM" comes from `formatUpdatedAt` (`src/lib/fantasyUtils.ts:19-28`), an `Intl.DateTimeFormat` with `timeStyle: "short"` and no `timeZone`, so the clock time is whatever zone rendered it and carries no label. The rankings board's date-only stamp took `timeZone: "UTC"` on 2026-08-23 for exactly this reason. Either name the zone or drop the time of day.
- "ADP current" sits beside a note that says the board stops refreshing once the season is under way. The chip is technically right until the bands widen on 2026-10-01, but "current" and "stopped refreshing" on the same line read as a contradiction. Not a finding, a wording to revisit when the band widens.
- "2026 Week 1" chip and "Week 1 of the 2026 season." say the same thing 40px apart.
- Two voice items in rendered copy: "here for next summer rather than for this week" (`:953`) is the "X rather than Y" reversal, and "not just yours" (`:1009-1010`) is a "not just X" pivot. Both are protected framing in substance and can be reworded without weakening ("so it is here for next summer. This week's ranks are on the weekly board.").
- The "Rostered 99.9%" stat cell in the drawer (`PlayerDetailDrawer.tsx:318`) has no definition trigger, unlike its three neighbors, and in a preseason draft drawer it is not obvious what it is rostered in.
- The "At #1" column label is the only header in the room that changes with state, and its sr text ("versus ADP at pick 1") is clearer than its visible text.
- QB shows a bare "1" in the lineup grid where every other slot is a select (`DraftSetup.tsx:528-532`); the comment explains one-QB only, the screen does not.
- The 24-pick tape at 1440 is an `overflow-x-auto` row (`:1126`) that clips a chip mid-word at the shell edge with no scroll affordance beyond the cut.
- The strip's "Best available" (`:1254`) and every `rec.tag` (`:1394`) label take signal at 10 to 11px on paper (4.57:1); labels are not data or state and this is the second place the accent leaks after the VORP column.
- Body scroll is not locked behind the open drawer (measured `overflow: visible` on body at both widths); the rankings board locks it since 2026-08-22.
- The site footer's "Now" link and the 44px Contact CTA h2 are shared shell, inherited, not filed.
- Still open and deliberately deferred as product work: Cmd+Z, next/previous in the drawer, and the below-sm h1/h2 inversion decision.

## Questions to Consider

1. The strip answers three questions and the disclosure answers the same three again with more words. If the disclosure only ever added the fourth card and the "why" sentence to cards that already exist, does the room still need two card grammars and two Log treatments?
2. The fascia was designed as a desktop readout row and the phone gets the same six tiles wrapped. What does a one-line phone fascia look like if the clock lives inside the pick tile and Undo becomes a single glyph, and does the 31% pinned band become 12%?
3. VORP is a column of measured values, which is the "mono readout" the design system reserves ink and tabular numerals for. What is the accent actually marking on this board once the column gives it back, and is that the highlighted rail, the queue star, and "You", and nothing else?
4. The header stack now carries kicker, chips, note, fascia, tape, roster and strip before the first row. Which of those is the phone user's, and which are the search arrival's, and should the running state shed the second group the way it already sheds the pitch?
