Scope note: this is the route's first critique. ## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment.** The page is authored, and it is honest in a way waiver-wire pages never are. The hero says in one sentence what the list is and when I would use it, the stamp line is a real mono readout with an absolute date, and the method paragraph prints the actual denominators (457 flex, 100 quarterbacks) and thresholds it is applying, then refuses to invent a bid because the source carries none. The table itself is a proper Working Instrument ledger, hairline rows, tabular mono numerals, the Gap column bold in ink and the supporting numbers muted, with the Board column keeping the two rank spaces visible per row. Signal orange is spent only on the active scoring pill, the inline link underline and focus rings, and there is no Instrument Serif at all, which is within the one-gesture budget. Where it goes generic is the shell around the table. The card's h2 repeats the h1, the card's first sentence repeats the hero's, and the sibling strip and Contact CTA are shared furniture. Where it misses its own character is on the phone, because the instrument's one reading, the gap, is the column that falls off the right edge.

**Deterministic scan.** Round1 sweep, both states (default, halfPpr), both themes, four widths, sixteen cells. Parser sanity gate reproduced at 16.29 light and 15.28 dark on every cell. Contrast 0 failures across 159 to 181 measured text elements per cell, floor 5.35 light (the 16.35px hero paragraph in ink-muted) and 5.99 dark (a 14.26px rostered percentage). Zero horizontal document overflow at any width, because the table's overflow lives inside its own `overflow-x-auto` wrapper, which is exactly the problem in issue 1. Zero sub-44px targets across 22 to 29 checked; the scoring pills measure 44px tall; the one exempt entry is the "weekly board" link inline in 247ch prose. One main, one visible h1, zero unnamed sections, no heading skips. Zero live regions on every cell. 31 tab stops at 1440 with one no-ring entry, the dev-only `nextjs-portal`. Zero console errors and zero failed requests on every cell. My own browser pass added one console warning the sweep does not collect (it filters to errors), the Chrome preload notice quoted in issue 6, and confirmed it on the network, two requests for `weekly.json`, one `other` (the preload) and one `fetch`.

**Where the mechanical evidence caught what the design review missed.** The review saw the phone table cut off; the measurement put numbers on it (Player 274px, wrapper 316px, Rank begins at x=369 against a 353px clip, Gap at x=604 to 645). The review read "157 experts · Current" as the page's stamp; the snapshot shows the quarterback board was updated 24 minutes earlier by 142 experts and the source never reaches the screen. **False positives set aside:** the 1x1 skip link, the dev-tools badge, the Chrome-only behavior that makes the overflow div a tab stop (real in Chromium, absent in Firefox and Safari, which is why issue 1 asks for an explicit one), and the "h1 63.2 vs h2 44" line in SUMMARY, which is the shared Contact CTA h2 (inherited, not fantasy code); the waivers-owned h2 sits at 22px.

## Overall Impression

This is the right page to have split off, and the copy is doing the hard work correctly. Everything the reader needs to reproduce a row is on screen, the thresholds are numbers rather than adjectives, and there is no invented bid or projection anywhere. What holds it at 30 is that the table was shipped as a desktop artifact. On a phone, which is where a Tuesday-night waiver check actually happens, the page shows nineteen names and the word Flex, and the gap the page is named for is one undiscoverable swipe away. Under that, the freshness stamp quietly describes only half the rows, and a few things the sibling rankings view already fixed (pinned column labels, a scroll box) did not make it across in the same file. None of it is a redesign; it is a layout pass, a small honesty fix to the stamp, and a distill of the duplicated header.

## What's Working

1. **The method is printed, not summarized.** `weekly-client.tsx:358-378` interpolates `activeBoard.flex.length`, `FANTASY_WEEKLY_STARTABLE_DEPTH`, `FANTASY_WEEKLY_WIDELY_ROSTERED_PERCENT` and `FANTASY_WEEKLY_MIN_WAIVER_GAP` into the sentence, so the denominators change to 468 when Half PPR is selected and any row can be checked by hand (Michael Mayer, rank 106 of 457, (457 minus 106 plus 1) over 457 is 77.0, minus 10.4 rostered is 66.6, which is what the row prints). The closing sentence, "There is no bid figure here, because the source carries none and I am not inventing one," is the credibility feature said plainly.
2. **The two rank spaces stay visible per row.** The Board column (`:434-436`) carries Flex or QB on every row, so a rank of 24 sits next to its space and a reader cannot mistake it for a flex rank, which is the brief's one non-negotiable for this snapshot.
3. **Orientation for a search arrival is complete.** Title "Fantasy Football Waiver Targets", breadcrumb Fantasy Football > Waivers with the current crumb marked, an h1 that names the page, one orienting sentence with an inline link to the weekly board, a dated stamp, and a sibling strip at the bottom. The page stands on its own without borrowing weekly's h1, and the URL carries scoring so a shared link restores the view.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] On a phone the table shows names and nothing else.** Measured at 390 light: the wrapper is 316px wide, the table 608px (`min-w-[38rem]`, `weekly-client.tsx:386`), and the Player row header alone is 274px because name, position, team and opponent share one cell (`:419-433`). Column x-ranges are Player 37 to 311, Board 311 to 369, Rank 369 to 418, Percentile 418 to 520, Rostered 520 to 604, Gap 604 to 645, against a viewport clip at 353. So Rank, Percentile, Rostered and Gap are all off-screen, the wrapper carries no scroll hint (background-image, box-shadow and mask are all none), no sticky first column, no `tabindex`, `role` or `aria-label` (`:385`), and the screenshots at 390 in both themes show nineteen rows of "name, Flex". Why this matters is that this page exists for the Gap number, the phone is where a waiver run happens, and the same brief that produced this route says scanability outranks expression. The fix is a layout pass on the table below md, either make the Player cell shrink (wrap the meta onto a second line and drop the 38rem floor, keeping Gap in the first 316px), or make the row header sticky-left with a hairline edge shadow and give the scroller `tabindex="0"` plus a label so Firefox and Safari keyboards can reach it too. Suggested pass, adapt.

2. **[P1] The freshness stamp describes only the flex board, and the table is one third quarterbacks.** `page.tsx:40` fixes `board: "flex"` for this view, and `weekly-client.tsx:139-148` derives `source` and `staleness` from that board, so the header line (`:216-234`) prints the flex source's `asOf` and `expertCount`. This week that reads "Source updated Sep 10, 2026, 7:26 PM · Current · 157 experts" while the snapshot's quarterback source is `asOf 2026-09-10T19:02:46Z` with 142 experts, and six of the nineteen rows (Bryce Young, Malik Willis, Daniel Jones, Sam Darnold, Tyler Shough, Jordan Love) come from that board. If the quarterback board ever went stale on its own, the stamp would still say Current. Why this matters is that as-of stamps are the protected honesty framing for the whole fantasy group, and this one is silently half true. The fix is small, compute staleness over both sources for the waivers view (worst of the two) and print both stamps, "Flex updated 7:26 PM, 157 experts · QB updated 7:02 PM, 142 experts", or one stamp with the earlier time and a "both boards" qualifier. Suggested pass, clarify.

3. **[P2] A scoring tap changes the list silently and nothing on screen counts it.** The only live region is the loading sentence (`role="status"`, `:239`), gone once data lands; the sweep reads zero live regions on all sixteen cells. Tapping Half PPR takes the table from 19 rows to 17 and Standard reorders the top (Bryce Young leads, Michael Mayer drops out), with only `aria-pressed` flipping (`:305`). A screen reader hears "Half PPR, pressed" and nothing about the table; a sighted user has no "17 targets" anywhere and must count. The rankings board settled this with an aria-live "n of m shown". The fix is one visually present count line above the table ("17 players clear the gap in Half PPR") inside a polite live region. Suggested pass, harden.

4. **[P2] The sibling's sticky header and scroll box did not cross over.** `STICKY_HEADER_CLASS` and its style are defined at `:56-60` and applied to every rankings `th` (`:553-587`) inside a `max-h-[70vh] overflow-auto` box (`:544`); the waivers `thead` (`:391-412`) uses neither, and my mid-scroll shot at 1440 shows rows passing under the fixed site header with the column labels gone. Nineteen rows at 37px is about 700px, so on a 900px viewport the labels leave before the last rows arrive. It is milder than the 150-row case because the list is capped at 20, but it is the same file making two choices. The fix is to reuse the same class on the six headers and, if the horizontal scroller from issue 1 stays, the same scroll box. Suggested pass, layout.

5. **[P2] The list is capped at 20 and the copy says everyone who clears the gap is listed.** `getFantasyWeeklyWaiverCandidates(board, limit = 20)` (`fantasyWeeklySnapshot.ts:219-221`) slices to 20 (`:250`) and `weekly-client.tsx:145` calls it with the default. The paragraph (`:374-375`) says a candidate has to "clear a gap of at least 20 to be listed at all", which reads as sufficient. The cap does not bite this week (uncapped counts are 19, 17 and 18 against the same shown rows), but PPR is one candidate from truncating with no note. The fix is either to print the cap in the same sentence ("the twenty widest gaps of those that clear 20") or to drop the cap and let the threshold be the only gate. Suggested pass, clarify.

6. **[P2] The snapshot preload is not reused, so the JSON is fetched twice.** `page.tsx:44-46` emits `<link rel="preload" href="/data/fantasy/weekly.json?v=…" as="fetch">` with no `crossorigin`, and the hook fetches the same URL with `fetch()` defaults (`useFantasyWeeklySnapshot.ts:34-37`). Chrome logs "A preload for '…weekly.json…' is found, but is not used because the request credentials mode does not match. Consider taking a look at crossorigin attribute," and the network shows two requests for the file (one `other`, one `fetch`). The 08-23 optimize pass on weekly, and the comment at `weekly/page.tsx:41-44` claiming the in-flight preload is reused, are both contradicted by this. The fix is `crossOrigin: "anonymous"` on the preload (or a matching credentials mode on the fetch) in both page files. Suggested pass, optimize.

7. **[P2] The title and the lead sentence are each printed twice.** h1 "Waiver Targets" (`VIEWS.waivers.title`, `:43`) is followed 300px later by h2 "Waiver targets" (`:352-357`) in a different case, and the card paragraph opens "Players the experts rank ahead of where the rostering rate puts them" (`:359-360`) which is the hero's first clause (`:204-205`) verbatim. Between that h2 and the first row sit about 120 words of method, nine lines at 1440 and twenty at 390 (roughly 430px of phone before row one). The method is protected framing and should keep every number; the duplication around it is not. The fix is a distill that retitles the card to what the h1 does not say ("This week's list", or the count from issue 3), cuts the repeated first sentence, and lets the method paragraph start at "The gap is". Suggested pass, distill.

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (impatient power user, desktop Tuesday night):** finds the list in one scroll and reads it top to bottom in about ten seconds, which is the page working. Then he wants to see only the flex adds because his quarterback is set, and there is no cut; he wants to sort by Rostered to find the ones still on his wire, and there is no sort; he wants to click Michael Mayer to see the expert range on the weekly board, and rows are inert. He also cannot tell whether Malik Willis's 83.0 percentile is "as fresh" as the flex rows (issue 2). Search and a position filter are recorded as deferred product work on the weekly brief, so I note them and do not file them.

**Sam (screen reader / keyboard-only):** the flow is genuinely completable. The table has an sr-only caption that states the sort ("ranked by the gap between board percentile and rostered percentage"), six `scope="col"` headers, a `scope="row"` header per row that reads as "Michael Mayer TE LV vs. MIA", and every focusable control carries the 2px signal ring (31 of 31 stops, dev portal aside). What breaks is around the table rather than inside it. Changing scoring announces only the pressed state (issue 3), the stamp Sam hears applies to thirteen of nineteen rows (issue 2), and in Firefox or Safari at phone width the horizontal scroller is unreachable by keyboard because it has no `tabindex` (issue 1). Contrast passes everywhere in both themes.

**Casey (distracted phone search-arrival, one thumb, on the clock):** arrives to a clear title, a one-sentence explanation and a dated stamp, which is good, then scrolls through about 430px of method to reach a table that shows names and the word Flex. The gap, the rostered rate and the rank are all off the right edge with no hint to swipe (issue 1). Casey either reads the list as "these nineteen players, in some order" or leaves. The stamp also wraps to three lines at 390 with a trailing "·" orphaned at the end of each line, which is cosmetic but reads as broken on the first screen.

## Minor Observations

- The h1 uses `clamp(2.25rem,1.7rem+2.5vw,4.2rem)` (`weekly-client.tsx:183`), off the DESIGN.md headline ramp `clamp(2.15rem,1.6rem+2.75vw,4.2rem)`; measured 63.2px at 1440 where the ramp would give 65.2, 37px at 390 where the ramp gives 36.4. Shared with weekly; note once.
- The mono stamp separators are `aria-hidden` spans in a `flex-wrap` row with `gap-x-3` (`:217-233`), so at 390 the line breaks after a "·". Wrapping each "label · " pair together, or using a `::before` separator that hides at line start, fixes the orphans.
- `sort((a, b) => b.gap - a.gap)` (`fantasyWeeklySnapshot.ts:250`) interleaves flex and quarterback rows in one order. The module comment (`:9-14`) says merging the spaces "would manufacture a cross-position ordering the source never published," and the on-screen paragraph says the same percentile means something different in each space, then the table orders both by a difference of those percentiles. It is defensible (the gap is a within-space reading and the order is only a presentation), but the page never says the list is one ordering across two spaces. One clause would close it, or two grouped blocks would sidestep it.
- The "Percentile" header carries no unit and the column prints 77.0 beside a 10.4% Rostered cell, which reads as two different kinds of number even though they subtract. Printing the percentile with the same % sign, or the gap as "66.6 pts", would make the arithmetic visible in the row.
- "157 experts" in the stamp is the flex expert count with no qualifier (issue 2 covers the stamp; this is the label).
- The "More fantasy tools" strip sends an in-season visitor to Rankings board, Draft tracker and Best ball with no note that those are frozen draft surfaces; the weekly hero says "Every other board here is a draft board" and the waivers hero does not. A four-word qualifier in the strip, or reusing that sentence, would keep the in-season routing honest on this route too.
- The "weekly board" link underline is signal orange while the sibling-strip links are plain ink with an arrow glyph; two link treatments on one short page.
- Card h2 at 22px, hero paragraph at 18px, method paragraph at 14px, table cells at 16px, headers at 10px mono; the 10px `text-3xs` column labels are the site's floor and they are the labels that carry the table. Fine on the ramp, worth a second look once the header is sticky.
- `page.test.tsx` asserts the props and the preload only; nothing tests that the waivers view renders a table, the empty sentence, or the stamp, which is where issues 2 and 5 would have been caught.
- The empty state ("No player clears the gap this week…", `:379-383`) and the not-published note never rendered in any state this week; both read correctly in source.
- Dark mode is at parity in every screenshot, and the dark contrast floor (5.99) is higher than light (5.35).
- The shared Contact CTA h2 (44px at 1440) outsizes the waivers h2 (22px); shared shell code, inherited, not filed.

## Questions to Consider

1. On a phone, is this a table at all? Nineteen rows with one number that matters might be better as a list where each row reads "Michael Mayer, TE LV vs. MIA, gap 66.6 (rank 106 of 457, 10.4% rostered)", with the ledger reserved for md and up.
2. The page owns the one question the weekly board does not answer, "who do I add," yet a row cannot get back to the weekly board's expert range for the same player. Is a row link into `/fantasy-football/weekly?board=…` with the player highlighted the missing piece, or is that scope creep for a list that is meant to be read in ten seconds?
3. Should flex and quarterback targets share one ordering at all? Two short blocks under one card would make the rank-space separation structural instead of a column, at the cost of the single scan.
4. The 20-row cap was chosen once in a default parameter. Is there a reason a wider wire late in the season should be trimmed, or should the three stated thresholds be the only gate?
