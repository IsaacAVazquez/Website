## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment. The board is still authored rather than templated. Tier plates with oversized ordinals, cliff separators whose spacing encodes the size of the drop, the plain first-person verdict sentences in the drawer, and the honesty chips that change with staleness are all still here and still specific to this site. The season note is the right shape of honesty for Week 1, a dated sentence that says what the board is and links to what has moved on. Where it goes generic now is the top of the page, which has grown into a stack of header, four chips, a note, six link chips, and a three-row control bar before the first row, so a phone visitor gets a landing page and no board. The VORP mode reads like a second board bolted onto the first, with its own single plate, its own count denominator, and a drawer that does not know it exists.

Deterministic evidence (round1 sweep, both themes, 390/768/1024/1440, default, drawer, queued, and phone search). Parser gate reproduced 16.29 light and 15.28 dark on every cell. Zero AA contrast failures across 483 to 711 measured text elements per cell, with the light floor at 4.57:1 on the 10px cliff label and 4.61:1 on the 14px VORP value in the queued state. Zero horizontal overflow anywhere. Exactly one `main` and one visible `h1` in every state, zero unnamed `section` landmarks, no heading skips, the dialog named "Ja'Marr Chase detail" with `aria-modal`, two polite live regions of which one renders at any width, 90 of 90 tab stops with a visible focus ring at 1440, zero console errors, zero failed requests. The only sub-44 targets are the seven header definition triggers (16px by rect, 32 to 81 by 40 by hit-test), a recorded decision, now including the two new "What is Player?" and "What is VORP?" triggers. Keyboard path verified again in this run: Enter on a row button opens the drawer, a neighborhood click swaps the player inside the same dialog, and Esc returns focus to the originating row button.

Where the mechanical evidence caught what the review could not. Hovering a row in light mode paints `color-mix(paper-alt 55%, transparent)` over the plate, which composites to rgb(242,241,235), and the 14px weight-500 VORP value in `--home-signal` measures 4.41:1 there against 4.61:1 at rest (dark is 6.32 and 6.59). This is the signal-on-paper-alt class the round1 context flagged on the redraft tracker, and no sweep enters hover.

False positives set aside. In the drawer state the sweep reports the seven header triggers as center-miss with no hit extent, which is the modal backdrop covering them, not a regression. My own mid-scroll screenshots showed the site header displaced about 265px down with ghosted rows above it, and re-measuring at 150ms, 1650ms, and after a scroll-up found the header at top 0 with no transform every time, so that was the smooth-scroll animation caught mid-flight. The assertive live region carrying the h1 text is the Next route announcer. The dev-tools badge at bottom left is dev-only.

## Overall Impression

The August work held. Every one of the four P1s from 2026-08-22 is closed in the browser, the mechanical hygiene is as clean as any surface on the site, and the season note is exactly what an in-season visitor needs to read first. What I think has happened since is that two additions, the VORP mode and the note plus the two new tool links, were each correct on their own terms and were never reconciled with the board around them. The VORP view changes the order, the plate, the count, and the freshness line but the drawer still speaks consensus, and the phone bar grew from one row to three while the header above it grew too, so the board's own rows now start below the first viewport at 390. The single biggest opportunity is making VORP a first-class citizen of the drawer and of the row's color logic, and the second is taking the phone chrome back to the one line the 2026-08-22 adapt pass measured.

## What's Working

1. The in-season state is plainly said. "Week 1 of the 2026 season. Every board on this page is the preseason draft consensus…" renders above the tool links in both themes at every width, with a dated snapshot chip, a dated ADP chip, and in VORP view a dated "VORP checked" chip, all absolute dates rendered in UTC. The visitor is told what is frozen and where the moving ranks are.
2. The queue rework is complete and legible. The row-edge star, the "Queued (n)" chip, the phone badge with a count, the "(in your queue)" suffix on the row's accessible name, the signal rail on the queued row, and the empty state with "Show all players" were all verified in this run, and the queued view filtered 668 text nodes to 93 at 390.
3. Accessibility plumbing keeps its lead over the genre. Named tier plates carrying tier, count, rank range, and cliff size, focusable definitions in the header and drawer, sr-only cell labels paired with aria-hidden micro-labels so nothing is read twice, a real dialog with trap and restore, and a phone search that keeps the other controls reachable.

## Priority Issues

1. [P1] In VORP mode the drawer does not know the board is sorted by VORP. Opening Cam Skattebo from VORP row #19 gives a kicker reading "R57 overall · Tier 5 of 16" (`fantasy-football-client.tsx:613-618`, driven by `publishedRank` at `:2147`), four stat cards with no VORP among them (`:637-673`), and a board neighborhood built from `filteredPlayers`, which is the VORP-sorted list (`:1183-1191`), but labeled with the consensus rank and consensus average (`:845`, `:851`), so it reads 39 Breece Hall 40.9, 45 Travis Etienne 46.1, 57 Cam Skattebo 57.3, 41 Javonte Williams 41.9, 9 CeeDee Lamb 9.6. Why it matters: the drawer is the judgment surface, and in the one mode where the board's own number is the ranking, the drawer never shows that number and its neighborhood list looks unordered. Fix: pass `vorpMode` and the player's VORP entry into the drawer, swap the kicker to the VORP rank and league size in that mode, add a VORP stat card, and render the neighborhood with `displayRank` and the VORP value so the list is monotonic. Suggested pass: harden.

2. [P1] At 390 the pinned chrome is 225px and the first player row starts below the viewport. Measured at 390x844: the sticky bar is 152px (row one 62px with the select, PPR/Half/Std, search, and star at `:1739-1885`; the count on its own 34px row at `:1891-1899`; the Consensus/VORP toggle on a third 54px row at `:1900-1914`, which the league-size select joins in VORP mode), under a 73px site header, so 26.6% of the phone viewport is fixed. Above it the header block is 263px, the note 163px, and the six tool chips 160px in three rows (`:1636-1655`), which puts the first row's top at document y=874. The 2026-08-22 adapt pass measured this bar at 64px. Why it matters: the brief's mode is Operate, and a phone visitor now sees zero rows on arrival and loses a quarter of every subsequent screen to controls. Fix: fold the ranking toggle into row one (a second compact select beside the position select, or a Consensus/VORP pair with the scoring toggle), make the count part of that row or sr-only with the number carried in the star badge and the h2, and collapse the six tool chips to the footer nav, which already lists all six, leaving the note's inline link as the in-season pointer. Suggested pass: layout.

3. [P1] A hovered row fails AA on its VORP value in light mode. The row's hover class is `hover:bg-[color-mix(in_srgb,var(--home-paper-alt)_55%,transparent)]` (`:1390`), which composites over the plate's `--home-paper-raised` to rgb(242,241,235), the paper-alt value, and the VORP value at `:1459-1473` is 14px weight 500 in `--home-signal` whenever VORP is above zero, measuring 4.41:1 on hover against 4.61:1 at rest (dark 6.32 and 6.59). The queued rank digit at `:1420-1434` is the same size and color and sits on the same hover. Why it matters: this is the third surface in the group to trip the signal-on-paper-alt class, and hover is exactly where a drafter is reading the number. Fix: either lighten the hover tint (mix toward `--home-paper-raised` at a lower share so the composite stays at or above paper) or move 14px signal text to the 72%-toward-ink mix the trackers adopted on 2026-08-22, and add a hover cell to the sweep so this state gets measured. Suggested pass: harden.

In VORP mode the plate rail also goes full signal (`:1293`). ## Persona Red Flags

Alex (impatient power user, desktop mid-season): he switches to VORP for a 12-team waiver-week check, sees a clean ranked list, opens a player, and gets a consensus panel with a neighborhood that reads 39, 45, 57, 41, 9 and no VORP number anywhere (issue 1). He hovers rows while reading and the orange values dip below AA on every hover (issue 3). Search, replaceState filters, the queue, and the keyboard restore all work for him, and the auto-extend at 1024 and up means he never presses Load more.

Sam (screen reader and keyboard): the board is completable end to end again, with named plates, focusable definitions, a real dialog, and per-cell labels read once. What breaks is the plate label in a filtered view, which announces "Tier 1, 1 player, ranks 3 to 3" for a search result (issue 5), and the count line whose denominator changes between 490 and 559 without the mode changing. Hover contrast does not affect him.

Casey (distracted phone arrival, one thumb): lands at 390 and sees the kicker, the title, the sentence, four chips, the note, six link chips, and a three-row control bar before any player, with the first row at y=874 (issue 2). Once she scrolls, a quarter of every screen is pinned. She opens a drawer, scrolls to the note, and the Close button is gone (issue 8). What works for her is real: the note tells her the board is frozen and where to go, the star badge counts her queue, and the phone search keeps position and scoring reachable.

## Minor Observations

- The tool link row (`:86-93`) lists the two live in-season surfaces fifth and sixth, and the note links only the weekly board, not waivers. In Week 1 the order could lead with what is moving.
- The weekly board is linked three times on one screen (note, chip, footer), and all six tool links appear twice (chips and footer). One of the two link rows is enough.
- "Snapshot Sep 10" appears in the header chip and the footer line, and "Sep 10" three times in all on the consensus screen.
- The spread bar scale is derived from the rendered window (`:1127-1135`, deliberate per the comment), so Chase's range bar is a sliver on the full board and about half the track in the queued view. Worth knowing when reading a filtered board.
- The phone row wraps its metric line so "±ADP +x.x" sits alone on a second line in 11 of the first 12 rows (111px rows). A shorter micro-label or dropping the ± glyph might pull it back onto one line.
- "kept as a reference once games begin rather than refreshed for weekly starts" (`:1623`) is the "X rather than Y" reversal WRITING_VOICE asks to avoid; the sentence is protected honesty framing, so any rewrite keeps the meaning and only straightens the shape. The points-per-game tooltip's "It is what he did, not a projection" (`fantasyUtils.ts:531`) and the Value tooltip's "not the Avg shown beside it" (`:484`) are the same shape in smaller doses.
- No em dashes, colons as connectors, or model vocabulary in rendered strings or aria-labels on this route. Instrument Serif appears once ("Rankings"). The h1 is on the DESIGN.md title ramp. No other `clamp()` font sizes.
- The "What is VORP?" tooltip says "projected season points" without saying the projection is preseason; the note above covers it for anyone who read it.
- The FAQ has no entry for VORP or league size, the two newest controls on the page.
- The light-mode VORP value at rest on paper-raised measures 4.61:1, the thinnest resting margin on the page.
- The shared Contact CTA h2 at 44px and the footer "Now" link at 34.8px are inherited shell items, noted and not filed.
- Dark mode is at parity in every state captured, and dark contrast floors are higher than light throughout.

## Questions to Consider

1. Is VORP a second ranking of the same board or a second board? Right now the plate, the count, the freshness line, and the h2 say the second, and the drawer says the first. Deciding that answers issue 1 and most of issue 7 at once.
2. In Week 1, what is the job of this page for someone who has already drafted? If it is "check a frozen reference", the header stack could shrink to the note and the board, and the draft tooling could move to the footer for the next seven months.
3. The 08-22 shape round gave the queue a row-edge control because the drawer round-trip was the bottleneck. Is the VORP number the next thing that wants to live in the row's own interaction rather than behind a global toggle, for example as the sort key a column header click chooses?
4. The hover tint has now cost three surfaces an AA margin on 14px signal text. Is it worth retiring the paper-alt hover mix site-wide in favor of a rule or a rail, so the accent never has to survive on a darker paper?
