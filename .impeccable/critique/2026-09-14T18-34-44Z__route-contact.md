## Overall Impression

This is the most honest page in the group. There is no decorative form that drops messages, the address comes from `profile.email`, and the reassurance copy lands exactly where cold outreach needs it. What costs it is small and fixable, since two of the three channels are shown as bare handles, the three conversion links are the smallest targets on the page, and a screen reader user has only one heading to navigate by. ## Priority Issues

[P2] LinkedIn and GitHub are unlabeled, shown only as "isaac-vazquez" and "IsaacAVazquez".
`elsewhere` defines `label: "LinkedIn"` and `label: "GitHub"` (`Catalog97Contact.tsx:7`, `:14`), but the render at lines 141-166 outputs only the handle and the note. A screen reader announces "isaac-vazquez, link", which fails WCAG 2.4.4 link purpose in context, while the email link is labelled by its section. The refuter kept the finding at P2. Choosing a channel is the single decision this page exists for, and two of the three choices make the visitor infer what they are.
Fix: render the label, either as a kicker above each handle or in the link text as "LinkedIn, isaac-vazquez".
Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

[P2] The email and channel links are 19 to 20px tall at 390.
`.c97-link` (`catalog97.css:614-620`) has no min-height or padding, so at 390 the email link measures 232 by 20px, LinkedIn 107 by 19px and GitHub 116 by 19px. The refuter found they pass WCAG 2.5.8 through its spacing exception but miss the site's own 44px minimum, which PRODUCT.md lists as already enforced, and the brand and microlink classes were fixed for exactly this. These are the only conversion actions on a Persuade page and the smallest targets on it.
Fix: give these standalone links the `.c97-brand` treatment (inline-flex, 44px min-height, padding-block with a cancelling negative margin) through a modifier class, without changing `.c97-link` inside prose elsewhere.
Suggested command: /impeccable harden

[P2] The page has one heading.
Main contains only the h1 "If you have something worth building." The section labels "Write to me", "Response time" and "Or go direct" are paragraphs with `c97-kicker` (`Catalog97Contact.tsx:92`, `:116`, `:133`), so a screen reader user moving by headings cannot jump to the channels.
Fix: make those three labels h2 while keeping `c97-kicker`. Their children are prose, so this is not the size inversion the résumé heading sweep fixed.
Suggested command: /impeccable harden

[P2] Three phrases in the page copy use compressions the voice spec replaces.
"anything on this site that has stopped telling the truth" (`Catalog97Contact.tsx:59`) is a metaphorical compression, and the plain version is "anything on this site that is out of date or wrong". "name it in the subject line and I will bring the numbers" (line 113) is an aphoristic compression, and the plain version is "and I will come with the data behind it". "Or go direct" (line 133) mislabels, since email is also direct, and "Elsewhere" matches the footer's own heading.
Fix: the rewrites above.
Suggested command: /impeccable clarify
