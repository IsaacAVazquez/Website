Mechanically the board is what it was, an authored page with a one-line header, dated mono chips, a contest pill row, a four-column prose lens card, round plates with fading rails, and micro-labeled phone rows, and none of the remediation touched that character. What changed visually is small and all in the right direction. The Week 1 note now sits inside the content column (180 to 1,260 at 1440, 16 to 374 at 390) so it reads as the page's orienting sentence rather than a system alert; the column labels ride under the controls in the sticky bar at md and up, so the bar grew from 65px to 94px and the numbers keep their names for the whole board; the two lens pills stack at 390 instead of the rules pill running through the card border; the seven study links are 44px inline-flex pills; the drawer's chip reads "Board rank 1" and the modal has a "Board rank" row above a "Consensus rank" row. ## Overall Impression

The page that was confidently wrong is now quietly right, and the machinery that let the wrong board through has been replaced at every layer it should have existed at. The builder refuses a consensus that disagrees with its own expert bands, the CI step mirrors the count and stops treating age as a failure once the season opens, the reader carries a withheld path that blanks ECR, value, tier, and position rank per row while keeping the expert spread, and the committed board is the last one that passes the test. Round 1 reads like a preseason market again, the modal names the market order and the consensus separately, the labels stay put, the pills stack, the note sits in the column. The one thing the fix brought with it is the freshness card. Restoring a September 1 consensus under a four-day stale band was the honest choice, and the commit says so, but the page now says two things about its own state in adjacent cards, and the second one invites a live room in Week 1. That is a P1 because it sits on the page's primary reading path on every lens at every width, and it is a copy and gating fix rather than a design one. Everything else left is P2 and known.

## Questions to Consider

1. What should a frozen in-season board's freshness gate mean? The chips already widen their bands on 2026-10-01; if the board is frozen at kickoff by design, the date that matters is the kickoff date, and a gate keyed to it (the consensus predates kickoff, the ADP is the final read) would replace both the four-day warning and the October widening on this surface.
2. Is a board-rank row in the compare modal worth its space once the consensus row exists? Market ADP carries the same ordering with one decimal more, and the tray order already shows it.
3. The round plates still encode nothing beyond the pick range. With a clean consensus, the gap between a plate's ADP range and its ECR spread is now meaningful and could be the plate's one variable.
4. Now that the reader gate exists, should the consensus note say something when zero rows are withheld? A one-line "consensus passes its own consistency check" is the kind of provenance the rest of the page already carries, and it would make the gate's existence visible before it ever fires.
