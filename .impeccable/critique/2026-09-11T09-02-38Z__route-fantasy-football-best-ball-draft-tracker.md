## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment. The tracker is authored and reads as this site. The setup-versus-room header split, the live bar as the single authority for round and pick, the muted provenance sentence behind a single dot, the first-person undo copy that names the pick it will remove, the held room rank that says why it is held, and the cards that refuse to appear on another team's turn because the model does not estimate who survives the intervening picks are all decisions no template makes. The honesty framing is load-bearing and intact everywhere I checked. Where it goes generic is the right rail, a stack of 2x2 stat tiles, progress bars, and chip rows that any dashboard kit produces, and the recommendation card, which is a stock card with a pill badge. Where it misses its own character is the transition. A precision instrument that has just been switched from setup to live should show the operator the live reading, and this one leaves the viewport where the button was and drops focus on the floor, on every room open and every pick. The other miss is that the explainer, the most honest paragraph on the page, is placed where it explains nothing for eleven of every twelve picks.

Mechanical evidence. Round 1 sweep, both themes, 390, 768, 1024, and 1440, all three states, with the parser gate reproducing 16.29:1 light and 15.28:1 dark on every cell. Zero AA contrast failures across 270 to 286 measured elements per room cell (the floor is the season note's "weekly board" link at 4.80:1 light and 5.32:1 dark, 14.3px at 390), zero horizontal overflow, zero targets under 44px (the 1x1 slot and position radios are exempt as hidden inputs whose card labels are the target, per the recorded decision), exactly one `main` and one visible `h1` in every state, zero unnamed `section` landmarks, two live regions in the room, zero console errors and zero failed requests, 36 of 36 tab stops with a visible ring at 1440 (the one no-ring entry is `nextjs-portal`). The 11 "unknown background" elements the sweep skipped per cell are all inside the contest chip `nav`, whose `scroll-shadow-x` gradient is the only `background-image` on the page. My own pass added the room's computed geometry, which is where the findings are: the setup button leaves `scrollY` at 778 (1440) and 1190 (390) with the on-the-clock h2 at −177 and −422 and `activeElement` on body; the explainer measures 220px tall, 11 lines, 503 characters, and the card-top-to-heading gap is 209px at 1440; 120 of 120 rendered rows carry the provenance dot; at `scrollY` 2453 and 5000 the search, pills, and column header sit at −1095, −1093, and −1022 (1440) and the search at −247 and −2801 (390); opening Why this player moves the Log button 500px (1440) and 421px (390). Source hygiene is clean: no hex, no `text-[Npx]`, no `transition-all`, no serif, no em dash in any rendered string or `aria-label`, one `clamp()` and it is the DESIGN.md headline step exactly, and the one `--home-moss` mention is a comment recording its removal.

False positives set aside. The site header's hidden mobile toggle; the Next dev-tools badge; the h1 at 32.8 and 29.6px against the shared Contact CTA h2 at 32.8 and 30.4px at 1024 and 768 (shared shell, inherited); the `title` on the undo tape chips, which sit under an `aria-label` and are not the only path to the sentence; and one instrument caveat worth recording, that `html` carries `scroll-behavior: smooth`, so any scrolled screenshot or rect taken within a few hundred milliseconds of `scrollTo` is mid-animation. My first mid-board and page-bottom captures were wrong for that reason and were thrown away and re-measured with `scrollBehavior` forced to `auto`.

## Overall Impression

The room is honest and well built, and most of what the last two loops set out to fix is fixed and verified. What holds it at 29 is one thing the design cannot see and three things it can. The thing it cannot see is that the consensus feed behind the cards is broken today, and the surface has no plausibility gate, so the "Best fits for your next pick" at pick 1 in a room that starts with Jahmyr Gibbs at ADP 1.0 does not contain Jahmyr Gibbs. The three it can see are that the two big transitions are silent for the eyes and for focus, that the board's controls leave the screen on the first scroll at every width while its siblings' controls stay, and that the most careful explanatory paragraph on the page is rendered on the eleven picks per round where it explains nothing and is laid out so it pushes the cards 209px down on the one pick where it does. Fix the gate and the transition and this is a 33.

## What's Working

1. The live bar is a single authority and it announces. `draft-tracker-client.tsx:625-646` sticks on the shared fascia offset (measured at top 72 after a 5000px scroll at both widths), carries the route's live region, and nothing under it restates round or pick; the status card names the state in words and carries the recovery action instead of tiles.
2. The honesty gates hold in the in-season state. The season note, the dated stamps, the held rank with its reason, the "cards stay hidden until your turn because this model does not estimate the chance that each player survives the intervening picks" sentence, the reference-only presets, the paused-source alerts, and the separate-simulation disclosure are all present and unweakened, verified in the rendered text of every state.
3. The recovery model is complete and explained. Undo names the pick it removes, the tape reaches eight picks back with `aria-label`s that say where each chip lands, Redo restores in order and says "nothing to redo" when there is nothing, reset arms before it fires, rooms are keyed by season and contest so a format switch cannot overwrite a draft, and the phone sheet traps focus, closes on Esc, locks body scroll, and returns focus to the trigger (all measured).

## Priority Issues

1. [P0] The consensus rank behind the cards is corrupted today and nothing on the surface or in the builder catches it. In the committed snapshot the FantasyPros PPR best ball consensus places Jahmyr Gibbs at 54, Bijan Robinson at 55, Puka Nacua at 57, Jaxon Smith-Njigba at 59, Amon-Ra St. Brown at 60, Jonathan Taylor at 61, James Cook at 62, CeeDee Lamb at 65, and Justin Jefferson at 68, each with four experts within a standard deviation of 0.47, while Ja'Marr Chase is 1, McCaffrey 2, Chase Brown 3, and Zay Flowers 9. The redraft PPR snapshot from the same provider on the same day has Gibbs at 2 and Bijan at 4. The prior committed best ball snapshot (source `asOf` 2026-09-06) already carried Gibbs at 56, so this is at least five days old. On the surface it renders as "The current standard Underdog ADP is 1.0. The PPR best ball ECR is 54." under the first row and the same shape under 9 of the first 12 rows (`best-ball-draft-board.tsx:193` printing `rankReason` from `src/lib/bestBall/rankings.ts:71`), and it drives the cards, where `baseRank` starts from the pick's distance past that ECR (`src/lib/bestBall/recommendations.ts:578`), so the three exact cards at pick 1 are Chase 2.3, McCaffrey 0.9, and Chase Brown −3.3, and the market's first two picks are absent. The builder's only gate is coverage by count (`scripts/buildBestBallSnapshot.ts:124-131` and the player-count throw at `:321`), and `getBestBallModelSourceIssue` (`src/lib/bestBall/sourceCapabilities.ts:26-54`) pauses the cards for a missing or stale source but not for an implausible one. Why it matters: this is the surface's central judgment on an Operate surface, and the provenance sentence, the most visible honesty feature on the board, is currently the thing telling the visitor something false on most rows. Fix: add a plausibility gate in the builder that compares the top of the consensus board against the top of the ADP board (for the 24 lowest ADPs, a median absolute gap over some small number of picks is not a consensus, it is a broken page or a misaligned parse) and fail soft to the previous snapshot the way a failed fetch already does; on the surface, surface the same test through `getBestBallModelSourceIssue` so the exact cards pause with a plain sentence ("the consensus board disagrees with the market by more than it ever should") instead of scoring against the bad rank; and check whether the public best ball page moved to an in-season shape on 2026-09-06, since the same provider's draft board is fine. The best ball board at `/fantasy-football/best-ball` prints the same sentences from the same snapshot and inherits this. Suggested pass is harden.

2. [P1] Opening the room and logging a pick move neither the viewport nor focus. Pressing "Open draft room from slot 1" (`draft-tracker-client.tsx:530-541`, `use-best-ball-draft.ts:95-97`) leaves `scrollY` at 778 at 1440 and 1190 at 390, puts the "You are on the clock at pick 1" h2 at −177px and −422px, and leaves `activeElement` on `body` because the pressed button unmounted. On the phone the visitor lands on the second recommendation card with the live bar as the only sign the room opened. Logging a pick does the same (`use-best-ball-draft.ts:99-101`, no focus handling anywhere in the tracker outside the sheet), `activeElement` is `body` after every pick at every width and theme. Chromium keeps a focus-navigation starting point at the removed node, so the next Tab landed on "Why this player" after opening the room (past the status card, Undo, and Redo) and on the next row's name after a pick; that is a Chromium behavior rather than a contract, and a screen reader's virtual cursor has nothing to follow either way. The mock draft already solved this in `mock-draft-client.tsx:552-558`, which moves focus to the on-the-clock panel because "the clicked control leaves the DOM with the pick." Why it matters: these are the two state changes the whole tool exists for, and the live region announces them to a screen reader while the page shows the sighted visitor nothing and takes the keyboard visitor's place away. ## Minor Observations

- The season note says "nothing here refreshes against the games being played now" while the chip beside it says "Rankings updated Sep 9, 2026" and the ADP source is stamped Sep 10. Both are true (the sources still refresh, they just do not react to results), but a visitor reads a tension. One clause would settle it.
- The undo tape at `draft-tracker-client.tsx:717-744` is random-access undo, so the item the 08-23 loop deferred as product work is partly closed on this surface; the brief should record it.
- The drawer now takes `onLogPick` (`:1010-1017`), which closes the "drawer dead-ends" item for this surface; it is still open on the redraft tracker.
- `html` has `scroll-behavior: smooth`, which makes any sweep that scrolls and measures inside a few hundred milliseconds read mid-animation. Worth a line in the sweep runner.
- The consensus board has four experts (`rankingSource.expertCount: 4`), which is thin for a consensus that carries half of every card's base score; the surface never says how many experts stand behind "ECR".
- "The room is 0% complete." after one pick is arithmetic (1 of 216 rounds to 0) but reads as if nothing happened.
- The room keeps the contest chips visible mid-draft; switching one opens a separate room, which is safe, but the pressed chip has no "this changes rooms" hint until you read setup.
- Redo and Undo both render in the status card before any pick, disabled at 50% opacity; fine, but Redo's `aria-label` "Redo pick (nothing to redo)" is the better pattern and Undo could say what it has nothing to undo.
- The board caps at 120 rows with "Show 100 more players", so a search for a deep player works but a scroll for one does not until the button is pressed.
- The h1 in the open room is `text-2xl` (34px) rather than the DESIGN.md title ramp the two boards moved to on 08-22 (33.6px); the difference is 0.4px and the h2s are 26px, so the ramp reads correctly either way.
- Dark mode is at parity everywhere measured, including both signal washes and the warning-toned notes.
- The footer "Now" link and the shared Contact CTA h2 are inherited shell items, not filed.

## Questions to Consider

1. When the consensus and the market disagree by fifty places at the top of the board, is the right response to pause the cards, or to trust the market and let the cards run on ADP alone with a sentence saying the consensus was set aside for the day? The second keeps the tool useful in exactly the state it is in now.
2. The explainer is the most honest paragraph on the page and it is placed where it is read least. If the score's construction lived inside "Why this player" as its first line, would the header need any prose at all?
3. The room is "saved in this browser", and the room controls say so, but the live bar does not. Is a one-word "Saved" state in the bar worth more than the rail card that repeats it, given the bar is the one thing that never leaves the screen?
4. Now that the drawer can log a pick and the tape can undo to any of eight, what is left that keeps the keyboard layer product-shaped rather than a `harden` pass?
