## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment. The page is authored rather than templated, and the authorship is mostly in the copy. The lead paragraph says the true thing about the rest of the fleet ("a draft board stops describing anything real once the season opens") and routes the visitor onward to waivers, the panel note refuses to merge the flex and quarterback rank spaces and says why, and the freshness row is an absolute date with a state word instead of "updated recently". The table itself is a competent plain table, mono readouts right-aligned, name in ink and position rank in muted, hairline rules, and nothing in it that FantasyPros could not ship. That is fine for an Operate surface. Where the character breaks is the footnote, which is not this page's voice at all; it is the draft builder's provider boilerplate rendered verbatim, and it says the opposite of the sentence forty pixels above it. The other missed opportunity is that the page's most useful column on a Tuesday, rostered percentage, has no definition and no interaction, so the number is a readout with nothing behind it.

Deterministic and mechanical evidence. The round 1 sweep across three states, both themes and four widths reproduced the gate at 16.29 light and 15.28 dark on every cell, zero AA contrast failures out of 2,792 to 2,800 measured text elements per default cell (floor 5.35:1 light on 14px nav text, 5.99:1 dark), zero document-level horizontal overflow, zero targets under 44px out of 30 to 37 checked, one `main`, one visible `h1`, zero unnamed sections, zero console errors, 14 tab stops with a ring on every real stop at 1440. My own measurements add what the sweep could not see. The table scroll box (`weekly-client.tsx:544`, `max-h-[70vh] overflow-auto`) holds 18,239px of table in a 630px box at 1440 and 17,111px in a 591px box at 390, with 457 rows on the PPR flex board (468 on half PPR, 458 on standard) and 100 on every quarterback board. The header cells (`:553-587`, `sticky top-0 z-10` with `--home-paper-raised`) hold at the top of the box after a 2500px scroll at 1440, 768 and 390 (measured `th.top === box.top`, first visible row 63 at 1440). At 390 the table is 608px wide (`min-w-[38rem]`, `:545`) inside a 316px box, `overflow-x: auto`, no hint; the default paint shows the `#` and Player columns only, and a 300px horizontal scroll shows Opponent, Expert range and Rostered with no name and no rank. The scoring toggles write `?scoring=` and the board toggle writes `?board=quarterbacks` (`:120-132`); search and the position filter stay in component state by design (`:150-156`). A search hit narrows to one row with no count and no live region (sweep `liveRegions: 0` on every cell). The page `h1` is `clamp(2.25rem,1.7rem+2.5vw,4.2rem)` (`:183`), which is off the DESIGN.md headline ramp of `clamp(2.15rem,1.6rem+2.75vw,4.2rem)` and renders 63.2px at 1440 where the ramp would give 65.2px. No Instrument Serif anywhere on the page. Signal orange is spent on the selected pill rings, the breadcrumb chip, the inline link underline and the focus ring only.

False positives set aside. The one no-ring focus stop is `nextjs-portal`, the dev overlay. The two sub-44 links the sweep exempted are real inline links in prose ("waiver targets" in a 370ch paragraph, "Source board" in a 295ch paragraph). The single "failed 1" on the quarterback cell at 768 light is an aborted `?_rsc=` prefetch. The sweep's `scrolled` state for this slug looked for a `region` role containing a table and matched nothing (the box is a plain `div`), so its scrolled cells are the default cell with the window moved 900px and say nothing about the box; the box scroll measurements above are mine. The sweep also cannot see the phone clipping, because the overflow lives inside a scroll container and its overflow check exempts exactly that.

## Overall Impression

With data in it, the weekly board does the one thing this fleet needed in September, which is to be the board that is still true, and it says so in plain words at the top. The rank spaces stay apart, the stamp is dated, the header survives the scroll, and the mechanical hygiene is as clean as the rankings board's was. What holds it at 29 is that the 08-23 layout decision (a 70vh box so the header could pin) was made for a 150-row table and now carries 457 rows, which turns the phone into a scroll trap with three columns hidden off the right edge, and that the one line of source disclosure on the page was inherited from the draft builder and contradicts the panel it sits under. Both are fixable in one pass each. The larger question is whether a Tuesday visitor needs 457 rows at all when the snapshot module already knows the startable depth is 120.

## What's Working

1. The season-state framing is honest and load-bearing. The lead (`:188-201`) tells an in-season arrival that every other board here is a draft board, the freshness row (`:217-234`) prints "2026 Week 1 · Source updated Sep 10, 2026, 7:26 PM · Current · 157 experts" from the source's own `asOf` and `expertCount`, and the quarterback board carries its own stamp (7:02 PM, 142 experts) rather than borrowing flex's. The stale state turns the chip negative (`:225-229`).
2. The two rank spaces are visibly separate and the reason is stated where the reader is looking. The panel `h2` names the board ("Flex rankings" or "Quarterback rankings", `:462`), the note beneath it (`:464-470`) says a flex rank and a quarterback rank are not comparable and why, and the position filter only renders on flex (`:492`). Nothing on the page invites reading the two lists as one.
3. The 08-23 passes held. The header cells pin at the top of the box at every width I measured, the scoring and board pills sit in labeled fieldsets with 44px targets, the URL restores a shared view, the search input has a real `label` (`:476-481`), the table has a caption and row headers with `scope`, and dark mode is at parity in every screenshot.

## Priority Issues

1. [P1] The footnote under the table is the draft builder's boilerplate and contradicts the panel above it. `weekly-client.tsx:631` renders `{source.playerCount} players from {source.provider}, {source.expertCount} contributing experts.` and `source.provider` is `FANTASY_PROS_PUBLIC_SOURCE` from `src/lib/fantasyProsPublicSource.ts:7`, copied into every `flexSource` and `quarterbackSource` in `weekly.json` by `scripts/buildFantasyWeeklySnapshot.ts:55` (`provider: board.sourceLabel`). On screen that reads "457 players from FantasyPros public consensus cheatsheets. Overall boards come from the public overall consensus pages. QB, K, and DST boards are scoring-agnostic and reused across scoring formats. Flex is derived locally from the published overall board., 157 contributing experts. Source board" at 11px across a 1,334px measure at 1440, and the quarterback board prints the same sentence with 100 and 142. The panel note at `:465-469` says FantasyPros publishes no single in-season overall board, and `fantasyWeeklySnapshot.ts:9-11` documents the same fact, so the page asserts two incompatible provenance stories forty pixels apart, and the source URL in the same object (`.../nfl/rankings/ppr-flex.php`) shows the second one is the true one. The stray "board., 157" is the provider's own full stop meeting the template's comma. Why it matters: this is the only source disclosure on the page, it sits under the protected as-of framing, and a reader who checks it finds the page describing a different pipeline than the one that built it. Fix: give the weekly builder its own short provider label per board (the flex page and the QB page by name, since the URL already distinguishes them) or have the client print the board name and host rather than provider prose, and end the sentence once. Keep the as-of stamp and expert count exactly as they are. Suggested pass: clarify (touches the builder, so the next refresh has to carry it).

2. [P1] On the phone the table clips three of its five columns with no hint, and the scrolled view drops the name. The table carries `min-w-[38rem]` (`:545`, 608px) inside the `overflow-auto` box (`:544`), and at 390 the box is 316px wide, so the default paint shows `#` and Player only (screenshot `critique/route-fantasy-football-weekly/table-top__390.png`), with Opponent, Expert range and Rostered off the right edge, `overflow-x: auto`, no fade, no scroll hint, and nothing sticky on the inline axis. Scrolling the box 300px to the right (`table-hscrolled__390.png`) shows the three data columns with no rank and no name beside them. Measured `scrollWidth 608 / clientWidth 316` at 390; at 768 and 1024 the table fits (678 and 918). The round 1 sweep reports zero overflow on every cell because the overflow is inside a scroll container, which its check exempts by design. Why it matters: the lead promises "each player's opponent, and how widely he is rostered", and on the device a Tuesday lineup check most often happens on, neither is on screen with the player it belongs to. Fix: below `md` drop the minimum width and let each row stack, name and position rank on the first line and opponent, range and rostered as a mono readout on the second, or keep the table and make the Player cell `sticky left-0` with a paper background so the identity travels with the horizontal scroll, plus a visible edge fade. The first option also removes the reason the box exists on the phone (see 3). Suggested pass: adapt.

3. [P1] The 70vh box was built for 150 rows and now holds 457 of them, which is a scroll trap on the phone and a 29-screen crawl on desktop. The comment at `:515-518` says the board "runs past 150 rows" and justifies the box by the horizontal scroll the wide table needs. Measured today, 18,239px of table in a 630px box at 1440 (15 rows per screen, 31 screens to the bottom), 17,111px in a 591px box at 390, `overscroll-behavior: auto`, positioned between the controls and the "More fantasy tools" row, so a thumb scroll that lands on the table is captured by the box until it reaches row 457 or the finger finds the 16px gutter. The quarterback board is the same shape at 100 rows down to a 0.0% rostered QB100. There is no depth cap, no "show top N", no jump and no row count, and `FANTASY_WEEKLY_STARTABLE_DEPTH` (`fantasyWeeklySnapshot.ts:37`, flex 120, quarterback 24) already encodes how deep the board is worth reading. Why it matters: on an Operate surface the table is the product, and the page's own scroll model fights the visitor on the device they are most likely holding. Fix: render the startable depth by default with an explicit "Show all 457" control (the rankings board's Load more pattern), and once the phone layout no longer needs horizontal scroll (issue 2) retire the box below `md` in favor of page scroll with the header row `sticky` under the site header, keeping the box only where the table is wide enough to need it. Suggested pass: layout.

4. [P2] "Expert range" and "Rostered" are readouts with no definition anywhere on the page. `formatSpread` (`:78-81`) prints `minRank to maxRank`, which is the lowest and highest rank any contributing expert gave, and `ownership` is documented in the schema as the average percentage of leagues rostering the player (`fantasyWeeklySnapshot.ts:60`) with no platform named, but neither sentence reaches the visitor, since the column headers (`:579`, `:586`) carry no `title`, `abbr` or trigger, the panel note explains only the rank spaces, and the rows are inert (`tabindex -1`, no button). "8 to 203" beside Ashton Jeanty at rank 25 is the most interesting number on the first screen and there is no way to learn what it is. Why it matters: Help scores 2 because the page has no help layer, and the two columns that carry the lineup decision are the two without a definition. Fix: one sentence in the panel note naming both readouts, and, if a column-level path is wanted, reuse the drawer's "What is…?" pattern from the rankings board as a focusable button with `aria-describedby`, not the board's hover-only header spans. Suggested pass: clarify.

5. [P2] A search or position cut changes the row count silently. `filteredPlayers` (`:158-172`) narrows the table with no count and no announcement, and the sweep found `liveRegions: 0` on every cell; a search for "Nacua" leaves one row and a screen reader hears nothing, while the rankings board announces "40 of 520 shown". The only spoken state is the empty case (`:527-531`). Why it matters: the filter is the one efficiency the surface offers, and it works by sight alone. Fix: a `role="status"` line beside the controls, "12 of 457 shown", updated with the filter, which also gives sighted users the count the box otherwise hides. Suggested pass: harden.

## Persona Red Flags

Alex (impatient power user, desktop, Tuesday morning). He gets what he came for in about ten seconds because the flex board is the default, the search box is two tab stops from the board toggle, and copying a name out of a row works since nothing overlays the cell. Then he hits the box, 15 rows per screen, no sort by rostered to harvest the low-owned names in one pass, no keyboard path to row 120. He will use the waivers page for the add question and keep this one for a single lookup.

Sam (screen reader or keyboard only). The structure is right, one `main`, one `h1`, a named `section` per view, `fieldset` legends on both pill groups, a labeled search input, a caption, `scope` on every header and row header. The gaps are the silent filter (issue 5), the "Source board" link that opens a new tab (`:637`) without saying so in its name while the footer's LinkedIn link does, and the fact that the pinned header is a visual convenience only, so nothing about the 457-row depth is announced either.

Casey (distracted, one thumb, phone). She lands 1,059px of header, lead, stamp and pills above the table, sees a ranked list of names with no opponent and no rostered figure, has no cue that three more columns exist to the right, and if she flicks down the page her thumb is inside a 17,111px box until she notices. The search box and the "Show all players" reset are the bright spots; the URL will restore her board if she gets interrupted.

## Minor Observations

- The `h1` clamp at `:183` (`clamp(2.25rem,1.7rem+2.5vw,4.2rem)`) is off the DESIGN.md headline ramp; it renders 63.2px at 1440 where the ramp gives 65.2px. Same class as the rankings board's detector finding.
- "157 experts" in the stamp (`:233`) and "157 contributing experts" in the footnote (`:632`) are the same number stated twice on one screen.
- The stamp's separators are inline `·` spans in a `flex-wrap` row (`:221`, `:223`, `:232`), so at 390 line one ends with an orphaned dot before "Source updated" wraps.
- `formatUpdatedAt` (`fantasyUtils.ts:19-28`) prints the viewer's local time with no zone; "7:26 PM" is true for the reader but nothing says whose clock it is.
- The "More fantasy tools" links (`:657-680`) carry ↗ on internal routes, the same convention the sibling boards use.
- The position filter and search stay out of the URL on purpose (`:150-153`) while scoring and board go in. Reasonable, and worth recording in the brief, which still lists search and a position filter as deferred product work; both have shipped.
- The empty-filter reset (`:532-541`) clears search and position together, which is what the button says it does, so it is not the rankings board's "Clear search" problem.
- At 1440 the Player column is 670px wide and Opponent starts 765px in, so the middle third of every row is paper. A tighter table or a right-aligned cluster would read faster.
- Search matches name and team (`:169`), so "DET" finds Lions but "vs. NO" as an opponent does not; the placeholder says "player or team", which is honest.
- "I would rather keep them apart than manufacture an ordering the source never made" (`:467-469`) is an X-rather-than-Y shape, but it states a preference the author actually holds rather than a misconception nobody had, and it is protected rank-space framing; leave it. Same for "describe the preseason rather than a waiver wire" in the `notPublished` copy (`:259-261`).
- The `notPublished` note (`:244-272`) still uses `color-mix` toward `--home-warning` and `--home-paper`, which is the allowed direction.
- The shared Contact CTA `h2` renders 44px under the 63.2px `h1` at 1440 and 30.4px under a 37px `h1` at 390; shared shell, inherited, not a weekly finding. The page's own `h2` is 22px and 18.4px.
- The stale doc line the 08-23 brief flagged in `CLAUDE.md` ("No route renders this snapshot yet") is gone; `CLAUDE.md` now describes the route and the hook correctly.

## Questions to Consider

1. `FANTASY_WEEKLY_STARTABLE_DEPTH` already says 120 flex and 24 quarterbacks are the rows worth a start decision. If the board rendered that depth by default and the remaining 337 behind one control, does the box need to exist at all, and does the phone layout become a plain page?
2. Rostered percentage is the column that turns a ranking into an add, and the page treats it as a readout. Would a sort on it, or a "rostered under 60%" cut beside the position pills, pull the waiver question back onto this surface, or is the split into a separate waivers page the right place to keep that reading and its definition?
3. The expert range is the only measure of disagreement on the board and "8 to 203" is a real signal. Is a range column the right form, or is it the same case as the rankings board's spread bar, geometry on a shared scale so the outliers read before the numbers do?
4. Both in-season pages share one client and one snapshot. As the season runs and the weekly builder adds weeks, does the page want a "Week 1 of 18" reading and a way to see last week's board, or is one week at a time the honest scope for a consensus that FantasyPros overwrites?
