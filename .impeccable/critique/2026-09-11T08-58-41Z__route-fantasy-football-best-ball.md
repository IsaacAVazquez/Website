## Design Specificity Verdict

Signal orange is spent on exactly one brand dot and the rails. Phone rows carry mono micro-labels so a 390 row reads "ADP 1 ECR 54 VALUE -53.0 BYE 6 W17 CHI" and not four bare numbers. And the in-season state was handled with a banner rather than a design, so the page in Week 1 still opens with "Draft with this lens" as its only primary action.

## Overall Impression

The surface has done the hard design work and then published a consensus that is wrong in a way no visitor could detect. The round plates, the honest lens copy, the dated chips, the one-line header, the micro-labeled phone rows, and the sticky-column compare table are all real and all measured clean, and the Week 1 note is exactly the boundary the season needed. But the first thing a visitor reads is Round 1, and Round 1 says nine of the twelve most-drafted players are 50-pick reaches against a consensus that ranks them first and second. The number is not the market disagreeing with the experts, it is the FantasyPros `rank_ecr` field disagreeing with FantasyPros' own `rank_ave` in the same record, carried into the board verbatim since at least the September 6 build, and the drawer then prints both halves of the contradiction six inches apart. Everything else on this page is a P1 or lower. That one is the page.

## What's Working

1. The season boundary is honest and routed. `SeasonalScopeNote` renders "Week 1 of the 2026 season" with a dated, plain explanation that this market closed at kickoff and a link to the weekly board where ranks still move. The ADP and ECR chips carry absolute dates in UTC, the contest rules carry "Rules checked Aug 23, 2026", and the snapshot line under the board names every source with its link. Nothing says "recently".
2. The phone row and the compare table both solved their hard layout problem. A 390 row wraps its five values under the name with aria-hidden mono micro-labels and sr-only full labels, so a screen reader hears each value named once and a thumb reads a labeled line. The compare table is a real `table` with a caption, `scope` on both axes, equal fixed columns, and a metric column that stayed pinned through the full 228px horizontal scroll at 390 in both themes.
3. Restraint that the rest of the fantasy group should copy. Zero text in signal orange, one brand dot, rails mixed toward rule, one serif gesture, the h1 on the ramp, and a header that fits on one line at 1440 without a pitch paragraph. The lens copy reads like someone who drafts these contests.

## Priority Issues

1. [P0] The consensus the board is built on contradicts itself, and the surface renders both halves as fact. In `public/data/fantasy/best-ball.json` (ECR as of 2026-09-09, four experts), Jahmyr Gibbs carries `rankEcr` 54 next to `rankAverage` 1.33, `minRank` 1, `maxRank` 2, `tier` 7, `positionRank` 19; 127 of 350 players have `rankEcr` more than 20 places above their own `rankAverage`, and the 2026-09-06 build had 131, so this has been live since at least then. `getBestBallEcr` (`src/lib/bestBall/rankings.ts:20`) and `getConsensusRank` (`best-ball-client.tsx:279`) prefer `rankEcr`, so the row prints PPR ECR 54, value −53.0 in warning amber, and the drawer prints "Tier 7", "RB 19", "Reach −53", and then "Tight consensus, best 1, avg 1.3, worst 2" from the same record. Measured on the rendered Round 1: nine of twelve rows are reaches, and the compare modal marks Chase "Best" on tier 1 against Gibbs' tier 7. Neither gate catches it, since `assertBestBallRankingCoverage` (`scripts/buildBestBallSnapshot.ts:124`) checks count and retention only and `getFreshnessWarning` (`best-ball-client.tsx:257`) checks dates only. Why it matters. Value, reach, tier, and Best are the board's judgments, and every one of them is computed from the corrupted field, so the surface is confidently telling a visitor the opposite of what its own experts say, under a note that promises these are the readings their drafts were made against. Fix. Add an internal-consistency assertion in the builder that fails the refresh (fail-soft to the previous good snapshot) when `rankEcr` and the rank implied by `rankAverage` diverge past a small tolerance for more than a handful of players, and in the reader treat a divergent record as "no consensus read" the way `getValueVsAdp` already treats rank past 150, so value and reach render as NA rather than as a number. Until the source is understood, the honest board falls back to `rankAverage` order or blanks the ECR column with a dated reason. Suggested pass. harden.

2. [P1] The modal and the drawer call the Underdog board rank "consensus". `CompareModal.tsx:116-120` labels its first row "Consensus rank" and renders `publishedRank(p)`, which on this surface is `bestBallRank` (`best-ball-client.tsx:1255-1257`), the ADP-ordered board position, and `bestIndex` (`:305-310`) awards Best on that value. Rendered: "Consensus rank | 1 Best | 2 | 3" for Gibbs, Robinson, Chase while the board's own PPR ECR column for the same three reads 54, 55, 1. The drawer does the same with its "Rank 1" chip (`PlayerDetailDrawer.tsx:229-233`, fed by `:1244`) directly above a footnote that says "Ranks, tiers, and expert ranges come from the published FantasyPros consensus snapshot" (`:517`). This is wrong independently of issue 1, because on this board the published rank is a market order by construction (`rankings.ts:51-55`). Why it matters. The compare modal is where a visitor settles a coin flip, and it settles it by labeling the market as the experts and then marking the market's pick Best. Fix. Give `publishedRank` a label alongside its value (the caller already knows it is "Board rank under this contest lens", the row title at `:390`), render that label in the modal row and the drawer chip, and let the modal's consensus row read `rankEcr` so the two rank spaces are named and separate the way flex and QB are on the weekly board. Suggested pass. clarify.

3. [P1] Column labels leave the screen on the first scroll at md and up. The label row (`best-ball-client.tsx:936-952`) is `hidden md:flex`, `aria-hidden`, and sits outside the sticky controls bar (`:841-895`). Measured at 1440 after a 2,500px scroll: the sticky bar holds at top 72 to 137, the label row is at −188. Five numeric columns then run unlabeled, and two of them (UD ADP and PPR ECR) are the same kind of number at similar magnitudes, so "54.3 31 +23.3" mid-board is recall-only. The rankings board fixed this exact defect on 2026-08-22 by moving the labels into its sticky bar; this board took the sticky bar and not the labels. Why it matters. This is the Recognition heuristic on an Operate surface, and the phone already has the right answer in its micro-labels. Fix. Move the label row inside the sticky container at md and up, as the rankings board does, with the same trailing spacer discipline. Suggested pass. layout.

Suggested pass. clarify.

## Persona Red Flags

Alex, impatient power user on desktop. He sees Gibbs at ECR 54 and value −53 in Round 1, assumes the site is broken or the experts are, and either leaves or spends his first minute opening drawers to find the contradiction (Rank 1, tier 7, avg 1.3) and confirm it. If he stays, the labels are gone by Round 3 and the two similar-magnitude columns blur; he cannot sort by value to harvest the +20s; pinning three players is three round-trips; and the modal then tells him the market order is the consensus. He will keep the tab for the lens copy and do the comparing in a spreadsheet.

Sam, screen reader and keyboard only. The flow completes end to end, and it is good. One tab stop per row with a full-row ring, the row's sr-only labels name every value once, both dialogs trap and restore, the compare table announces player columns and metric rows properly, and the seven mono column labels are `aria-hidden` so they are never read twice. What breaks is meaning rather than mechanics. Sam hears "PPR best ball consensus 54" on the row, then "Rank 1" and "Tier 7" in the drawer, then "Consensus rank 1, Best" in the modal, with no way to know which of those three is the consensus. The seven 13px study links are reachable and named; they are simply small.

Casey, distracted phone arrival. The Week 1 note is the first thing she reads and it sends her to the weekly board, which is the right outcome. If she scrolls on, she passes eight pills and a four-paragraph card before the first row at y=1,593, the "Scoring rules" pill looks torn off at its edge, and once she is in the list a quarter of her screen is pinned controls. The rows themselves are the best thing on the page for her, every value labeled, the tray collapsing to "3 pinned", and the compare modal's metric column staying put as she swipes.

## Minor Observations

- The lens footer says "Board order follows current Underdog ADP" (`best-ball-client.tsx:330`) and the row title says "Current Underdog average draft position" (`:417`) directly under a note that says the market closed at kickoff and is kept rather than refreshed. "Current" should read "final" or carry the date in season.
- Recommendation 04 (`:228`) says "Roles and prices keep moving through the summer … so I would keep refreshing the board" in Week 1, under a page that says it has stopped refreshing. Either date it as August advice or swap in the in-season version.
- The policy line joins three sentences with middle dots (`:317`, `:326`, `:330`, `:334`, `:336`), which is the same connector habit the voice file bans for colons. Three sentences with periods read the same and take no more room.
- "at the contest-floor placeholder, not a literal price" appears twice (`:416`, `:1052`), and it is the contrast-pivot shape. "a placeholder at the contest floor rather than a price" is still a reversal; "the contest-floor placeholder, so value stays blank" says the same thing plainly.
- The snapshot line renders "FantasyPros public PPR best ball consensus PPR best ball" (`:1065`), since the provider string already ends in "consensus" and the template appends "PPR best ball" again. "Four experts" on the same line is thin for a consensus and is worth saying out loud rather than only as a count.
- "350" is on screen three times at once (the "350 ranked" chip and "350 of 350 on this board"), and "12 teams, 18 rounds, half PPR" is on the lens card and again in the format reference card.
- The search input is `w-[200px]` (`:878`) with 32px left and 10px right padding, so its placeholder "Search player or team" needs 182px at 1440 and gets 156, rendering "Search player or"; at 390 it needs 158 and clips by 2px.
- The "↗" glyph marks internal `Link`s (Draft with this lens, Rankings board, Draft tracker, Mock draft) and external new-tab anchors (Scoring rules, the study links) alike, so it does not tell a visitor which links leave the site.
- The drawer's player-name h2 is `text-2xl` (`PlayerDetailDrawer.tsx:235`), measured 34px at 1440 against the page h1 at 33.6, the same step the 2026-08-23 loop dropped to `text-xl` elsewhere. It is inside a modal dialog, so it is a context of its own, but it is the last `text-2xl` heading under a compact h1 in the group.
- The round plates say "full round on this board" on every plate of the unfiltered board; the phrase only carries information once a position filter thins a round.
- Value colors on the row use `--home-warning` for reaches, but the drawer's "Reach" chip, the modal's reach token, and the row all agree, so the mapping is at least consistent; the compare range bars still fill in `--home-positive` (known, deferred).
- Dark mode is at parity in every cell measured, and the dark contrast floor (5.32) is above the light one (4.8).
- The ADP chip is dated Sep 10 and the note says the market closed at kickoff on Sep 9; if that build is the final read it is worth saying "final" on the chip.

## Questions to Consider

1. What is FantasyPros actually publishing on the best ball page in season? If `rank_ecr` has become a rest-of-season or already-played ordering while `rank_ave` stayed a draft average, the honest in-season board may be `rankAverage` order with a note, and the builder should decide that rather than the reader discovering it.
2. The round plates are the board's structure and they encode nothing beyond the pick range. Is there a best ball equivalent of the rankings board's cliff, meaning the ADP gap between consecutive picks, that the plate spacing or the rail could carry?
3. Is a frozen preseason board in Week 1 a reference document or a tool? If it is a reference, the lens card and the field notes are the content and the rows are the appendix, and the phone order could follow that; if it is a tool, the rows should come first and the lens card should collapse.
4. The compare modal has one row label per number and the board has a different label for the same number ("PPR ECR", "PPR best ball consensus", "Rank", "Consensus rank"). Which one is the word, and can the other three surfaces use it?
