## Overall Impression

The pine band holds the clearest statement of who Isaac is anywhere on the site, and the principles show thinking rather than asserting values. The trouble is that the record under it does not hold up against the résumé one click away. Figures shift between the two pages, the current role is missing, and the opening sentence stretches the quality span. The single biggest opportunity is to make /about and /resume two views of one data source, so the page whose job is to show sound thinking stops contradicting itself.

## What's Working

The principles band shows thinking rather than asserting values. "Every model gives something up somewhere. I would rather put that on the page than hide it behind a confident number." is a real position, in first person, and it matches how the retirement and score-pool engines disclose their assumptions.

The opening prose links the career to the site. "The dashboards and ledgers on this site are the same instinct pointed somewhere lighter" turns the tool fleet into evidence of a habit rather than a hobby count, which is the framing PRODUCT.md asks for and which home does not use.

The timeline is legible at both widths. The year sits in its own tabular column, the company is set in the label step, metrics are woven into sentences, and the sweep found no horizontal overflow at any width. The heading outline is one h1, two h2 and twelve h3 in order.
