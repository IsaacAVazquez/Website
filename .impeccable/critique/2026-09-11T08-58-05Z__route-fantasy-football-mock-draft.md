## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment.** The room is authored. The fascia strip with mono readouts, the "since your last pick" tape, the roster as dashed "open" chips, the You're up banner with two named quick picks ("Best left", "Fills RB1"), the tier plates with data-scaled cliffs carried over from the rankings board, and a recap that leads with a letter grade in signal at 80px over a real `table` board grid with the user's column outlined, none of that would ship unchanged from ESPN or Sleeper. The honesty framing is load-bearing: "seeded room #913", "summed pick delta vs the ADP-or-consensus baseline across 5 of your 5 picks", "Practice reps", "nothing leaves this device", and a scope note that says in plain words that rehearsing here in November rehearses August. Where it goes generic: the setup card is a stock settings form with a tagline, the value report is a 2x2 stat-cell grid, and the fascia at 768 and 390 turns into an unfinished CSS grid with painted-in empty tracks. Missed character: the recap grade has a floor of C+ and no visible scale, so the one expressive number on the surface is one nobody can interpret.

**Deterministic scan.** Round 1 sweep, both states, both themes, four widths: gate 16.29 light / 15.28 dark on every cell, zero AA failures across 404 to 412 measured text nodes in the running state (floor 4.57:1 on the 10px "Best left" tag in light, 5.32 in dark), zero horizontal overflow, zero sub-44 targets after hit-test (the one exempt entry is the "weekly board" link inside 313 characters of scope-note prose, correctly exempt under 2.5.8), one `main`, one visible `h1`, zero unnamed `section` landmarks, no heading skips, 38 of 38 tab stops with a visible ring at 1440 (the one no-ring entry is the `nextjs-portal` dev overlay), zero console errors, zero failed requests. The source sweep's one finding stands: the h1 at `mock-draft-client.tsx:712` is `clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 2.125rem)`, off the DESIGN.md title ramp, the same finding the rankings board closed on 08-22. Source checks: zero `text-[Npx]`, zero `transition-all`, zero `color-mix(…, white)`, zero legacy accent tokens, every em dash in the file is an empty-value glyph or a code comment, no colon connectors in any aria-label or title, Instrument Serif exactly once (the italic "Draft"). Signal is spent on the kicker dot, the You readout, the You're up label and dot, the two quick-pick tags, the four cliff labels, the tier rails and the grade letter, all data, state or action.

## Overall Impression

The room itself is in good shape. The pick loop announces, keeps focus, and reads honestly, and nothing the sweep can measure is failing. What holds it at 27 is that the surface has not actually been designed for the two situations it is in right now: the season has started, and the person rehearsing is as likely to be on a phone as at a desk. In season the surface's own freshness gate contradicts its own scope note within four days, and on a phone the sticky fascia spends 39% of the viewport on a grid that truncates three of its four readouts and paints an empty cell. The single biggest opportunity is deciding what "left running" means in September and making the gate, the note and the pause copy agree.

## What's Working

1. **The pick loop is right.** After a pick the status region reads "You drafted Amon-Ra St. Brown at pick #5. 10 room picks since. You are on the clock at pick #16, round 2 of 5." and focus lands on the "You are on the clock" section (measured, both widths, both themes). The tape then shows exactly the ten picks the sentence names. That is the 08-23 harden pass holding.
2. **The recap board is a real table.** `thead` slot headers with "Your slot" as the accessible name, `th scope="row"` rounds, an sr-only caption, a `role="region"` scroller that is focusable, position tones in the cells, and the user's column outlined. On a phone it scrolls inside its region instead of breaking the page (zero overflow at 390).
3. **The honesty framing is specific, not boilerplate.** The grade cell says what it summed and over how many picks, the room is named by seed, the biggest reach names the slot and the delta, and the scope note says which market the room drafts from and where the moving one lives. Every stamp is an absolute date.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] The in-season gate contradicts the in-season note.** The scope note (`mock-draft-client.tsx:745-747`) says the board "stops refreshing once real games start" and "I have left it running rather than hiding it." The room is gated by `simulationAvailable = boardReady && boardStaleness !== "stale"` (`:299`), and `getSnapshotStaleness` in the July-to-December window marks a board stale after four days (`fantasyUtils.ts:33-36, 94-96`). The board's upstream stamp is 2026-09-10T00:19Z. Measured with a fixed clock at 2026-09-15: chips read "Board Stale" and "ADP unavailable, consensus only", Start mock is disabled (`:970`), and the status reads "The ranking source is stale, so simulated picks are paused until the published board refreshes." (`:599`), a refresh the note says is not coming. At 2026-09-13 it already reads "Board Aging". **Why it matters:** on the note's own premise the surface turns itself off on 2026-09-14 and tells the visitor to wait for something the page above says will not happen; the two protected honesty framings are about to disagree on the same screen. **Fix:** decide what in-season means. Either the stale gate widens to the season (a frozen preseason board is the intended input from Week 1, so "stale" should mean older than the season's last preseason stamp, not older than four days), with the chip relabelled "Preseason board · Sep 10, 2026" instead of a freshness band, or the note stops promising the room stays running and says when it pauses. Whichever way, the pause copy must not point at a refresh in season. **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify.

2. **[P1] The fascia is sized by its button stack and falls apart below 1024.** The grid is `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(min(148px, 30vw), 1fr))` (`:1022`) with the three action pills in a wrapping cell (`:1051-1088`). Measured: at 1440 five 199px columns, every cell 160px tall for about 60px of content, strip 162px, pinned at top 72, so 234px of a 900px viewport is fixed. At 768 four columns, the button cell is 160px tall and the other three tracks of its row are empty and painted in `--home-rule` (the grid background at `:1023`), a grey block about 525x160, strip 245px. At 390 three 118px columns, strip 259px pinned under the 73px header, 332px of 844px fixed (39%), 512px left for the board, one empty grey cell, and three readouts truncate by `text-ellipsis` (`:1037, :1043`): "slot 5 of 10 · snake" (120 into 94px), "4 room picks" (135 into 94), "before your first turn" (132 into 94). **Why it matters:** this is the one element pinned on every scroll of a clock-driven surface, and on the phone it is the largest thing on screen, showing "4 room …" and "before your fi…" beside a solid grey rectangle. **Fix:** take the actions out of the readout grid (a second row of three pills at every width, or an inline action row below the cells), let the readouts be a single 44 to 56px line of mono pairs below md ("Pick #5/50 · R1 · You · 4 since · 555 left"), and never paint the grid background where a track can be empty. **Suggested command:** /impeccable layout.

3. **[P1] The board's numbers have no labels a phone or a screen reader can use.** The only column-label row is `aria-hidden` (`:1293`), static (measured `position: static` at every width), and rendered once above tier 1. At 390 the row wraps (`:1383` `flex-wrap`), so measured on the first row the labels sit at x 158-202 ("ADP") and 216-272 ("At #5") on line one while the values "6.9" and "−1.9" sit at x 33-77 and 91-147 on line two, 84px rows; the labels are not over the numbers they name. The value spans carry `title` only (`:1419, :1426`), so a screen reader hears "6.9 −1.9" with no name, and touch and keyboard users cannot open the definitions. The redraft tracker and best ball board gained sr-only and below-md labels for exactly these columns on 08-23; this surface did not. **Why it matters:** the delta at the current pick is the whole reason the row exists during a rehearsal, and on a phone it is an unlabelled negative number. **Fix:** sr-only "ADP" and "at pick N" prefixes on the two spans, and below md a two-character mono micro-label in front of each value the way the sibling boards do; make the header row sticky under the fascia at md and up. **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden.

4. **[P1] The scope note is rendered outside the shell.** `<SeasonalScopeNote>` sits directly under the header with no `SHELL_CLASS` wrapper (`:743-750`), while the rankings board wraps it in `${SHELL_CLASS} pb-4` (`fantasy-football-client.tsx:1620`). Measured at 1440: note left 0, width 1440, header shell left 180, width 1080; at 1024, 768 and 390 the note is edge to edge with its 8px radius and border clipped by the viewport and a 16px inset from `px-4` where every other block has the shell's 41px at 1024 and 16px only at 390. **Why it matters:** the one dated, in-season disclosure on the page is the only element that ignores the page's own column, so it reads as a banner injected by something else rather than part of the instrument. **Fix:** wrap it in the shell the way the rankings board does. One line. **Suggested command:** /impeccable polish.

5. **[P1] Three of the four state transitions drop focus to body with nothing announced.** Measured with Enter on Start mock at every width: `activeElement` is BODY and the status region reads "". The same after Take back (the announcement is built from `lastUserPick`, `:619`, so undoing to zero picks yields an empty string) and after Run it back (BODY, ""). Only the per-pick loop and Sim to end announce, and Sim to end still lands focus on body because the fascia that held the button unmounts. **Why it matters:** a keyboard or screen-reader user presses Start mock, the button disappears, focus goes to the top of the document, and nothing says a room opened; on the recap, Run it back does the same thing in reverse. **Fix:** reuse `onClockRef` for Start mock, Take back and Run it back, and give the announcement a first-turn sentence ("Room #913 open. You are on the clock at pick #5, round 1 of 5, 4 room picks before your first turn."); on Sim to end focus the value report section. **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden.

6. **[P2] Sim to end's differentiation does not survive light mode.** By token it is `WARNING_CARD_STYLE` (`:1076`), measured light fill rgb(237,229,217) with a warning border, sitting beside the On the clock cell's signal wash at rgb(242,230,224). The two warm tints are within a few RGB points, so in light the one-way button reads as "the highlighted action" rather than "the one that ends the room"; in dark the yellow border does read as warning. The plain-words explanation exists only as an `aria-label` and a `title` (`:1073-1074`) and as a footer sentence measured about 3000px below the button at 1440. **Why it matters:** the 08-23 decision was to differentiate and explain rather than confirm, and for a sighted mouse user in light mode neither the differentiation nor the explanation is visible at the control. **Fix:** move the caption under the button ("finishes the room · no take back", the same mono 3xs used elsewhere) and give the warning tone a visible glyph or border weight that does not depend on hue. **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify.

7. **[P2] The same numbers are printed two and three times.** The current pick appears in the kicker (`:565`), the fascia (`:658`) and the You're up banner (`:1220`); the round in the fascia (`:659`) and the banner (`:1220`); the pool count in the fascia (`:679`) and "555 of 555 available" (`:1287`); the room label in a chip (`:575`), "seeded room #913" (`:1582`) and "room #913 · snake order" (`:1616`); and in setup the team count, order, rounds and scoring appear in the header chips (`:576-578`), in the controls, and again in the summary line (`:963-965`). **Why it matters:** the 08-23 distill pass stopped the best ball tracker "stating the same four numbers three times"; the mock draft has the same shape. **Fix:** the kicker keeps "Live", the banner keeps the sentence without the pick and round, the recap says the room label once. **Suggested command:** /impeccable distill.

8. **[P2] Recap facts truncate at 390 and the user's column starts off screen.** "Biggest room reach" subtext truncates by `text-ellipsis` (`:1598`), measured "slot 10 · −30 vs baseline" at 166 into 150px, so the phone reads "−30 vs basel…"; at 1024 "slot 3 · −31 vs baseline" also clips at 159 into 157. The board grid's "You" header is at x 403-491 in a 390 viewport, so the outlined column the caption promises is off screen until the region is scrolled. **Fix:** let the sub line wrap (two lines cost 14px), and scroll the region to the user's column on mount, or pin the round column and the user column. **Suggested command:** /impeccable adapt.

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (impatient power user, desktop mid-draft):** the loop works. Quick picks, search, position pills, and the room advances itself. What he hits: 26 tab stops to the first Draft button, no shortcut for search or the quick picks (deferred product work), the "At #5" column means nothing until he hovers a value, and the pinned 234px fascia leaves 666px of board on a 900px display. If he comes back on 2026-09-15 with the current snapshot, Start mock is disabled and the page tells him to wait for a refresh.

**Sam (screen reader / keyboard-only):** the pick loop is genuinely good, with a named on-the-clock section that takes focus and a sentence that names the pick, the picks since, and the next turn. What breaks: Start mock, Take back and Run it back all send focus to body with an empty status region (issue 5), every ADP and delta value is an unnamed number because the header row is `aria-hidden` and the spans carry only `title` (issue 3), and the tier ordinal "01" is a bare span with the word "Tier" after it, which reads as "01 Tier 2 left". Contrast holds everywhere, both themes, and the recap table is exemplary.

**Casey (distracted phone search-arrival, one thumb):** arrives in September to a plain note that says the board is preseason and links the weekly board, which is the right thing to say. Starts a mock anyway: 332 of 844px are pinned (issue 2), the fascia reads "4 room …" and "before your fi…" beside an empty grey cell, the numbers under each name are unlabelled (issue 3), and the Sim to end button looks like the highlighted action (issue 6). The Draft pills are 72x44 and the position pills 44 and up, so the taps themselves are fine. The recap's grade is readable at 51px but the reach line clips (issue 8).

## Minor Observations

- The h1 at `:712` is `clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 2.125rem)`, off the DESIGN.md title ramp `clamp(1.55rem, 1.3rem + 1.25vw, 2.1rem)`; the rankings board closed the same detector finding on 08-22. Measured 34px at 1440 and 24px at 390 against the ramp's 33.6 and 24.8.
- `formatUpdatedAt` (`fantasyUtils.ts:19-28`) formats with no `timeZone` and no zone label, so the chip's "12:19 AM" is the visitor's local zone with no way to tell; the 08-23 loop fixed the date-only version of this on the rankings board. A zone suffix or a date-only stamp closes it.
- "I have left it running rather than hiding it" (`:746-747`) is the "X rather than Y" reversal, and "Practice reps, not predictions" (`:1824`) is the "X, not Y" contrast, both listed in WRITING_VOICE.md. Both are protected honesty framing, so rewrite the shape without weakening the claim ("I left it running because the practice is still practice", "Practice reps only, with no prediction in them").
- "Rep the rounds that decide leagues." (`:767`) is a compressed tagline in a system that asks for the plain literal thing.
- The grade scale (`:467-480`) runs A, A−, B+, B, B−, C+ on average delta per pick, and the floor is C+; a room where every pick is a 10-pick reach still grades C+. The scale is never shown and the letter is the largest number on the recap.
- The tape shows at most 12 picks (`:114`, `:327`) but the fascia counts all of them, so a 14-team snake wraparound reports "26 room picks · tape below" and the tape shows 12 with no "and 14 more".
- The footer links (`:634-643`) point at the rankings board and the redraft tracker, two more frozen draft surfaces; in season the useful pair is weekly and waivers.
- The scope note wears the warning tone at 8% over the whole width of the page; it is the one large wash on a system that refuses washes, and its warning hue is doing status work that the words already do.
- The cliff labels and tier rails in signal are inherited rankings-board grammar; fine, but with the You readout, the You're up banner, the quick-pick tags, the grade, and the kicker dot, the running state is close to the 10% budget.
- The setup card caps at 780px inside a 1080px shell, so at 1440 the header chips and scope note are wider than the card they introduce.
- The dark theme is at parity everywhere measured, and dark contrast floors are higher than light (5.32 vs 4.57).
- Shared shell, inherited: the Contact CTA h2 at 44px above a 34px h1 at 1440 and 30.4 above 24 at 390, and the footer "Now" link. Not fantasy-owned.

## Questions to Consider

1. What is the mock draft for after Week 1? If the answer is "reps against the last preseason market", then the board is a fixed input and freshness is the wrong frame; the chip could say "Preseason board, final Sep 10, 2026" and the gate could stop looking at the calendar until next July.
2. The fascia was designed as five equal cells of readout, but three of the five are single numbers and one is a button stack. Would a one-line mono readout plus a separate action row pin less, truncate nothing, and still read as an instrument?
3. The grade letter gets the expressive budget, but the summed delta beside it is the honest number and the haul chips already show every component. Is the letter earning its 80px, or is the delta the headline?
4. The recap is the only place the visitor sees what the room did while they were not on the clock. Is there a version of the tape that reads as a narrative ("the room took three RBs between your picks") rather than twelve chips?
