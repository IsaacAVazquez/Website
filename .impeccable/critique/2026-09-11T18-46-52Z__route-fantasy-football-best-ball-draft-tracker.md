## Priority Issues

1. [P1] The pause describes a deliberately frozen board as a broken one and promises a refresh the builder does not intend. The state is accepted; the words are not. Today's room prints "Draft Outlook is paused because the required ranking source is stale. Exact player cards are paused too." in the header alert (`draft-tracker-client.tsx:273-285`, sentence from `sourceCapabilities.ts:162`), "Exact player cards are unavailable because the required ranking source is stale." in negative red inside the recommendations card (`best-ball-recommendations.tsx:97-105`), and in the rail "Draft Outlook paused" over "Draft Outlook is paused because the required ranking source is stale. Three statements of one state in one 1440 viewport, two in warning wash and one in negative, on every turn for the rest of the season. Meanwhile the season note two cards up says "this board is the preseason board, so nothing here refreshes against the games being played now", and the builder's own new fallback says "The season has opened and the best ball market is closed, so the committed snapshot stays frozen at its dated consensus" (`scripts/buildBestBallSnapshot.ts:112-113`, `src/lib/bestBallSource.ts:54-63`). The mock draft's remediation in this same loop removed its "pointing at a refresh that may not come" copy for exactly this reason. Why it matters: the pause is now the surface's primary message, and "stale" plus "will return" tells a September visitor the tool is broken and waiting, when the truth is that it is frozen on purpose at a dated consensus and the room still logs picks. Fix: from Week 1, have the gate return a frozen-board clause ("the best ball consensus is frozen at its Sep 1, 2026 board because the draft market has closed") instead of "stale"; drop "It will return after the published sources refresh" in season; keep one full statement in the header alert and let the recommendations card and the rail say the short form ("Exact cards are paused on the frozen board") in the warning tone, not negative, since nothing failed.
