## Overall Impression

The archive rows are a good reading surface and the arrival is strong. The page then undoes its own promise. It hands a product or VC peer two fantasy-draft posts as "the ones I would hand someone first," though no one chose them, and a first 32 pieces that are mostly sports. The single biggest opportunity is a short curated "Start here" shelf from the clusters the page already groups, which fixes the false curation claim and the audience mismatch in one change.

## What's Working

The archive rows work as a reading surface. Serif titles, excerpts held to a readable measure and right-aligned meta let someone judge a piece from its first-person excerpt, and at 390 the rows stack with zero horizontal overflow in both themes.

The archive slice keeps the page manageable. It shows 30 rows, a new filter or search resets it, one control reveals the rest, and slicing rather than CSS hiding keeps the page at 9,279px at 1440. Keyboard order runs from the skip link, to the header, to search, to sort, to filters, to articles, with a visible focus ring on the filter buttons.

Search is useful for its size. It matches every token across title, excerpt, category, cluster, bucket and tags, "pricing" narrowed the list to 11, and the Archive count updates as the query narrows.
