## Overall Impression

The record itself is sound, every date and title matches the PDF, and the download is one tap from the top at every width. What breaks is trust at the two points a peer checks first, since the band beside the download button describes a two-page PDF that is one page, and the figures here disagree with the ones on /about. [P2] The date gutter is sized per row, so role titles start at a different x on every entry and the text column collapses on a phone.
Each article is its own `gridTemplateColumns: "auto 1fr"` grid (`Catalog97Resume.tsx:237-242`) with `minWidth: "9ch"` on the date (line 249), so the column follows each date string. At 1440 the role column starts at 336, 336, 369, 333, 375 and 335px. At 390 it starts at 161 to 198px and is only 164 to 203px wide, so the Engineer paragraph runs about 20 lines at roughly 22 characters. The gutter is the page's only vertical axis and a scanning eye loses it on every row.
Fix: size the date column once for the whole list, for example `minWidth: "15ch"` (the width of "Sep 2019 to Dec 2020"), or use one parent grid with `display: contents` on the articles. Below about 640px, stack the date above the role as a kicker so the description gets the full shell width.
Suggested command: /impeccable layout

[P2] There is no print stylesheet, so printing /resume produces four pages of site chrome.
No `@media print` rule exists in `src`. The Letter render is four pages carrying the header nav, the full pine and camel bands, and a last page that is almost entirely the espresso footer and emblem. With browser backgrounds off, which most print dialogs default to, the light ink on pine and espresso could come out near-invisible, though that case was not rendered. People do print résumé pages for interviews, and the PDF is the workaround, so this is not blocking.
Fix: add an `@media print` block to `catalog97.css` that hides the header, footer and chocolate download band, forces the surfaces to paper tokens, and sets `break-inside: avoid` on each article.
Suggested command: /impeccable adapt
