## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] The recap board's cell labels fall under AA in light, on every cell.** Pre-existing, not introduced by the fix, and outside the round 1 sweep's two states, so this is the first time it has been measured. Each grid cell prints "#n · POS" at 10px in `--home-ink-muted` (`:1881-1884`) on the cell's `getPositionTone` background (`:1872`). Measured at 1440 and 390, light: 4.39:1 on the RB wash rgb(212,227,219), 4.38 on the neutral wash rgb(224,223,219), 4.41 on the QB wash rgb(240,220,211) and 4.16 on the TE wash rgb(229,216,198), 200 text nodes across all 50 cells of a 10-team, 5-round room. Dark: only the TE wash fails, 4.16 on rgb(62,51,21), 12 to 20 nodes. The name line in the same cell is ink at 12px and passes. **Why it matters:** the recap grid is one of the three things the critique called out as working, and its smallest text, the pick number and position that let the visitor read the room's shape, is the one thing on the page under the floor. **Fix:** the pick number and position line takes ink, or the muted tone mixes toward ink the way the fascia's signal value does (72% toward ink took 4.41 to 6.54 on the rankings board this loop); or the wash lightens under text. **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden.

2. **[P2] At 2026-09-15 the header chip says "ADP unavailable, consensus only" while the note's new stamp line says "ADP dated Sep 10, 2026" on the same screen.** `adpStampLabel` (`:791-794`) tests `snapshotMatches && metadata?.adpSource` and never consults the ADP freshness gate that hides the columns and writes the chip. Both statements are true (the ADP has a date, and it is past its four-day window), but the page does not say the second part, so the pair reads as a contradiction two lines apart. Introduced by the clarify pass, since the stamp line is new. **Fix:** when the ADP is out of its window, the stamp line says so ("ADP dated Sep 10, 2026, past its window, consensus only") or drops to "ADP dated Sep 10, 2026 · not in use". **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify.

3. **[P2] Sim to end's differentiation still does not survive light mode.** Unchanged from before: fill rgb(237,229,217) with a warning border beside Take back on rgb(246,245,241), the explanation in an `aria-label` and a `title` only (`:1222-1223`). Dark has a visible yellow border. **Fix:** as before, a caption under the button and a border weight that does not depend on hue. **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify.

4. **[P2] The same numbers are printed two and three times.** Unchanged: the current pick in the kicker ("Pick #5"), the fascia ("#5 / 50") and the banner ("Pick #5 · round 1 of 5"); the room label in a chip, in "seeded room #098" and in "room #098 · snake order". **Suggested command:** /impeccable distill.
