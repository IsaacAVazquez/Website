[P1] The SPY readout is 31 days stale and presented as today's move.
"-1.54" with "SPY day move · -0.20%" is the difference between the 2026-08-13 close (777.88) and the 2026-08-14 close (776.34) in `public/data/investments/SPY/snapshot.json`, rendered on 2026-09-14 with no date. The refuter upheld it and found the cause, since the snapshot's last close is 2026-08-14 and `update-investments.yml` failed 37 of its last 40 runs.
